# Bundled piano samples — license and attribution

## `acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.js`

Pre-rendered acoustic grand piano samples in the MIDI.js soundfont format
(88 base64-encoded mp3 samples, one per key from A0 to C8), loaded at runtime
by [`smplr`](https://github.com/danigb/smplr)'s `Soundfont` player.
