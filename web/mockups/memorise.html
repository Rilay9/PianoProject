Memorise mode on the score screen (mockups)

The score screen tuned for learning a piece by heart: the piece in chunks of bars; the app plays, hides, checks, replays from where it went wrong, loops and moves on by itself, so you just play and take in the corrections. Pictures only; nothing is built.

Mode-specific settings, everywhere on the score screen: the Tempo panel shows Ladder steps only in Ladder, "On time" only in the modes that judge timing; ⋯ / a mode sheet holds each mode's own options (Memorise as in 5). Display stays shared by all modes.

Questions these pictures raise (the highlighted choices are my suggestions):

  • Chunk size: a fixed number of bars, or the file's own phrases where it marks them (rehearsal marks / line breaks)?
  • Feedback: stop at the first wrong note (like Wait for me), or keep time and show the slips at the end of the pass (like Play along)? The pictures show stopping at once.
  • Timing: should a pass need to be in time to count as clean, or just the right notes in order?
  • Joining up: after each chunk (or every few), ask for everything so far once?
