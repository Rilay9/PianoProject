One exercise screen for Simon and the drills (mockups)

One layout for every exercise: what it is and the progress on top; the item in the middle, on the staff at every level except name only and by ear (the level decides how much help comes with it); your keyboard at the bottom, where you answer on the MIDI keyboard or the screen's keys. Simon is the same screen with a growing chain. These are pictures only, nothing is built.

The content behind each: a set of items (single notes, intervals, chords, arpeggios, or bars from a piece), each with its keys, how they are played (together or in order) and a name. A pool (like "chords in C") adds a random item each round; a passage (like bars 1–4) grows in its own order. The same sets feed the drills, one item per card.
