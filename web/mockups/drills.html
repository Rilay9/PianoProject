Drills: flashcards on the exercise screen (mockups)

Drills share Simon's screen, but each card is a whole short thing to play (a few bars to sight-read, a name, something to play back by ear) instead of one link in a growing chain, and nothing is from memory. Timing counts as well as notes, with an optional metronome and count-in. Pictures only; nothing is built, and the music and numbers on them are placeholders.

Content: card sets in the same numbered set files as Simon (web/public/content/sets), each card one item that keeps its rhythm from MusicXML; fresh generated cards (new sight-reading bars each time) plug in later as another source of items.
