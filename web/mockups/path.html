Path, lesson pages and exercises: mockups

Your answers: the fourth tab is Path (not Progress): where you are, your progress, and an easy way to switch between lessons and rungs. A rung is one skill or step on your path (e.g. "Accompaniment patterns in the left hand"), made as we go, never a fixed course; a lesson is one sitting's worth of steps on that rung; a step is one thing to do (an activity: a piece in a mode, a drill set, Simon, the lab), sometimes with a short explanation or a video.
