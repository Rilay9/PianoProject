          Each line of music is between a top (blue) and a bottom (orange) handle: drag them to fit; the blue × removes that line. Green handles are where a line is split into parts (shown when this screen shows split lines): drag one sideways, its × removes it, and a tap on a line adds one there.

              More

              Fingering is written into the score: it shows when Display → Show → Fingering is on, and the exported file opens in MuseScore with it. The app's own settings (sound, keyboard controls, theme, the practice log, backup) are in ⚙ on Home.

              Keyboard controls

              Play the app from your keyboard, without reaching for the phone.

              A mash (palm or forearm on the keys)
              Your lowest and highest key

              Lowest key: back / again (the score: to the loop's or the bar's start; Memorise: Hear; Drills: Again, or Hear it; Simon: Hear again; the lab, stopped: back to the steps). Hold it in Memorise: Peek. Highest key: start / next / carry on (the lab: Start, or Again). Where any octave counts (the lab while the band plays; Drills by name; Simon in any octave) they are notes like the rest; a mash still stops.
