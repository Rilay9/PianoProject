# Piano samples: Splendid Grand Piano

A Steinway grand sampled by Akai with four loudness layers, released by Akai into the **public domain**
(early 2000s). Fixed, converted and mapped to SFZ by kinwie
([sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano)); converted to
OGG (Opus) and M4A (AAC) by the smplr project.

- Copied from: https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-splendid-grand-piano (`samples/`),
  commit 009793ebf849aee57f9a213aeaf8f2d1e2178f4f (2023-12-06), retrieved 2026-10-11. Unchanged.
- Played with [smplr](https://github.com/danigb/smplr) (MIT) `SplendidGrandPiano`, `baseUrl` pointed here.
- OGG is used by Chrome, Firefox and Android; M4A by Safari, iPhone and iPad.
