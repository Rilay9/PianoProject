# The rungs (step 2, 2026-10-09)

The learning steps that turn `curriculum.md` into a path. Each rung says what is learned, what it needs first, the kinds of activity that teach it, when it is done, and where it comes from. Pieces are chosen in step 3; exercises, generators and modes are decided in step 4. Source tags are those of `curriculum.md` (F, Al, R, AB, T, J, RK, TR, AZ, B1, B2, R1, K1, K2); pages are in `docs/sources/`.

## How to read a rung

- **Kind:** *meet* (first exposure, in a piece or exercise), *prepare* (play it fluently in prepared music), *unseen* (read it at sight). The same topic can be met levels before it is read unseen (curriculum.md, step-2 note 2).
- **Core or alternative:** each area follows one core source (table below). Items from the other sources are listed as *Alt*: optional extras or substitutes, never added on top as requirements (step-2 note 1).
- **Activities** name the kind only: *piece* (real music, step 3), *exercise* (drill or generated material, step 4), *ear*, *improvise*, *read* (sight-reading).
- **Done when.** Speeds and test contents come from the sources and are cited. Where the sources give no number, the criterion says what is observed and ends **[threshold: step 4]**; no threshold is invented here.
- **Needs** lists the rungs that must come first. Rungs without a shared need can run in parallel.

## Core source per area

| Area | Core | Alternatives | Why this core |
|---|---|---|---|
| Levels P–A (reading, rhythm, first technique) | F | Al | Faber's published unit order; Alfred differs in a few placements (noted where it does) |
| Technique, Levels B–8 (scales, arpeggios, double notes) | AB | R, T | One syllabus with a speed guide at every grade, cumulative and widely used; RCM and Trinity keys differ grade by grade |
| Sight-reading (unseen) | AB | R, T | ABRSM's table is cumulative and states what is new at each grade (AB p. 16); Trinity's runs about two grades below the exam |
| Aural | R | AB, T | RCM's tests are answered at the keyboard (play back, identify) except an optional sung alternative; ABRSM asks for singing at most grades, and whether the app can assess singing is open (step 4) |
| Improvising (classical spine) | T | R, F | Trinity's graded stylistic, motivic and harmonic tables, Initial–Grade 8 |
| Harmony at the keyboard | F (to Grade 3), then R and AZ | Al, T | Method books carry it to about Grade 3; above that RCM's chord tests and lead-sheet option and ANZCA's lead-sheet tasks |
| Kinds of piece | AB lists A/B/C | R periods, T groups | Character-based lists (agile, lyrical, other style) keep choice open in step 3 |

## Level P (F Primer, Al 1A)

**P.1 The keyboard and the body** · core · meet
Learns: sitting and hand shape; finger numbers; the black-key groups; key names A–G anywhere on the keyboard. | Needs: — | Activities: exercise (find keys by name, finger-number patterns); piece (black-key tunes). | Done when: names any white key and plays a called finger number in either hand [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer Units 1, 3 (pp. 4–13, 24–33); Al 1A pp. 3–17.

**P.2 Pulse and first note values** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: steady beat; quarter, half, dotted half and whole notes; quarter rest; tie; 4/4 and 3/4. *Alt:* half and whole rests, incomplete measures (Al 1A). | Needs: P.1 | Activities: exercise (clap or tap with a click); piece (black-key and position tunes). | Done when: plays a four-bar rhythm using these values in time with a click [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer Units 2, 9, 10; Al 1A pp. 6–26.

**P.3 Reading on the staff** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: the grand staff from guide notes (Middle C, treble G, bass F) out to Bass C–Treble G; reading by steps and skips. *Alt:* reading by positions and intervals (Middle C, C, G positions, Al 1A). | Needs: P.1, P.2 | Activities: exercise (note naming and playing from notation); piece (short pieces in position). | Done when: names and plays any note from Bass C to Treble G from notation [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer Units 4–8; Al 1A pp. 27–58.

**P.4 The C five-finger pattern** · core · prepare
Learns: C–D–E–F–G in each hand, firm fingertips, even tone. *Alt:* legato and staccato here (Al 1A; Faber places them at Level A). | Needs: P.1 | Activities: exercise. | Done when: plays the pattern up and down in each hand, evenly [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer p. 28; Al 1A pp. 33–49.

**P.5 First pieces** · core · prepare
Learns: short pieces (about 8 bars) hands separately or alternating, with p and f; playing with a duet part. | Needs: P.2, P.3, P.4 | Activities: piece (with teacher-duet or backing parts). | Done when: plays three such pieces through at a steady pulse with the marked p and f [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer (Q&A, short pieces); Al 1A.

**P.6 Free improvising** · core · meet
Learns: improvising on black keys or white keys over an accompaniment. | Needs: P.1 | Activities: improvise. | Done when: improvises for the length of a backing track in time (participation, not assessed). | Source: F Primer (Q&A).

## Style rungs

These run alongside the spine. Each names the spine rungs it needs; a learner takes them at the pace they choose. Levels in brackets are where the source places the material.

### Jazz (J, with RK alongside)

**J.1 Swing and the first jazz tunes** [G1]

**J.2 The blues scale and minor pentatonic** [G2]

**J.3 Lydian and four-bar quick study** [G3]
Learns: Lydian on B-flat; blues scales on C and F; four-bar continuation over chord symbols; the harmonised major scale in 7ths. | Needs: J.2 | Activities: exercise; improvise. | Done when: improvises four bars in time from the given scale [threshold: step 4]. | Source: J G3 pp. 37, 41; RK G3 p. 50.

**J.4 Seventh-chord arpeggios, simple bass, grooves** [G4]

**J.5 More 7ths; pedal in tunes; 11th voicings** [G5]

**J.7 Altered dominants and longer solos** [G7]

**J.8 Voice leading and bebop** [G8]

## Open points for review

- **Bracketed style levels** are my reading of where the sources place the material; the method books for blues, ragtime and rock give no grades (`docs/sources/style-methods.md`).
- **Every [threshold: step 4]** is a criterion with its measure still to be set. Step 4 decides it, together with the activity that tests it.
- **Pedal and una corda criteria** (B.8, 1.6, 4.4, 6.7, 7.4) need the piano to send pedal over MIDI; this is not checked on the owner's piano.
- **Singing** appears only as an alternative in the aural rungs; whether the app can assess it is a step 4 question.
- **Joplin's *School of Ragtime*** is unread; it may change RG.1–RG.3.
