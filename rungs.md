# The rungs (step 2, 2026-10-09; revised after the rung-by-rung review of a0996c9f)

The learning steps that turn `curriculum.md` into a path. Each rung says what is learned, what it needs first, the kinds of activity that teach it, when it is done, and where it comes from. Pieces are chosen in step 3; exercises, generators and modes are decided in step 4. Source tags are those of `curriculum.md` (F, Al, R, AB, T, J, RK, TR, AZ, B1, B2, R1, K1, K2); pages are in `docs/sources/`.

## How to read a rung

- **Kind:** *meet* (first exposure, in a piece or exercise), *prepare* (play it fluently in prepared music), *unseen* (read it at sight). The same topic can be met levels before it is read unseen (curriculum.md, step-2 note 2). A *meet* item is never itself a pass/fail gate.
- **Core or alternative:** each area follows one core source (table below). Items from the other sources are listed as *Alt*: optional extras or substitutes, never added on top as requirements (step-2 note 1). "Or" in a rung means either route completes it.
- **Activities** name the kind only: *piece* (real music, step 3), *exercise* (drill or generated material, step 4), *ear*, *improvise*, *read* (sight-reading), *self-review* (the learner records a passage, listens back, and checks it against a short list of what to listen for).
- **Done when** has a check for each main thing the rung teaches. Where the sources give no number, the check says what is observed and ends **[threshold: step 4]**; no threshold is invented here.
- **Speeds.** A speed from a syllabus is that syllabus's exam target. A technique rung is done when the item is correct (notes, fingering, shape) at a steady, even tempo [threshold: step 4]; the published speed stays shown as the goal and is not a gate.
- **What MIDI can and cannot show.** Notes, timing, hands lining up and key velocity can be measured. Tone, phrasing, voicing as heard, pedal cleanliness and style are checked by self-review, with MIDI evidence alongside where it exists. A rung never depends on the piano sending pedal or una corda data over MIDI; without it, the pedal check is self-review only.
- **Unseen rungs** are checked on several unfamiliar examples, never one. Exam timings (for example ABRSM's half-minute look) are noted as exam format, not as a rule for practice.
- **Ear rungs** check each test type separately, not as one total.
- **Needs** lists the rungs that must come first. Rungs without a shared need can run in parallel.

## Core source per area

| Area | Core | Alternatives | Why this core |
|---|---|---|---|
| Levels P–A (reading, rhythm, first technique) | F | Al | Faber's published unit order; Alfred differs in a few placements (noted where it does) |
| Technique, Levels B–8 (scales, arpeggios, double notes) | AB | R, T | One syllabus with a speed guide at every grade, cumulative and widely used; RCM and Trinity keys differ grade by grade |
| Sight-reading (unseen) | AB | R, T | ABRSM's table is cumulative and states what is new at each grade (AB p. 16); Trinity's runs about two grades below the exam |
| Aural | R | AB, T | RCM's tests are answered at the keyboard (play back, identify) except an optional sung alternative; ABRSM asks for singing at most grades, and whether the app can assess singing is open (step 4). RCM levels sit approximately against app grades above Grade 4 (curriculum.md, level spine) |
| Improvising (classical spine) | T | R, F | Trinity's graded stylistic, motivic and harmonic tables, Initial–Grade 8 |
| Harmony at the keyboard | F (to Grade 3), then R and AZ | Al, T | Method books carry it to about Grade 3; above that RCM's chord tests and lead-sheet option and ANZCA's lead-sheet tasks. RCM makes the lead sheet an option; this curriculum makes it core because chord and pop playing are among the owner's styles |
| Kinds of piece | AB lists A/B/C | R periods, T groups | Character-based lists (agile, lyrical, other style) keep choice open in step 3; they guide what to choose, they are not three separate gates |

A rung can combine two areas, each from its own core (for example B.5: chord changes from F, arpeggios from AB).

## Level P (F Primer, Al 1A)

**P.1 The keyboard and the body** · core · meet
Learns: sitting and hand shape; finger numbers; the black-key groups; key names A–G anywhere on the keyboard. | Needs: — | Activities: exercise (find keys by name, finger-number patterns); piece (black-key tunes); self-review (posture and hand-shape checklist, with a photo or video). | Done when: names any white key; plays a called finger number in either hand [threshold: step 4]; the posture checklist is done once. | Source: F Primer Units 1, 3 (pp. 4–13, 24–33); Al 1A pp. 3–17.

**P.2 Pulse and first note values** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: steady beat; quarter, half, dotted half and whole notes; quarter rest; tie; 4/4 and 3/4. *Alt:* half and whole rests, incomplete measures (Al 1A). | Needs: P.1 | Activities: exercise (clap or tap; a click is a practice aid, not the definition of pulse); piece (black-key and position tunes). | Done when: plays short rhythms in 4/4 and in 3/4 that include each value, a quarter rest and a tie, keeping a steady beat [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer Units 2, 9, 10; Al 1A pp. 6–26.

**P.3 Reading on the staff** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: the grand staff from guide notes (Middle C, treble G, bass F) out to Bass C–Treble G; reading by steps and skips. *Alt:* reading by positions and intervals (Middle C, C, G positions, Al 1A). | Needs: P.1, P.2 | Activities: exercise (note reading); piece (short pieces in position). | Done when: names and plays notes from Bass C to Treble G, and plays a short notated phrase in that range [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer Units 4–8; Al 1A pp. 27–58.

**P.4 The C five-finger pattern** · core · prepare
Learns: C–D–E–F–G in each hand, firm fingertips, even tone. *Alt:* legato and staccato here (Al 1A; Faber places them at Level A). | Needs: P.1 | Activities: exercise; self-review (relaxed hand, firm fingertips). | Done when: plays the pattern up and down in each hand with correct fingers and even timing [threshold: step 4]; even tone and relaxed hand checked by self-review. | Source: F Primer p. 28; Al 1A pp. 33–49.

**P.5 First pieces** · core · prepare
Learns: short pieces (about 8 bars) hands separately or alternating, with p and f; playing with a duet part. | Needs: P.2, P.3, P.4 | Activities: piece (with a duet or backing part the app supplies). | Done when: a few such pieces (three suggested) played through at a steady pulse with the marked p and f [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F Primer (Q&A, short pieces); Al 1A.

**P.6 Free improvising** · core · meet
Learns: improvising on black keys or white keys over an accompaniment. | Needs: P.1 | Activities: improvise. | Done when: has improvised over a backing track; exploration only, no pass or fail. | Source: F Primer (Q&A).

**A.2 Five-finger patterns, legato and staccato** · core · prepare

**A.3 Rests, eighth notes, upbeats, 2/4** · core · meet → prepare

**A.4 Hands together** · core · prepare

**A.5 Sharps, flats, I and V7** · core · meet → prepare

**A.6 Expression marks and first pedal** · core · meet
Learns: accent, fermata, ritardando, crescendo/diminuendo; the damper pedal held through a passage. | Needs: A.4 | Activities: piece; self-review. | Done when: in a played piece the marked loud and soft passages differ in key velocity [threshold: step 4]; fermata, ritardando and the held pedal are met (self-review). | Source: F L1; Al 1B pp. 13–14, 19, 23, 26.

**A.7 Ear: clap back, major or minor, play back** · core · prepare
Learns: clapping back a two-bar rhythm; telling a major from a minor triad; playing back a four-note melody in C or G. | Needs: P.2, A.2 | Activities: ear (clap-back entered by tapping a key or pad; how is a step 4 choice). | Done when: passes each of the three R Prep A test types separately [threshold: step 4]. | Source: R Prep A p. 9.

**A.8 Level A pieces** · core · prepare
Learns: short pieces using the Level A reading, rhythm, hands-together and expression rungs. | Needs: A.3, A.4, A.5, A.6 | Activities: piece; improvise over the method's accompaniments (F L1). | Done when: a few pieces (three suggested) played with correct notes and rhythms, steady pulse and the marked dynamics [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F L1; Al 1B.

**A.9 Reading unseen, Level A** · core · unseen
Learns: two four-note stepwise melodies, each hand alone, 4/4, whole/half/quarter notes. | Needs: A.1 | Activities: read. | Done when: plays several such unfamiliar melodies in time [threshold: step 4]. | Source: R Prep A p. 9.

## Level B (F 2A–2B, Al 2, R Prep B, AB Initial, T Initial)

**B.1 All notes on both staves** · core · prepare
Learns: the five C's to two ledger lines, then the notes between; 6ths, 7ths, octaves. | Needs: A.1 | Activities: exercise (staged: landmark C's, then ledger notes, then larger intervals); piece. | Done when: reads and plays short passages using notes to two ledger lines and intervals to the octave [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F 2B Units 1, 3; Al 2 pp. 8–26.

**B.2 Eighth patterns and the dotted quarter** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: eighth-note patterns; eighth rest; dotted quarter–eighth; a change of time signature (prepare); swung eighths (meet only). | Needs: A.3 | Activities: exercise; piece. | Done when: plays rhythms with eighth patterns, the eighth rest, dotted quarter–eighth and a time-signature change at a steady beat [threshold: step 4]; swing is met, not checked. | Source: F 2A Unit 1; F 2B Units 7–8; Al 2 pp. 4–6, 11, 35.

**B.3 Major and minor five-finger patterns; transposing** · core · prepare
Learns: W-W-H-W; major and minor patterns and chords on C, D, G, A; reading a piece written in another key and playing a known piece in a new position. | Needs: A.2, A.5 | Activities: exercise; piece. | Done when: each pattern and its chord in both hands; one short piece read in its written key and one played in a new position [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F 2A Units 2, 4–7.

**B.4 One-octave scales** · core · prepare
Learns: C major and D minor, one octave, hands separately, thumb under; C major contrary motion over a fifth, hands together; the key signature of each scale played, read and named. *Alt:* C, G, F major (F 2B); C, G, D (Al 2); C, G, A natural minor (R Prep B); C major, A minor (T Initial). | Needs: B.3 | Activities: exercise. | Done when: each scale hands separately, and the contrary-motion pattern, from memory with correct fingering, legato and even [threshold: step 4]; target ♩ = 54 (AB Initial speed guide). | Source: AB Initial p. 20; F 2B Units 4, 5, 10; Al 2 pp. 24–29. Key signature at this level: F 2B, R Prep B, AB and T Initial; Al 1B pp. 40–44 (curriculum.md, Level A differences).

**B.5 Triads and primary chords** · core · prepare
Learns: I–IV–V7 in C, G, F, blocked and broken (from F); first arpeggios across a fifth in C major and D minor (from AB). *Alt:* broken tonic triads in all positions (R Prep B); cross-hand arpeggios I–V–I (F 2B). | Needs: B.3, A.5 | Activities: exercise; piece. | Done when: I–IV–V7–I in each of the three keys, blocked and broken, in time; and each arpeggio hands separately, correct and even [threshold: step 4]; arpeggio target ♩ = 52 (AB Initial). | Source: F 2B Unit 9; AB Initial p. 20; R Prep B.

**B.6 Left-hand accompaniment patterns** · core · prepare
Learns: block chords and waltz bass (prepare); ostinato and boogie bass (meet). | Needs: B.5, A.4 | Activities: exercise (each pattern alone first); piece. | Done when: each pattern alone in the left hand in time; block-chord and waltz-bass accompaniments under a melody in a piece [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F 2A–2B (Q&A). Leads into BL.1.

**B.7 First lead sheet** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: reading chord symbols (letter, major or minor) and turning them into left-hand chords under the melody. | Needs: B.5 | Activities: exercise (symbol to chord); piece (lead sheet). | Done when: plays the chord named by a symbol on request; plays a lead sheet not learned by ear, melody and chords together in time [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F 2A pp. 58–59; F 2B pp. 48–49. Leads into PR.1.

**B.8 Phrasing and connected pedal** · core · meet → prepare
Learns: phrases and phrase marks; crescendo/diminuendo; pp; connected (legato) pedalling, changing with the harmony. | Needs: A.6 | Activities: piece; exercise (pedal change on each chord change); self-review. | Done when: phrase shapes and pp audible in a recording (self-review); pedal changes with each chord change in a pedalled piece, checked by self-review and by MIDI pedal timing where the piano sends it [threshold: step 4]. | Source: F 2A Unit 3; F 2B Unit 6; Al 2.

**B.9 Ear: Level B** · core · prepare
Learns: major or minor after a scale and triad; playing back a four-note melody in C, G or A minor; clapping back. *Alt:* sing back, loud/quiet, smooth/detached (AB Initial); highest/lowest note (T Initial). | Needs: A.7 | Activities: ear. | Done when: passes each R Prep B test type separately [threshold: step 4]. | Source: R Prep B p. 14.

**B.10 Improvising with scales** · core · meet
Learns: four bars improvised over I and V in C; improvising with five-finger and full major scales. | Needs: B.4, B.5 | Activities: improvise. | Done when: improvises four bars in time over the given chords [threshold: step 4]; low stakes, a teaching step rather than a gate. | Source: T Initial pp. 83–86; F 2A–2B.

**B.11 Level B pieces** · core · prepare
Learns: pieces of contrasting kinds: agile, lyrical, another style (a guide to choosing, not three gates). | Needs: B.6, B.8 | Activities: piece; self-review. | Done when: a few contrasting pieces (three suggested) with correct notes and rhythms at a steady pulse and the marked dynamics [threshold: step 4]; pedalling by self-review (never blocked by missing MIDI pedal). | Source: AB Initial pp. 18–19.

**B.12 Reading unseen, Level B** · core · unseen
Learns: four bars of 4/4 or six of 2/4; five-finger position; hands separately; C major, D minor; legato, staccato, f, p. | Needs: B.1, A.9 | Activities: read. | Done when: plays several such unfamiliar pieces in time after a short look (exam format: half a minute, AB p. 15) [threshold: step 4]. | Source: AB Initial p. 16.

**B.13 Composing a short melody** · core · meet
Learns: writing down a short rhythm of one's own, then a short melody in a known five-finger position, in binary form. | Needs: B.1, B.2 | Activities: compose; self-review. | Done when: notates and plays a short melody of their own, in time [threshold: step 4]. | Source: Hoffman Academy L12 "Rhythm Composition", L77 "Composing in Binary Form", L98; PianoVideoLessons 4-14 "Melody Writing" (free courses, rung-suggestions.md); composing tasks in the F theory books and the T own-composition option (curriculum.md, Track improv-compose).
