## What it's for

Answering a phrase with a phrase. It is the foundation of improvising: a solo
that is a stream of notes is exhausting to listen to, and a solo made of
questions and answers is a conversation.

## How to practise it

Leave a gap. The commonest fault by far is starting your answer before the call
has finished — you are meant to listen to all of it and then reply.

Answer the *shape* rather than the notes. If the call went up, come down. If it
was three notes, three notes is a good answer. Copying exactly is a fine place
to start and a dull place to stay.

## Common mistake

Playing more notes than the call. An answer twice as long as the question stops
being an answer.

## How you'll know you've got it

Your replies fit the space without being counted out, and somebody listening
could tell where the call ended.
