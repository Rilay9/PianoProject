## What it's for

Holding a sound long enough to play it. Every other ear drill hands you a small
set to choose from — major or minor, a fourth or a fifth — and a good guesser
gets a long way on them. A chain has nothing to choose from: the only way to
play back five notes is to have kept five notes.

## How to practise it

Sing the chain back before you touch a key. The voice is the memory: a chain you
can sing is a chain you can find, and one you cannot sing you will only hunt
for.

Play it as music rather than as a list — in time, at the speed it was played,
with the shape it had. A chain remembered as a rhythm is one thing to keep; the
same notes taken one at a time are five.

Past four or five, hear groups rather than single notes: two shapes of three are
easier to keep than six in a row.

## Common mistake

Starting to play before the chain has finished. The last note is the one you are
most likely to lose, and reaching for the first while the last is still sounding
is how it goes.

## How you'll know you've got it

Five notes come back without effort, and you notice you are remembering a short
tune rather than a row of keys.
