## What it's for

Playing one note louder than another on purpose. A piano's only expressive
control is how fast the key goes down, and a player who cannot vary that reliably
sounds mechanical however accurate they are.

## How to practise it

Use arm weight for loud and finger alone for quiet. Trying to play loudly by
pressing harder with the fingers produces tension and not much volume; letting
the arm drop into the key produces volume and no tension.

Exaggerate. Whatever contrast you think you are making is smaller than you think
— every recording anyone makes of themselves proves it. Aim for twice as much
difference as feels sensible, then pull back.

## Common mistake

Getting louder by speeding up. They are wired together for almost everyone, and
the fix is to practise a crescendo against the metronome until they come apart.

## How you'll know you've got it

The loud notes are at least 1.6 times the velocity of the quiet ones, on demand,
without the tempo moving.
