## What it's for

Reading the bass clef, which for most self-taught players is years behind the
treble. The lines and spaces are different, and the habit of silently converting
from treble is the thing to kill.

## How to practise it

Learn the landmarks rather than the whole staff: bass F on the fourth line, and
the C below middle C on the second space. Everything else is a step or a skip
from one of those.

If you catch yourself thinking "that would be G in the treble, so two down…",
stop the set. That conversion works and it will never get fast, and every
repetition of it makes it harder to replace.

## Common mistake

Practising the bass clef only as part of the grand staff, where the treble
carries you. Bass alone, on its own, is the only way it gets fixed.

## How you'll know you've got it

Bass notes arrive as fast as treble ones, and the two lowest ledger lines no
longer make you count.
