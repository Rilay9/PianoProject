## What it's for

Hearing a mode as a colour rather than as a scale starting on a different note.
Dorian and Aeolian are one note apart and sound completely unlike each other,
and that difference is the whole point of knowing them.

## How to practise it

Play the mode over a drone on its own root. Modes only sound like themselves
against their own tonic; played as a scale from A to A they all sound like the
same seven notes rearranged.

Find the note that makes it. Dorian is minor with a raised sixth — play the
minor scale, then that one note, and listen to what changes.

## Common mistake

Thinking of a mode as "C major starting on D". That is how it is spelled and it
is not how it sounds, and it leads to playing D Dorian that sounds like C major.

## How you'll know you've got it

You can name a mode from a few bars over a drone, and you know which single note
you would change to get to the next one.
