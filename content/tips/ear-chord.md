## What it's for

Hearing whether a chord is major, minor, diminished or a seventh — the quality,
before the name. It is the difference between transcribing a song and guessing
at it.

## How to practise it

Listen for the third: it is the note that decides major from minor, and it is
the one to sing. Sing the bottom note, then the third, then decide.

Compare rather than identify. If you are unsure, ask "is this brighter or darker
than the last one?" — a comparison is much easier than an absolute judgement and
usually gets you the answer.

## Common mistake

Deciding from the overall mood of the sound. A major chord played low and quiet
sounds sombre, and that has misled everyone at some point. Find the third.

## How you'll know you've got it

Major and minor are instant, sevenths are reliable, and diminished no longer
sounds like "some kind of minor".
