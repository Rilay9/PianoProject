## What it's for

Building a scale from its formula rather than recalling it. Tone, tone,
semitone, tone, tone, tone, semitone gives you every major scale in every key,
including the ones you have never played.

## How to practise it

Say the pattern as you build it. The formula is short and reciting it is faster
than remembering which key has which sharps.

Build the awkward keys deliberately — F sharp, C flat, the ones with six and
seven accidentals. They follow the same rule and skipping them is why they stay
frightening.

## Common mistake

Recalling the key signature and working backwards. That is a different skill,
and it fails exactly where you need this one: on a scale you do not already
know.

## How you'll know you've got it

You can build any major scale from its first note without hesitating, and the
minor forms from their relationship to it.
