## What it's for

Connecting a name to a place under your hand without looking down. The keyboard
is a repeating pattern of two and three black keys, and once you navigate by that
pattern rather than by counting from the left you stop losing your place.

## How to practise it

Feel for the black-key groups first. Every white key is defined by the group next
to it: D sits between the two, F sits below the three. Find the group, then the
key — that is one movement, not a search.

Try it with your eyes on the screen rather than on your hands. If that is too
hard, look once to place the hand and then look away before you play.

## Common mistake

Hunting from middle C every time. It works and it is slow, and it collapses the
moment you need a note three octaves up. The pattern is the point.

## How you'll know you've got it

You land on the key without looking, in any octave, and a wrong answer feels like
a slip rather than a guess.
