## What it's for

Playing back exactly what you heard, rather than answering it. This is the
listening half of the skill: before you can have a conversation you have to be
able to repeat the other person's sentence.

## How to practise it

Sing it first, every time. The voice is a much more accurate record of what you
just heard than your fingers are, and a phrase you can sing you can usually find.

Hold the shape in mind rather than the notes. Up-up-down-hold is easier to keep
for four seconds than four pitch names, and it gets you most of the way there.

## Common mistake

Reaching for the keyboard immediately. The phrase fades in about five seconds,
and spending those five seconds hunting means answering from a memory that has
already gone.

## How you'll know you've got it

Two-bar phrases come back first time, including the rhythm — which is the half
most people drop.
