## What it's for

Holding a left-hand pattern steady while a track runs. The left hand is the
engine — walking bass, boogie, stride — and it has to keep going without
attention, because your attention will be needed elsewhere.

## How to practise it

Get it automatic before you add anything. Play the pattern alone until it
continues while you think about something else — deliberately think about
something else, out loud, and see whether the hand keeps time.

Watch the tempo rather than the notes. A left-hand pattern that speeds up is the
commonest fault and the hardest to hear in yourself, which is what the track is
for.

## Common mistake

Practising it hands together from the start. The left hand never becomes
automatic that way, and it is the hand that has to be.

## How you'll know you've got it

The pattern holds for a full chorus at a steady tempo while you count the form
out loud.
