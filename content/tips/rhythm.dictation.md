## What it's for

Hearing a rhythm and reproducing it, with no page. Reading a rhythm and hearing
one are separate skills and most people are much worse at the second.

## How to practise it

Find the pulse before you try to place anything. Tap the beat with one hand while
the pattern plays; the pattern makes sense against a beat and is nearly
impossible to hold without one.

Count as you listen, not afterwards. Reconstructing from memory adds a step you
do not need.

## Common mistake

Copying the *sound* of the pattern without knowing where the beats were, which
works for two bars and fails on anything syncopated.

## How you'll know you've got it

You can tap it back first time, and say where the beat fell while you do.
