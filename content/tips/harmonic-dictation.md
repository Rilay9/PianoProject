## What it's for

Writing down the chords you hear. It is the skill that lets you take a song off
a recording, and it is the most direct proof that your ear and your theory have
joined up.

## How to practise it

Find the key first, then the bass line, then the quality of each chord. In that
order — trying to name chord three before you know what home is means guessing.

Hum the root of each chord as it goes past. If you can sing the root you can
usually name the chord; if you cannot, the problem is hearing the bass, not
knowing the harmony.

## Common mistake

Trying to hear all four notes of a chord at once. Nobody does that. You hear the
bass and the quality, and the rest follows from the key.

## How you'll know you've got it

You can write down a four-chord loop after two hearings, in Roman numerals,
without an instrument.
