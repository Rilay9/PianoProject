## What it's for

Reading a chord symbol at the speed a chart goes past. Not spelling it, not
building it — recognising it, the way you recognise a word rather than sounding
it out.

## How to practise it

Group them. The symbols you will actually meet are a small set: major, minor,
seventh, minor seventh, and the sus and slash forms. Drill those to instant and
the rare ones can take a moment.

Read ahead. On a real chart your eye should be a bar in front of your hands, and
that habit is built here or not at all.

## Common mistake

Reading the letter and then working out the quality as a second step. `Am7` is
one symbol, not three decisions.

## How you'll know you've got it

The common symbols land under the hand before you have consciously read them,
and a slash chord no longer needs a pause.
