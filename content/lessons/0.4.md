Optional, and only useful if you have played before. If you have never sat at a
piano, skip it — start at Stage 1 and the first weeks will be quick anyway.

The test is eight short items, in rising order of difficulty:

1. Name a note on the staff.
2. Clap a rhythm back.
3. Play a five-finger pattern hands together.
4. Play a C major scale hands together.
5. Sight-read a four-bar phrase on the grand staff.
6. Play I–IV–V in G from chord symbols.
7. Play a swung twelve-bar blues left hand.
8. Play the Petzold Minuet in G at 60 % tempo.

**The first item you fail sets your starting point.** Everything before it is
marked *passed (placement)* and stops appearing in your plan; everything from
there on is yours to work through. Nothing is locked — you can open any stage at
any time — so a placement result is a suggestion, not a verdict.

**Be strict with yourself.** The temptation is to pass item 4 because you can
"sort of" play a scale. If your hands are not together, or you look at them the
whole time, that is a fail, and failing it here saves you from a gap that shows
up two stages later as an unexplained wall.

**Common mistake.** Taking the test on the first day, passing several items on
old knowledge, and then discovering the foundations were never solid. If you are
unsure, start lower. Stages you already know go quickly; stages you skipped do
not.

**How you'll know you've got it.** You have a starting unit and you agree with
it.
