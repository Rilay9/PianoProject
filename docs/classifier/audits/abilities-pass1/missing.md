**Checker:** an independent agent that did not write `docs/classifier/abilities.md`. **Base:** worktree cut from origin at cac27f54. **Read:** `docs/prompts/FABLE.md` (whole); `abilities.md` sections 1 (the 207 ability titles, AB-001 to AB-207), 2 (trace), 3 to 5, 6 (GAPs) and 8 (merge calls); the style draft's coverage lines marked UNCOVERED; `content/curriculum/00-tracks.json` (track descriptions). **Not read:** the full cells of most section-1 rows (titles and the rows named below only); the other three drafts beyond their headings.

**How each claim is labelled.** Sources marked *(pdf)* were extracted with `pdftotext` and the quote read in the extracted text by me. Sources marked *(fetch)* were read through the web-fetch tool, which passes the page through a summarising model; the quote is what that tool returned as verbatim and I did not see the raw page. *(snippet)* means a search-result summary only and is not used as a row's source. "Nearest existing row" judgements are my reading of the row titles (and of the full cells where I quote them).

**Scope reading used (mine, from FABLE §2 and §3).** In: anything a learner does at the piano to progress, including how to practise at the keyboard (the practice track in `00-tracks.json` exists: "chunking, slow practice, interleaving, when to stop"). Out: planning and scheduling away from the keyboard. A disagreement with a recorded decision is marked as a finding.
