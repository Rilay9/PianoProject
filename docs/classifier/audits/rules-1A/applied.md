# Rules for area 1.A: the check's findings applied (Phase 2)

**What this is.** One line per non-OK verdict of `docs/classifier/audits/rules-1A/rows.md` (the checker's verdicts, measured by the checker unless the line says otherwise), and per writer finding the check corrected, with what was done to `docs/classifier/rules/area-1A.md`. Applied in a worktree cut at 7553c400. Nothing was re-measured here: where a correction invalidates a count on the page, the page marks the count as old-model or not re-run rather than replacing it. Where the check names two options, the one chosen is stated. No disagreement with a verdict was found, so none is declined; the departures are the additions named in the lines (the agent-written word list, the provisional ledger limit, the real-scores-only guard on the hymn test).
