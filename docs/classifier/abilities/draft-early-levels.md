# Draft abilities: the levels below Grade 1 (Phase 1a, method-book family)

Draft of 2026-10-08 for FABLE.md section 2, phase 1a. Written by an Opus drafting agent from the sources in section 3; not yet checked by an independent pass (FABLE section 3). Every row is a research claim: its evidence is the cited source and the page or unit it names. Nothing here is implemented, and nothing here is a characteristic of a piece (that is phase 1b).

**Range.** From the first lesson up to, and not including, Grade 1. The lower edge is each method's first page. The upper edge is ABRSM Initial and RCM Preparatory A/B, read in the syllabi themselves. Faber's correlation chart maps its levels to the exams: Level 1 to RCM Preparatory A, 2A to Preparatory A/B, 2B to Preparatory B, 3A to RCM "Grade 1" (Level 1) and ABRSM Grade 1 ([fcorr]; which column each value belongs to was read from the chart's extracted text in its column order). So the books in range are Faber Primer to 2B, Hal Leonard Books 1-3 (the chart puts Book 4 beside Faber 3A) and Alfred's Basic 1A, 1B and Level 2. Alfred's Basic Level 2 sits beside Faber 2A/2B on the chart. A row marked **[boundary]** is taught in range by at least one source but at Grade 1 or later by others. It is kept so the edge is visible. The Grade 1 demands themselves are in `docs/classifier/audits/iter1/completeness.md`, whose lowest-level lines (RCM Prep A p.9, ABRSM Initial p.16) were re-read here at the source.

**What a row is.** Something a learner does at the piano or with music, written so that one could watch and tell whether the learner can do it. One ability per row, at the grain the books teach. Prerequisites are my reading of the order the methods use, not a stated dependency in any source.

**Scope update (the owner, relayed 2026-10-08, origin 2be57f6d).** Abilities away from the keyboard are out. Written theory and analysis rows were removed, and their ids are left unused so references stay stable: EL-103 (naming the sections and form of a piece is analysis away from the keyboard); EL-104 (spotting repeated lines on the page is score analysis away from the keyboard); EL-106 (writing down a composition is written work); EL-111 (writing notes and completing bars is written theory). A response that is not played (clapped, tapped, sung or said) is kept and marked **BORDERLINE** in its notes cell for the owner to decide. Three rows were reworded to keyboard actions: EL-030, EL-071, EL-072.

**Method abbreviations.** **FB** = Faber Basic (Primer-2B; 3A/3B = Grade 1 boundary); **FA** = Faber Adult All-in-One Book 1; **AB** = Alfred's Basic (1A, 1B; Level 2+); **AA** = Alfred's Adult All-in-One Book 1; **HL** = Hal Leonard Student Piano Library, Books 1-3; **RCM** = RCM Preparatory A/B (2022); **ABRSM** = ABRSM Initial (Grade 1+ marked). Faber Primer units are from the publisher's unit pages ([fpu4] etc.). Faber Levels 1-3B concepts come from the publisher's level lists ([fcat], [fcorr]); the order inside a level was not available. Alfred's Basic lesson-book pages come from its teacher's guides ([tg1a], [tg1b]): the lesson page is the number printed before "CD" in each guide page footer. A locator starting with **~** is inferred, either from the neighbouring footer or from a heading whose content was not itemised. Alfred's Adult pages and Faber Adult units are from their printed contents ([aaio], [fad]). Hal Leonard is located by book only ([hlb]).

**"Not found in sources read"** in a row means the sources listed in section 3 did not name it. It does not mean the book lacks it: most sources are contents pages and concept lists, not the books.

**Approach differences that are not an order of two rows** (each in its row's "differ" cell):

- Reading approach. Hal Leonard reads by finger number, then note name, then interval, from clef landmarks (bass F, middle C, treble G) ([hlb]). Faber merges note names with interval reading from Primer and anchors on middle C and bass C ([fcat]; Primer U5, U8). Alfred reads by interval from position landmarks ([a1a]: "note reading through interval recognition").
- First position. Alfred's Basic and Faber Basic start in Middle C position. Alfred's Adult and Faber Adult start in C position. Hal Leonard moves through several positions from Book 1.
- First legato. Faber prints no slurs at Primer, to protect finger independence ([fcat]). Alfred introduces legato in its first book (1A p.35).
- Building scales. Faber builds five-finger patterns by the W-W-H-W formula (2A). Alfred builds tetrachords W-W-H and plays the scale divided between the hands (1B p.40). RCM and ABRSM ask a one-hand fingered scale (Prep B; Initial).
- Exams against methods. ABRSM sight-reading at Initial allows only 4/4, C major and D minor, hands separately, f and p. 3/4, dotted halves, mf/mp, hairpins and accents wait for Grade 1, and hands together for Grade 2 ([abrsm] p.16). Each method located here teaches 3/4 and dotted halves in its first book (FB Primer, AB 1A, AA p.40, HL Book 1); mf is in FB Primer, AB 1A and HL Book 1. ABRSM Initial asks paired eighths, a D minor scale and a one-octave C major scale. Faber Basic teaches these at 2A, 3B and 2B; Alfred's Basic at 1B, Level 3 and Levels 2-3.
