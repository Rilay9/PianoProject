# 02 — Curriculum (the study plan)

This is the pedagogical spine of the app. Builders turn it into `content/curriculum/stage-*.json`
and `content/catalog.json` entries (Phase P4/P5). The owner can also read it as "what I will learn".

Terminology used throughout:

- **Stage** — a big milestone (0–9). Stages 0–4 are the *core path* every pianist needs.
  From Stage 3 on, **Tracks** (genre/skill lines) run in parallel and the learner picks which
  to advance.
- **Unit** — a group of 2–6 lessons around one theme inside a stage.
- **Lesson** — one screen in the app: concept text, 1–3 videos (links), **exercise options**
  (pick at least one), **song options** (pick at least one), and a **mastery check**.
- **Level number** — `stage.unit` style decimal (e.g. `3.4`) also used as the difficulty of a
  catalog item. Approximate mapping to the ABRSM/RCM grade ladders is given so external
  material can be slotted in: Stage 4 ≈ Grade 1, Stage 5 ≈ Grade 2–3, Stage 6 ≈ Grade 4–5,
  Stage 7 ≈ Grade 6, Stage 8 ≈ Grade 7–8, Stage 9 = beyond grades.
- **Source tags** for repertoire: `[AUTH]` = builders author it in ABC/tinyNotation (short
  public-domain melodies, easy arrangements); `[MT]` = MuseTrainer public-domain MusicXML
  library (github.com/musetrainer/library, verified reachable); `[KERN]` = Humdrum `**kern`
  repositories by Craig Sapp on GitHub (Mozart & Beethoven sonatas, Chopin preludes/mazurkas,
  Scarlatti sonatas, Joplin rags, Bach chorales; verified reachable); `[PDMX]` = PDMX public
  domain MusicXML dataset (filter by title/instrument); `[MUTO]` = Mutopia Project (LilyPond
  source → convert); `[IMSLP]` = IMSLP MusicXML/PDF (PD scans; use only if a MusicXML exists);
  `[GEN]` = generated by `tools/content/generate_exercises.py`; `[IMPORT]` = user must import
  (copyrighted). See `03-content-pipeline.md` for the rules.

Time estimates assume ~30 min/day, 5–6 days/week. They are advisory.

## Part A — Principles the app enforces (and why)

0. **No assumptions about level; free movement both ways.** Nothing is locked. The learner can
   open any lesson, mark it "I already know this", take a 2–3-minute quick check, or come back
   to any earlier concept from the Skills review screen. The plan is a map, not a corridor.
   (The owner already reads basic notation and plays some chords; Stages 1–2 are a fast review
   for him and a real start for anyone else.)
1. **Read from day one, but play before you read.** Each new concept is first played by
   imitation (the app plays it, the learner copies with the on-screen keys highlighted), then
   read from notation. Rote-only apps produce players who cannot read; notation-only apps
   produce frustration. We do both, every lesson.
2. **Hands separately → hands together → slow → tempo.** Every song has this built-in ladder
   (the app's "hand focus" and "tempo %" settings). The mastery check requires hands together
   at ≥ 80 % tempo.
3. **Technique is tiny and daily.** 5 minutes of scales/five-finger patterns/Hanon-style
   patterns at the start of each session, drawn from `[GEN]` drills in the current keys.
4. **Chords and reading are two literacies.** Classical reading (notes on staff) and chord-
   symbol literacy (C, Am, F, G7…) are taught side by side from Stage 2. This is Bill Hilton's
   core argument and it is right: a pianist who can do both can play anything.
5. **Many options, one requirement.** Each lesson offers ≥ 3 songs and ≥ 2 exercises; the
   learner passes the lesson by mastering **any one song + any one exercise** (configurable to
   "two songs" for thoroughness). Options span genres so the learner stays motivated.
6. **Spaced review.** Passed items come back in a "Review" queue (1 day, 3 days, 1 week,
   3 weeks). Mastered items appear in "Repertoire" for occasional replay.
7. **Ear before theory before name.** Every theory concept has an ear-training drill the
   learner answers *on the keyboard* (MIDI or screen keys), then a short text explanation.
8. **Session templates.** The owner practises 20–30 min on some weekdays and for hours at
   weekends, so the app builds sessions by length:
   - **15 min:** technique 4 · review 4 · one new item 7.
   - **30 min:** technique 5 · review 5 · new material 10 · repertoire 8 · free play 2.
   - **60 min:** technique 8 · review 8 · new material 20 · repertoire 12 · jam/chord-chart or
     improv 8 · free play 4.
   - **120 min (weekend):** two 60-min halves with a break reminder; the second half is
     repertoire-heavy (a "beautiful piece" project), jam practice, and a sight-reading set.
   Goals are weekly minutes; missing weekdays never breaks anything.

## Part B — Stage map

| Stage | Name | Approx. duration | Outcome |
|------:|------|------------------|---------|
| 0 | Orientation & placement | 2–4 days | Sits correctly, names keys, knows finger numbers, has done the placement test, app configured |
| 1 | First notes | 3–5 weeks | Plays simple 5-finger melodies with each hand, reads C–G (treble) and F–C (bass), quarter/half/whole notes, 4/4 & 3/4 |
| 2 | Two hands | 5–8 weeks | Hands together in C position with single-note and block-chord LH; eighth notes, ties, dotted half, dynamics; first chord symbols (C, F, G) |
| 3 | Keys, chords, reading | 8–12 weeks | C, G, F major & A minor; I–IV–V(7) chords in each; ledger lines; 8ths fluently; pedal basics; reads simple grand-staff pieces at sight |
| 4 | Fluency & technique | 10–14 weeks | Scales HT (C G D A F Bb + Am Em Dm); arpeggios; Hanon 1–10; 6/8, syncopation, triplets; ≈ Grade 1 repertoire |
| 5 | Early intermediate | 4–6 months | ≈ Grade 2–3: Clementi/Burgmüller/Bach AMB; chord inversions & 7ths; lead sheets; 12-bar blues; swing feel |
| 6 | Intermediate | 6–9 months | ≈ Grade 4–5: Bach Preludes, Chopin Preludes 4/7/20, Satie, Für Elise complete; jazz ii–V–I; boogie LH; Joplin (easy) |
| 7 | Upper intermediate | 6–9 months | ≈ Grade 6: Bach Inventions, Mozart K.545, Moonlight I, Pathétique II; rootless voicings; stride basics; Maple Leaf Rag (slow) |
| 8 | Advanced | 9–12 months | ≈ Grade 7–8: WTC preludes & fugues, Chopin Nocturnes/Waltzes, Debussy, Beethoven sonatas, full Joplin; bebop vocabulary |
| 9 | Open repertoire & specialisation | ongoing | Learner-chosen projects; the app becomes a practice tool with the full library and imports |

**Tracks (from Stage 3):** `classical`, `chords-pop`, `blues-boogie`, `jazz`, `ragtime`,
`theory-ear`, `improv-compose`, plus optional mini-modules `hymns-gospel`, `latin`, `holiday`,
`film-game` (import-only). Each track has its own ladder inside stages 3–9 (Part D).

## Part C — Core path, lesson by lesson (Stages 0–4)

Format per lesson: **Concepts** · **Exercises** (≥ 2) · **Songs** (≥ 3) · **Videos** (free) ·
**Mastery**. "Videos" give the teacher + topic; builders find the exact URL and confirm it is
the teacher's own free public video (Bill Hilton's beginner course = the 21-video YouTube
playlist "How To Play Piano For Beginners" plus his free course-notes PDF at
billspianopages.com/beginners; Hoffman Academy = free lesson videos on their site/YouTube;
Lypur = Andrew Furmanczyk's "Learn Free Piano" and "Learn Free Music Theory" playlists).
Lesson numbers for Bill Hilton are *topic mappings to be verified* against the playlist.

### Stage 0 — Orientation & placement

**0.1 Your instrument and your body.** Concepts: sitting height/distance (forearms level,
elbows slightly in front of body), curved fingers "holding a bubble", relaxed shoulders,
thumb as finger 1, finger numbers 1–5 both hands, the HP-130's controls that matter (volume,
headphones, MIDI OUT). Exercises: `[GEN]` finger-number tap drill (app names a finger/hand,
learner presses any key with it — with MIDI the app can't verify which finger, so it verifies
*hand* via pitch range and trusts the learner), posture checklist. Videos: Bill Hilton
(posture & hand shape), Hoffman Academy Unit 1 lesson 1, Josh Wright "piano posture".
Mastery: checklist ticked.

**0.2 Keyboard geography.** Concepts: black-key groups of 2 and 3; C is left of the 2-group,
F left of the 3-group; octaves; middle C (C4, MIDI 60); the 7 letter names; higher/lower =
right/left. Exercises: `[GEN]` "Find the key" drill (app names a key, learner presses it;
timed; all octaves), "Play all the Cs / Fs / Gs". Songs: none. Videos: Bill Hilton (keyboard
layout), Lypur lesson 1. Mastery: 20 keys in 40 s with ≥ 95 %.

**0.3 How the app works & how to practise.** Concepts: Wait vs Tempo mode, hand focus, tempo
%, loops, bars-per-window, the review queue, what "mastered" means, the 30-minute template,
"slow is fast". Exercise: guided tour on `song.folk.hot-cross-buns` `[AUTH]`. Mastery: tour
completed.

**0.4 Placement test (optional).** A branching set of 8 short items (read a note, clap a
rhythm, play a 5-finger pattern HT, play a C major scale HT, sight-read a 4-bar grand-staff
phrase, play I–IV–V in G from symbols, play a swung 12-bar blues LH, play Bach Minuet Anh 114
at 60 %). The first failure sets the starting stage/unit. Absolute beginners skip this.

### Stage 1 — First notes

**1.1 Right hand C position (C-D-E-F-G, fingers 1–5).** Concepts: hand position, one finger
per key, quarter note = 1 beat, the staff (5 lines, 4 spaces), treble clef, Middle C on a ledger
line, D–G stepping up, bar lines, 4/4, counting "1 2 3 4". Exercises: `[GEN]` RH 5-finger
walk up/down (quarter notes, 60 bpm), `[GEN]` "note-name flash" (treble C4–G4), rhythm clap
drill (quarters/rests; learner taps any key). Songs `[AUTH]`: *Hot Cross Buns* (RH), *Mary
Had a Little Lamb* (RH), *Merrily We Roll Along*, *Au Clair de la Lune* (RH), *Ode to Joy*
(theme, RH only, C position). Videos: Bill Hilton (right-hand first tune), Hoffman Unit 1,
Lypur lesson 2. Mastery: one song, Wait mode ≥ 95 %, then Tempo mode ≥ 90 % at 60 bpm.

**1.2 Half notes, whole notes, rests.** Concepts: half = 2 beats, whole = 4, quarter/half/whole
rests, ties preview, phrasing (breathing between phrases). Exercises: `[GEN]` rhythm reading
(mixed q/h/w on one pitch), `[GEN]` RH melodies with long notes. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Lightly
Row*, *Go Tell Aunt Rhody*, *Jingle Bells* (chorus, RH), *Twinkle Twinkle* (RH, C position
version), *Frère Jacques* (RH). Mastery: as 1.1.

**1.3 Left hand C position (C3–G3 with fingers 5–1) and the bass clef.** Concepts: bass clef,
Bass F, C3 & G3 landmarks, mirror-image fingering, LH reading practice. Exercises: `[GEN]` LH
5-finger walk, `[GEN]` bass-clef note flash (F2–C4), `[GEN]` LH melodies. Songs `[AUTH]`
(LH-only versions): *Hot Cross Buns*, *Mary Had a Little Lamb*, *Ode to Joy* (LH), *Old
MacDonald* (LH). Videos: Bill Hilton (left hand), Hoffman Unit 2, Lypur "bass clef".
Mastery: one LH song at 60 bpm ≥ 90 %.

**1.4 The grand staff; hands alternating.** Concepts: grand staff joined by a brace; RH and LH
never both play at once yet ("call and response"); reading two staves vertically; 3/4 time
and the dotted half note. Exercises: `[GEN]` alternating-hands patterns, `[GEN]` 3/4 rhythm
reading. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Ode to Joy* (RH melody bars 1–4, LH answers bars 5–8), *Lavender's
Blue* (3/4), *Kum Ba Yah* (alternating), *Alouette*, *Oh When the Saints* (alternating). Mastery:
one song HT-alternating at 70 bpm ≥ 90 %.

**1.5 Steps and skips; the first "sight-reading" habit.** Concepts: steps (2nd) vs skips (3rd)
on the staff (line→space vs line→line), reading by interval not by name, looking ahead one
beat. Exercises: `[GEN]` **Sight-reading generator level 1** (random 4-bar RH melodies in C
position, quarters/halves; new one each time — this is the app's killer practice feature),
`[GEN]` interval ear drill (2nd vs 3rd; learner plays back). Songs `[AUTH]`: *Skip to My
Lou*, *Yankee Doodle* (RH), *Aura Lee* (RH). Mastery: 5 generated melodies at ≥ 90 % first
attempt.

### Stage 2 — Two hands

**2.1 Hands together: LH holds, RH moves.** Concepts: LH whole/half notes under RH melody;
vertical alignment; "LH is the floor". Exercises: `[GEN]` HT pattern drills (LH C hold, RH
5-finger), `[GEN]` HT with LH changing every bar (C / G). Songs `[AUTH]`: *Ode to Joy* (HT,
LH long notes), *Twinkle Twinkle* (HT), *Jingle Bells* (HT), *Mary Had a Little Lamb* (HT
with LH C–G). Videos: Bill Hilton (hands together), Hoffman Unit 3–4. Mastery: HT ≥ 90 % at
60 bpm Tempo mode.

**2.2 Eighth notes and counting "1 & 2 &".** Concepts: eighths, beams, subdivision, keeping a
steady pulse (metronome on). Exercises: `[GEN]` rhythm reading with eighths, `[GEN]` RH
melodies with eighths. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Old MacDonald* (HT), *This Old Man*, *Camptown Races*
(RH+simple LH), *Row Row Row Your Boat*, *London Bridge*. Mastery: one song HT at 72 bpm.

**2.3 First chords: C, F, G as block triads (and what a chord *is*).** Concepts: a triad =
root+3rd+5th; C major chord (C-E-G), F (F-A-C), G (G-B-D); chord symbols above the staff;
LH plays chords, RH melody; I, IV, V naming. Exercises: `[GEN]` chord drill (app names/shows
chord, learner plays all 3 notes together — MIDI verifies), `[GEN]` C–F–G changes at 60 bpm.
Songs `[AUTH]`: *Amazing Grace* (melody + block chords), *Oh When the Saints* (chords),
*Kum Ba Yah*, *He's Got the Whole World*, *Down in the Valley* (3/4 chords), *Happy Birthday*
(3/4). Videos: **Bill Hilton (C, F, G chords — the heart of his beginner course)**, Hoffman
"chords". Mastery: chord drill 20 changes ≥ 95 %; one song HT.

**2.4 Ties, dotted quarter, dynamics, tempo words.** Concepts: tie vs slur, dotted quarter +
eighth, p/mp/mf/f, crescendo/diminuendo, Andante/Moderato/Allegro, ritardando. Exercises:
`[GEN]` dotted-rhythm reading, dynamics drill (MIDI velocity: app shows a meter; play a
phrase p then f). Songs `[AUTH]`: *Silent Night* (3/4, dotted rhythms), *Scarborough Fair*
(3/4, minor feel, single-note LH), *Greensleeves* (easy, 6/8 preview optional), *Auld Lang
Syne* (dotted). Mastery: one song; dynamics drill ≥ 80 % contrast (avg velocity f ≥ 1.6× p).

**2.5 Moving out of C position: thumb-under and finger-crossing preview; the octave leap.**
Concepts: extending the hand, shifting positions marked by fingering numbers, reading
fingering. Exercises: `[GEN]` position-shift drills, `[GEN]` C major scale RH one octave
(thumb under after 3), LH one octave (3 over after thumb). Songs `[AUTH]`: *Ode to Joy* (full
theme with the B-section), *Shenandoah*, *The Water Is Wide*, *Beethoven — Ecossaise in G
(simplified)*. Videos: Bill Hilton (C major scale), Lypur "scales", Hoffman "scale". Mastery:
C major scale HS at 60 bpm in eighths, ≥ 95 %.

### Stage 3 — Keys, chords, reading

**3.1 Sharps, flats, half/whole steps, the major scale formula.** Concepts: W-W-H-W-W-W-H;
G major (F#) and F major (Bb); key signatures; accidentals last for the bar. Exercises: `[GEN]`
G & F major scales HS, `[GEN]` "build a major scale from any note" ear+key drill, `[GEN]`
note-flash with sharps/flats. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Minuet in G (simplified, Anh 114 melody only)*,
*Oh Susanna* (F), *Red River Valley* (G), *Home on the Range* (F, 3/4). Videos: Bill Hilton
(sharps/flats & keys), Lypur theory 1–3, Open Music Theory "Scales". Mastery: both scales HS.

**3.2 Primary chords in G and F; V7; chord changes with smooth voice leading.** Concepts:
I–IV–V7 in C, G, F; G7 = G-B-D-F; "keep common tones" (C → F/C → G7/B); LH chord shapes
with inversions previewed; playing from a chord chart. Exercises: `[GEN]` I–IV–V7 drills in
C/G/F (root position and smooth versions), `[GEN]` chord-symbol flash. Songs `[AUTH]`:
*Amazing Grace* (G, smooth chords), *When the Saints* (F), *Tom Dooley*, *Clementine*, *Michael
Row the Boat*, *Banks of the Ohio*. Videos: Bill Hilton (chord inversions/smooth changes),
Hoffman "V7". Mastery: chord-chart song at 80 bpm HT.

**3.3 A minor & the relative minor; natural/harmonic minor; minor chords.** Concepts:
relative minor shares key sig; raised 7th; Am, Dm, Em, E7. Exercises: `[GEN]` A harmonic minor
scale HS, `[GEN]` Am–Dm–E7 changes, `[GEN]` major-vs-minor chord ear drill. Songs `[AUTH]`:
*Greensleeves* (Am, full), *House of the Rising Sun* (Am, arpeggiated LH — classic), *Scarborough
Fair* (Dm/Am), *Wayfaring Stranger*, *Hava Nagila* (E harmonic-minor flavour), *Erev Shel
Shoshanim*. Videos: Bill Hilton (minor chords), Lypur "minor scales". Mastery: one song HT.

**3.4 Reading ledger lines and wider ranges; 8va; both hands away from Middle C.** Concepts:
ledger lines above treble/below bass, landmark notes (Treble G, Bass F, high C, low C), 8va.
Exercises: `[GEN]` note-flash extended (C2–C6), `[GEN]` sight-reading generator level 2
(two-hand, wider range, quarters/8ths). Songs: *Bach/Petzold — Minuet in G, BWV Anh. 114*
(easy edition) `[MT]`, *Mozart — Minuet in F, K.2* `[AUTH/MUTO]`, *Haydn — German Dance in G*
`[AUTH]`, *Türk — March in C* `[AUTH]`, *Beyer op. 101 nos. 12–20* `[MUTO/AUTH]`. Mastery: 5
generated sight-reads ≥ 85 %; one song.

**3.5 The sustain pedal (and why the HP-130 sends CC64).** Concepts: damper pedal, "legato
pedalling" (change pedal *after* the new chord sounds), pedal marks, don't blur harmony.
Exercises: `[GEN]` pedal-change drill on I–IV–V (the app reads CC64 and shows a pedal lamp;
scores the change timing), broken-chord LH with pedal. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Amazing Grace* (broken
chords + pedal), *Danny Boy* (Londonderry Air), *Ashgrove* (Welsh air), *Canon in D (very easy)*
`[MT]`. Videos: Bill Hilton (pedal), Josh Wright (pedalling). Mastery: pedal drill ≥ 90 %
"clean changes" (no chord overlaps > 120 ms).

**3.6 Broken chords, Alberti bass, waltz bass; accompaniment patterns.** Concepts: root-5th-
3rd patterns, Alberti (C-G-E-G), waltz "oom-pah-pah", 8th-note arpeggio in 4/4. Exercises:
`[GEN]` LH pattern drills in C/G/F/Am at 60→90 bpm, HT drills with RH scale over them. Songs
`[AUTH]`: *Beautiful Dreamer* (waltz bass), *Streets of Laredo* (waltz), *Oh Shenandoah*
(arpeggio), *Clementi — Sonatina op. 36 no. 1, bars 1–8 as Alberti drill* (preview),
*Lavender's Blue* (waltz). Videos: Bill Hilton (LH patterns/arpeggios). Mastery: two patterns
in two keys at 80 bpm; one song.

### Stage 4 — Fluency & technique (≈ Grade 1)

**4.1 Scales hands together: C, G, D, A (sharp side); the circle of fifths.** Concepts:
circle of fifths, standard fingerings (RH 1-2-3-1-2-3-4-5; LH 5-4-3-2-1-3-2-1), contrary
motion as a trick to learn HT, similar motion 1 octave → 2 octaves. Exercises: `[GEN]` scales HT
each key (contrary, similar, 1 & 2 octaves, 60→100 bpm). Songs: *Beethoven — Ecossaise in G,
WoO 23* `[PDMX/AUTH]`, *Schumann — Melody, op. 68 no. 1* `[PDMX/MUTO]`, *Gurlitt — op. 117 no. 1*,
*Le Couppey — op. 17 no. 1*. Mastery: 4 scales HT 2 octaves at 80 bpm in 8ths ≥ 95 %.

**4.2 Flat keys: F, Bb, Eb; minor scales Am, Em, Dm (harmonic & melodic).** As 4.1 for the
new keys. Songs: *Bach — Musette in D, BWV Anh. 126* `[MT/MUTO]`, *Bach — Minuet in G minor,
Anh. 115* `[MUTO/PDMX]`, *Schumann — Soldier's March op. 68 no. 2* `[PDMX]`, *Tchaikovsky —
Old French Song op. 39 no. 16* `[PDMX]`. Mastery: as 4.1.

**4.3 Arpeggios and chord inversions (all triads, root/1st/2nd).** Concepts: inversion
shapes, arpeggio fingering (RH 1-2-3-5 / LH 5-3-2-1), why inversions make smooth chord
changes. Exercises: `[GEN]` inversion drill (app shows symbol like "F/A", learner plays it),
`[GEN]` arpeggios HS 2 octaves C/G/F/Am/Dm/Em. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Amazing Grace (inversion
arrangement)*, *Simple Gifts*, *Morning Has Broken* (Bunessan, 3/4 broken chords), *Loch
Lomond*. Videos: Bill Hilton (inversions — a key lesson), Hoffman "inversions". Mastery: 30
random inversions ≥ 95 % within 3 s each.

**4.4 Hanon-style finger patterns (1–10) and independence.** Concepts: what Hanon does and
doesn't do (evenness, finger strength perception, but keep the wrist loose; never play through
pain), transposition of patterns. Exercises: `[GEN]` Hanon nos. 1–10 (generated, any key,
40→108 bpm; the generator builds the ascending/descending forms exactly). Mastery: 1–5 at 80
bpm HT ≥ 97 %.

**4.5 Compound time (6/8), triplets, swing preview, syncopation.** Concepts: 6/8 = 2 dotted-
quarter beats, triplet feel, ties across the beat, "off-beat" accents. Exercises: `[GEN]`
rhythm reading 6/8 + syncopation, `[GEN]` sight-reading level 3. Songs `[AUTH]`: *Greensleeves*
(6/8 proper), *Row Row Row* (6/8), *Hickory Dickory*, *Camptown Races* (syncopated), *Tarantella
(Neapolitan, simplified)*, *Scott Joplin — The Entertainer, main theme (easy)* `[MT]`. Mastery:
one 6/8 song + one syncopated song.

**4.6 Sight-reading & phrasing capstone; performance habit.** Concepts: reading ahead, keeping
going after mistakes, phrase shaping, dynamics plan, memorising a short piece, "performance
mode" in the app (no stopping, scored). Songs (choose two, different composers): *Bach —
Minuet in G Anh 114 (full)* `[MT]`, *Beethoven — Ecossaise*, *Clementi — Sonatina op. 36 no. 1,
mvt 1* `[PDMX/MUTO]` (stretch), *Burgmüller op. 100 no. 1 "La candeur"* `[PDMX/MUTO]`,
*Satie — Gymnopédie no. 1 (very easy arr.)* `[AUTH]`, *Für Elise (easy A-section)* `[MT]`.
Mastery: two pieces in Performance mode ≥ 90 % at 90 % tempo. **End of core path.**

Ashokan Farewell 1982, I'll Fly Away 1932, Bésame Mucho 1940, Por una Cabeza 1935, Bartók
Mikrokosmos 1940, Kabalevsky, Shostakovich, all film/game/pop). They can appear as **import
suggestions** with a note on where to buy/obtain MusicXML.

## Part G — Mastery, review, and progression rules (for the engine and curriculum JSON)

- `pass` = accuracy ≥ 90 % **and** tempo ≥ 80 % of target in Tempo mode (or, without MIDI:
  the learner self-reports after a Tempo-mode run — the app shows a "How did it go?" 3-button
  prompt and records it as self-assessed).
- `master` = accuracy ≥ 97 % at 100 % tempo, twice on different days.
- Lesson complete = **1 exercise passed + 1 item passed**, where the second item may be a song
  *or* another exercise (`00` D21). A unit whose skill no song tests — 1.5 reading by
  interval, 2.5 the first scale, 3.6 accompaniment patterns, the whole theory-and-ear track —
  sets `songOptional: true` in its lesson and is complete on two exercises. Setting: "require
  2 songs" for anyone who wants the stricter rule.
- **Every rung offers at least three alternatives** for each of `exerciseOptions` and
  `songOptions` (`00` D21). Where three songs do not exist, exercises make up the number and
  the lesson text says which skill they stand in for. `tools/content/validate.py` enforces
  the count; it is not left to the author's judgement.
- Unit complete = all lessons complete. Stage complete = all core units + the capstone.
- Review queue intervals: 1, 3, 7, 21 days after `pass`; an item drops out of review after
  `master`, but reappears in "Repertoire" every ~30 days.
- Prerequisites are advisory; "strict mode" locks items until prerequisites are passed.
- The placement test can mark whole stages `passed (placement)`.
