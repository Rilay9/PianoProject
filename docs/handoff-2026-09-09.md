### 3c. More room during a run — the owner's "use the sides, the tops, expanding and shrinking menu tabs"

What is worth doing, and what is not:

- **Do:** a shrink handle on the keys strip. A small `▾`/`▴` at the strip's right end,
  sideways only, that collapses the keyboard strip to the ribbon (32 px, the same
  `KeyRibbon`) for this run and back, without changing the `keys` setting. That is 24 px of
  360 back to the music during a run; the fit follows (the stage's `ResizeObserver` refits,
  and `setRunning`'s freeze happens 150 ms after the run starts, so shrink *before* pressing
  play keeps the larger size for the run — say so in the docs, and consider re-freezing at
  the new scale when the strip shrinks mid-run: `setRunning(false); setRunning(true)` is the
  cheap way). Persist the shrunk state per session in memory, not in settings.
- **Do:** the beat dot and the corner text share the stage's top edge; make sure they
  never overlap the first system's fingering digits at zoom 1.5+ (the corner is at
  `top: 4px; right: 8px`; the ink is anchored by the stave, so a high fingering can reach
  the top edge — if it does, move the corner into the bar's row instead while folded).
- **Do not:** make the run's scale follow each window. The Hanon picture (sideways, two
  bars, a hand's width of room under the bass staff) is the tallest-system reserve of P21e
  A2; the owner saw the room and read it as the bar's. The bar was not taking it: during a
  run the stage already extends under the bar (`[data-running='true'] .score-stage
  { margin-bottom: 0 }`). Per-window scale was the 13 % shrink and the 45 px stave jump the
  corpus measures against. If the owner wants the reserve smaller, the honest control is
  `FROZEN_OVERFLOW` and the probe's choice of "tallest system" (e.g. ignore the tallest 2 %
  of systems and let those overflow the margin) — propose it with numbers from the corpus
  before changing it.

### 4b. Waiting on the owner — a decision, not a fix

- **The grand staff.** Fourteen authored songs now print on one staff because the pipeline drops
  a bass staff of nothing but rests. Sound engineering; whether a beginner should see the silent
  staff is a teaching call.
- **What `barsPerWindow` means.** Setting 3 renders identically to setting 2 (both give one bar
  per slot, three slots) on Au Clair de la Lune. `barsPerSlot = ⌊n/2⌋` was written when SLOTS
  meant exactly two systems; packing to 2–4 broke the arithmetic, so the number on screen no
  longer follows the setting. Three ways out: cap the slot count so the total honours the
  setting; relabel it *bars per system* and drop ⌊n/2⌋; or round odd numbers up. `08` §4.1
  still states the old rule and is stale either way.
