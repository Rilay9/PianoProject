# The plan from 2026-09-25: diagnose the learning system, then fix it at the model

**Where it comes from.** On 2026-09-25 the owner shared an outside audit of this project
and of the working method used here (`audit-2026-09-25-outside.md`), upgraded the usage
plan, and asked for a plan built around the audit's good recommendations, carried out by
builder-tier agents with briefs that carry the details, the guardrails and the room to
think. The working-method changes are already made (`operating-procedure.md`, the top of
`CLAUDE.md`, the stop hook, `00-invariants.md` §1a); this file is the work.

**What the audit says, in one line each.** The harness had become better at proving work
was not misreported than at deciding what the work should be; put product truth first.
Treat the app as a learning system (objective → experience → performance → evidence →
skill state → next experience), not a list of screens. Distrust one-number difficulty,
blunt fallbacks, generators without a stated purpose, feedback that scores without
diagnosing, and two definitions of one concept. Diagnose before implementing: trace real
data through the pipeline, find the five most consequential problems, classify them, and
stop for the owner's review before another large build cycle.

**Verdicts on the recommendations.** All of the last message's method changes are
adopted. One is adopted with a change: "You → the assistant → tools" would remove the builder
agents the owner mandates, so it becomes *agents build, the orchestrator reads enough to
brief well and to judge, and no agent hands off to another except after a crash*. Of the
twenty-point procedure, everything is adopted as a lens; two are scoped rather than taken
whole, because each could become a rebuild of a working subsystem: treating PDMX as an
excerpt corpus (§5) is an experiment on one rung after the diagnosis, and the learner
model (§3, §4) is built as the smallest change that establishes the right model, then
expanded (§14). Nothing is rejected outright.

**What is folded in from the plan of 2026-09-21.** The window fit's two red specs and the
un-run rotate spec (T34, Entry 63); the five state-machine choices (T33, briefed); the
three rungs not built for want of music (`latin.4`, `latin.8`, `ragtime.4`); six carols not
homed; the stale local Windows screenshot baselines; the usability items of Entry 49 that
need a real session; and the standing fact that nothing has been heard on a piano. The
genre corrections of 2026-09-17 (rock is a style, not those songs; the earliest *honest*
stage for each element; the public build carries the personal items) are checked by the
diagnosis rather than assumed done.

Three rules the reviewer added, now in every brief: every trace runs the object all the
way to the next recommendation, because the question is whether performance produces
evidence that changes what comes next, and whether that differs by content type; each
hypothesis below is reported as *supported by observed evidence*, *contradicted by
observed evidence* or *unresolved*, never "confirmed" because a code path is consistent
with it; and each trace fills the rows it touched of a source-of-truth table (learner
level, difficulty, skill, mastery, performance evidence, repertoire level, curriculum
stage: source of truth, consumers, competing definitions), which the synthesis merges and
fixes nothing in.

**Then the synthesis, by the orchestrator, not an agent:** the three traces, the window
classification, the merged source-of-truth table, and the orchestrator's own reading of
`selectors.ts`, `session.ts`, `Scoring.ts`, `progressStore.ts` and `02` Part G become
`docs/prompts/diagnosis-2026-09-25.md`: the five most consequential problems, each with
the current implementation, why it is a problem, evidence in the code and data, its
class, the recommended direction, and *now or deferred*; and a line per hypothesis below
saying what became of it. **Then stop.** The owner reads it and says which go ahead. That
is the one decision in this plan that is his: it sets product direction. The test the
reviewer set for the synthesis: does it change its model where the evidence contradicts
it, rather than build a longer case for the hypotheses it started with.

The orchestrator's hypotheses going in, written so the traces can refute them, and held
loosely — the traces are an investigation, not a confirmation exercise, and a different
architectural problem, if the evidence points there, is the finding:

- **H1 (P1?)** The learner model is completion plus best numbers. A progress row is
  status, best accuracy, best tempo, attempts; a skill row is a self-assessed state; the
  session builder takes the *stage number* of the next incomplete rung as the learner's
  level; nothing reads a run's hot spots, timing or missed concepts when choosing what
  comes next; review is calendar-only.
- **H2 (P2?)** Feedback scores without diagnosing. The engine measures timing, hot spots,
  articulation, voicing and shaping; the summary shows numbers and offers a weak-bars
  loop; whether it ever says *what this probably means* and *what to do next* is the
  question.
- **H3 (P1?)** Difficulty has several definitions: `level` (stage.decimal), `levelSource`,
  `abrsmGradeApprox`, the nineteen-feature model collapsed to one number in Python and in
  a TypeScript port, `levelBand` per rung, `levelOverrides`, per-rung pass numbers against
  one global master rule. The features are multi-dimensional and collapse before any
  selection sees them.
- **H4 (P1?)** Fallbacks are blunt and can become the curriculum: technique and new slots
  fall back to anything within one level of the stage, review to anything at or below the
  stage plus one, jam to a track, and the swap sheet to anything of the same type within a
  level.
- **H5 (P2?)** Generator families have invariants and pictures (T13) but no stated
  purpose per family and no check that the target skill is present and no other hard
  skill was introduced.
- **H6 (P2)** PDMX is used at the piece level only; a 4–8 bar excerpt of a hard piece is
  never a learning experience.
- **H7 (P2?)** Some lesson prose explains the app (the *Tools for this rung* paragraphs)
  rather than the music; part of that was deliberate (T19, T28) and the line needs
  drawing.
- **H8 (record)** No musical judgement has been made by ear; the structural checks stand
  in for it everywhere.

## Rules for the waves

- **An experience before an item** (Part 9): every selection first chooses the kind of experience
  the learner needs, then the content; a feature is a teaching tool, never a destination. The
  choice responds to evidence, curriculum intent, retention and transfer, the learner's goals and
  well-rounded exposure (L26), never only to the weakest measured skill.
- **Evidence grammars differ by experience** (L24): sight-reading, technique, ear, harmony,
  improvisation, repertoire and PDF or external practice each yield their own kind of evidence,
  and one learner model consumes all without pretending they are measured alike.
- **Provenance on every content object** (R35): where it came from, what was done to it, what was
  measured, inferred or supplied by a person, and by which version.
- **Extend the harnesses that exist** (Q13, U30): the four Playwright configurations, the render
  workflow, `devicePreview.ts` and the content tooling grow into what a wave needs; no new framework.

- Agents run on the builder tier (the builder tier), never the orchestrator's. One bounded
  task per agent; the brief is self-contained per `operating-procedure.md` §13; no agent
  continues another's transcript except after a crash, and then from a compact handoff.
- One Playwright user at a time on port 4173. Read-only traces run beside a builder.
- One verification chain per wave, run by the orchestrator, exit codes from unpiped runs:
  content build, `tsc -b`, lint, vitest, `build:app`, the named Playwright specs one at a
  time. Commit named paths at every stopping point; push after a green chain, as the
  owner allowed on 2026-09-22; CI read, not theorised about.
- Every report: judgement first; technical and pedagogical verdicts apart; unverified
  beside what passes; per fix the mechanism, the discriminating test, before and after.
- The owner is asked when a choice materially redefines the product's educational
  philosophy or the learner's experience: the curriculum's core shape, what a mastery
  threshold is, how memory is taught, what the musicality strand means, how much
  familiar versus exploratory repertoire, the score-size control, how the portrait
  preview behaves, the repertoire progression. Ordinary design and implementation
  choices are made and stated in a line. (Reworded 2026-09-25 on the reviewer's note
  that "asked once" was too absolute.)
