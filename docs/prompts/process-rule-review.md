## The changes that save the most time

1. **Collapse the three fast-path/pre-review rules into one** (OP:253, OP:257, FABLE:197): pre-review only for a new product rule, schema meaning, inference rule, authority or broad migration; everything else lands and is reviewed after. Saves a review round per ordinary seam.
2. **Drop the mandatory seven-question pre-action gate and seven-check post-action gate** (OP:263, OP:296, anti-drift 16/17): they restate the preflight and the report questions and add a paragraph to every brief and handoff.
3. **Replace the per-ruling clause-to-file/test/CI map** (FABLE:196, CLAUDE:103-109, OP:299-305) with one sentence: a rule claimed in force names its enforcing check in the same edit, else says "not yet enforced". Saves a map per ruling per landing and a reviewer check.
4. **Delete the duplicate copies** (OP:24-37, OP:159-173, OP:199-229, OP:236-240, OP:265, OP:352, anti-drift 1/2/5/8/13/18): the same rules are stated two to four times and drift apart (OP and CLAUDE already differ on the closing question).
5. **Scale the generated-content gates by job** (FABLE:89-104, FABLE:141, FABLE:143): a mechanical CONTROL drill needs one independent check, not Hypothesis plus near-miss plus witness-independence note plus outside-reviewer read, and the brief should point at the chain record instead of restating it.
