## The grammar (`tools/content/study.py`; `docs/02` Part E2)

Harmony first, then the tune; a grammar and a distribution, never one template.

- **Forms, in four-bar phrases.** 8 bars: an antecedent closing on the dominant (a half cadence) and a consequent restating the antecedent's first two bars and closing on an authentic cadence. 12 bars: antecedent, a contrasting phrase closing on the dominant, consequent. 16 bars: antecedent, consequent, contrast, and the consequent again. The restated bars are declared (`drill.study.restated`), and neither the evaluator nor the pedagogical gate counts them as repetition.
- **Progressions.** Antecedent `I x y V`, with `x y` one of `IV I`, `vi IV`, `I IV`, `V I`, `ii IV`. Consequent: the antecedent's first two chords, then `V | I`, `V7 | I`, `IV V | I` or `ii V | I`. Contrast: `IV I IV V`, `vi IV I V`, `IV V I V`, `ii IV I V`. Minor: `i x y V` with `x y` from `iv i`, `VI iv`, `i iv`, `V i`; closing `V | i`, `V7 | i`, `iv V | i`; contrast `iv i iv V`, `VI iv i V`, `iv V i V`. The minor's dominant carries its leading tone (spelled by interval: E G♯ B in A minor, A C♯ E G in D minor). The hands-together target moves the opening bars of each phrase through two chords a bar (`I V`, `IV I`, `ii V` …), never the cadence's own bar. A chord whose root the left hand cannot reach at the rung is not drawn: at 2.1 the hand keeps a five-note position, so `I ii IV V` (and `V7`, whose seventh a held root leaves to the tune) and not `vi`.
- **The left hand**: the texture over the progression — a held root (at the pre-2.5 rungs within a fifth: C3 D3 F3 G3 in C), blocked chords, broken chords (1-3-5-3; 1-3-5 in 3/4 and twice in 6/8), Alberti (1-5-3-5), a waltz bass (root, then the chord twice); the last bar a root and a held chord, so a pattern stays a pattern in every bar; F2 to middle C, off ledger lines.
- **The tune.** A cell for bar 1 (the motif) from the metre's cells the rung has taught (quarters and halves at 2.1; eighths from 2.2; the dotted quarter from 2.4; off-beat quarters only for the syncopation target), restated or varied in the bars after it; a cadence cell holding each phrase's last note a beat or more (a breath after a half cadence where the cell has one). Pitches by a walk guided toward a contour chosen per phrase (arch, ascent, descent, valley): strong beats on the chord's tones; **against the contour's direction only a step back**; a leap recovered by a step the other way; never a third strike of one pitch; no rocking; the note before a phrase's last a step from it (else the dominant); the consequent restating the antecedent's opening; the contrasting phrase carrying the motif's interval shape to its own chord (a sequence); for position-shift, the contrasting phrase in a second five-note position (up a fourth where the staff has room, else down).
- **The hard layer** (valid first): the rung's taught set in the realiser's own terms (rhythms, the five-note position, the key, off ledger lines), the leap cap (a fifth), every melodic interval diatonic in quality (no tritone, augmented second, diminished fourth or augmented fifth), no pitch struck three times running, every phrase closing on its cadence's tone and not on a repeated note, the close approached by step or from the dominant, the target at the recipe's density (skips and steps; eighths; off-beat notes; the widened position; the right hand sounding at every left-hand strike), no bar repeated exactly beyond the grammar's restatement. A draw breaking any is never scored.
- **The choice**: up to 24 valid candidates within 600 draws, scored by the musical evaluator; those at or above the valid draws' lower quartile of notes (D1's density floor) and at or above the musical floor with no wrong cadence are eligible; the earliest within 0.01 of the best is kept. None eligible: refused with the reason (`StudyRefusal`), and the build stops.

## The evaluator (`tools/content/musical_evaluator.py`), its parts and weights

**Port, not bridge — the call and its reason.** D1's scorer (`app/src/engine/sightReadingScore.ts`) models a four-bar phrase in a major key and infers the harmony from the left hand's first note in a bar; a study has several phrases, may be minor and declares its harmony. A bridge would call a scorer that cannot read a study, and the scorer is the sight-reading generator's module, which D3 may not change. So the parts are ported, and every ported part is held equal to its TypeScript twin on a shared fixture of 51 constructed phrases (`tools/content/tests/fixtures/evaluator_twins.json`): `app/tests/unit/studyEvaluatorTwin.test.ts` holds the scorer to the recorded values (and records them with `STUDY_TWIN_WRITE=1`), `test_musical_evaluator.py` holds the port to the same values. The bridge's precondition — reading a written file — held (D1's helper reads MusicXML); it was the semantics that ruled it out, not the file.

**The study's semantics, stated and tested** (each on a case built to pass and one to fail; parity on major four-bar phrases proves none of them):

- *Degrees in the key's own scale*: the major, or the natural minor with a raised degree read as the degree it raises (D1's rule, which the minor's leading tone needs: G♯ is A minor's seventh degree, a step below the tonic).
- *The harmony declared*: each bar's chords from the recipe's progression (`drill.study.progression`), a note judged against the chord sounding at its onset; never inferred from the left hand (a first-inversion V read from its bass note is vii).
- *An arrival per phrase*: D1's rhythm grades on each phrase's final event, a close judged against that phrase's own cadence (an authentic cadence's tonic, or third or fifth for a half; a half cadence's tone of V), and the approach (by step, or from a tone of the chord before); the phrases' mean, the last counting twice.
- *A cadence per phrase* (a part of its own): the progression has the cadence the phrase claims (V then I; V), and the melody closes on its chord's tone. A cadence on a note outside its chord is wrong, and the gate refuses the item whatever the total.
- *The motif on the opening cell*: the first bar's rhythm under other pitches, or its shape at another pitch, in a bar the grammar does not restate (D1's part counts any two bars sharing a rhythm, which two bars of half notes always do); exact repeats beyond the grammar's restatement and beyond one cost a half each.
- *Contour, rests, beginning, harmony, leaps*: D1's functions as they stand, the harmony read from the declaration.

| Part | Weight | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| arrival | 4 | an ending per phrase is what makes eight bars of legal notes a piece in phrases; below D1's 5 because the cadence now carries part of the ending |
| cadence | 3 | the cadence that makes each ending one, and the authentic close |
| contour | 3 | as D1: one shape per four bars against a line that wanders or rocks |
| motif | 2 | a study promises a cell restated and varied — heavier than in a sight-reading phrase |
| harmony | 2 | a tune agreeing with the progression it declares |
| beginning | 1 | as D1 |
| rests | 1 | as D1; rests fall at a phrase's breath |
| leaps | 0.5 | a tiebreaker below the cap, never a reason to lose the target's skips |

**The floor: 0.80**, below which the realiser refuses a candidate and the gate an item; any wrong cadence refused whatever the total. Set from the distribution table: every study the grammar wrote on the suite's seeds cleared it (the worst 0.86), and the random walk's median fell below it for every recipe (0.74–0.78).
