## 2. Product architecture: content is not one bucket

The target teaching system should reason:

**Learner need → musical requirements → choose best content source → validate that content for its actual role → present it → measure what can actually be measured → update learner model**

Possible content sources have different jobs:

1. **Controlled generated exercise**
   - isolate a narrow skill;
   - exact allowed demands;
   - useful when precision matters more than musical richness.

2. **Generated musical mini-piece**
   - controlled demands plus phrase/harmony/musical coherence;
   - requires musical-quality review beyond mechanical validity.

3. **Real excerpt / PDMX excerpt**
   - transfer into authentic music;
   - exact passage can be first-class content;
   - title/genre metadata never substitutes for score inspection.

4. **Full repertoire piece**
   - integrate several established skills musically;
   - may be transfer, stretch, shelf, project or performance material.

5. **External recommendation**
   - best musical choice may not live in the app;
   - the app can still recommend it honestly and explain why.

A legitimate teaching sequence can be:

**lesson/demo → controlled exercise → generated sight-read → authentic excerpt → full piece → independent application**

Do not hard-code:
- “sight-reading = generator”
- “real music = PDMX”
- “song on rung = it teaches every named concept”
- “metadata constraints satisfied = good piano material”

## 5. Review model for every important ability

Do not ask only whether a concept appears somewhere.

For major abilities, look for a defensible development cycle:

### CONTROL → MODEL / TRANSFER → MUSIC → INDEPENDENCE

1. **CONTROL**
   - isolated enough to acquire correctly.

2. **MODEL / TRANSFER**
   - real excerpt, simple piece, lead sheet or structured task applies it.

3. **MUSIC**
   - contributes to a satisfying piece, accompaniment, improvisation, arrangement or performance.

4. **INDEPENDENCE**
   - learner can recognize when to use it, choose it, recover when it fails, and apply it without step-by-step prompting.

Not every rung needs four separate items.
But an important ability should not be named once and disappear.

## 11. Generated content: what to improve and what not to rebuild

Do not start by adding more generator infrastructure.

First enumerate the **current** generator families/contracts; do not inherit a stale family count.

### Four useful generated-content jobs

#### A. Canonical technical drills
Scales, arpeggios, inversions, repeated notes, octaves, etc.

Mechanical checks can establish notation/structure.
They cannot establish healthy movement, fingering use, tone or relaxation unless directly observed.

#### B. Progressive sight-reading
This is the highest-value generator-quality experiment.

Use source-backed progression constraints:
- keys;
- metre;
- rhythm;
- range;
- hand configuration;
- position changes;
- articulation;
- accidentals;
- density;
- polyphony;
- length.

Then generate short **musical phrases**, not merely valid random notes.

#### C. Named style/pattern drills
Examples:
- Alberti;
- waltz/oom-pah;
- broken chord;
- stride;
- boogie;
- walking bass;
- clave;
- tresillo;
- tumbao;
- montuno.

Use:
**sourced definition → explicit family contract → independent structural checker**

Do not resurrect a universal arbitrary-score classifier.

#### D. Musical mini-pieces
Highest risk.

Preference order:
1. real public-domain excerpt;
2. simplified/authored arrangement of a public-domain tune;
3. curated phrase/harmony/accompaniment templates;
4. freer generated mini-piece only when the first three cannot serve.

Any generator family that promises “music” needs human musical audition/curation.

### Useful libraries/reference tools

- **music21** — independent symbolic checks, harmony/interval/key/voice-leading where appropriate.
- **Hypothesis** — property-based adversarial testing and minimized counterexamples.
- constraint solvers only where they delete brittle custom search, not as general architecture.

Do not make a library a pedagogy oracle.

## 13. Technical score-intake gate — narrower than pedagogy

Before a PDMX/import/finder score is treated as a supported piano asset, perform a cheap technical gate.

It should answer only whether the file is a sane supported score asset:

- parses/normalizes as MusicXML/MXL;
- not empty/effectively empty/truncated/obviously corrupted;
- pitched notation rather than percussion/unpitched content;
- inspect actual XML parts/staves rather than trusting PDMX CSV/MIDI-program metadata;
- classify actual asset shape rather than pretending every “piano” row is ordinary solo piano;
- accept supported piano-only shapes such as:
  - `solo_piano`
  - `piano_duet`
  - `lead_sheet`
  - `melody_only`
- classify unsupported/mixed shapes separately:
  - `vocal_with_accompaniment`
  - `ensemble`
  - `non_piano`
  - `malformed`
- sensible note/time/bar structure;
- supported/playable piano range;
- successful render/readback/cursor/round-trip checks where applicable.

### Crucial boundary

This gate does **not** certify:
- musical quality;
- style;
- difficulty;
- arrangement quality;
- lesson fit;
- teaching usefulness.

Keep:
**score admissibility first; musical/pedagogical verification second.**

- calling the curriculum complete before the explicit track denominator is met.

---

## 22. Final finish condition for this restart phase

The curriculum/content gate is ready to hand off to the representative learner journey when:

1. every current curriculum track has an explicit review record;
2. materially important omissions/wrong teaching have been fixed or consciously scoped out;
3. the major cross-track abilities have been checked for recurrence and transfer;
4. content-limited promised rungs have a defensible decision;
5. primary acquisition material is not being confused with transfer/project material;
6. generated claims are independently checked only to the scope actually provable;
7. PDMX/library admission is separate from curriculum admission;
8. remaining unknowns are explicit;
9. the final record states the exact denominator and scope.

The goal is not to prove PianoProject is a complete substitute for teachers, conservatories or a lifetime of repertoire.

The goal is a curriculum that is **trustworthy enough that a motivated learner can follow it hard without later discovering that a major foundational bridge was omitted or materially taught wrong.**
