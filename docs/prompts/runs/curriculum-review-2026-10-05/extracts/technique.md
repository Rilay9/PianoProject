## 4. Sequencing concerns

1. **Two lessons describe different starts for the same exercise.** Core `4.1.md:29-31` says "Start both thumbs on the same C and move outwards: now the hands are exact mirrors", and lists `exercise.scale.c-major.2oct.contrary.both.2` as a 4.1 option. `technique.4.md:26-30` calls that same exercise "the one to start on: the left hand begins on the C above the right hand's, so in the first bar they pass through each other." The notation agrees with technique.4 and not with 4.1: right hand C4 up to C6, left hand C5 down to C3 (read from the file; first bar RH C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5, LH C5 B4 A4 G4 F4 E4 D4 C4; the pairs run (C4,C5) ... (G4,F4), so the left hand begins to the right of the right hand and the hands pass at beat 4). The one-octave form `exercise.scale.c-major.1oct.contrary.both.2` starts both hands on C4 and diverges, as 4.1 describes. All twelve two-octave contrary files have the same construction; mechanism: `tools/content/generate_exercises.py:1085-1089, 1101-1102`, where the left hand's start is its own low octave and the contrary run reads from the top of that range. See Section 5 item 1.
2. **The rung's pass requirement is not tied to its promise.** Every technique unit's requirement is `{kind: runs, from: exercises, count: 2}` with mastery 0.9 accuracy and 0.8 tempo (`stage-4.json` to `stage-8.json`, unit entries). Any two of the 13 exercises pass `technique.4`, so a learner can pass it on two legato phrases and never play a scale; the lesson says "Two exercises pass this rung. Take the scales at a tempo where the thumb is silent" (`technique.4.md:46-47`) and the rung has one scale. Same shape at technique.6 (two arpeggios pass; voicing and the held-melody pedal are never required) and technique.7 (the half pedal is never required). This is a design fact about requirements, not a lesson defect; it bears on the honesty of each "How you'll know you've got it".
3. **A step in tempo from drill to étude at stage 4.** The generated scale drills are written at 60-72 (eighths, 2.0-2.4 notes per second); Lemoine Nos. 1 and 2 carry a tempo mark of quarter = 180 in 2/2 with eighth-note scales (6 notes per second at the marked tempo, 4.8 at the 80 % mastery line; read from the files: `bpm 180`, referent quarter). Whether that is a sensible first-étude target is unverified as music; the mark is the file's own. Recorded as a lead, not a verdict.
4. **Pedal sequence is sound as laid out**: legato pedalling in core `3.5` (stage 3), held melody `technique.6` (stage 6), half pedal `technique.7` (stage 7). The half pedal presupposes a pedal that sends continuous CC64; the lesson says so (`technique.7.md:41-43`).
5. **Voicing is taught twice at the same stage in two tracks, each with half.** `technique.6.md:32-39` has the measure and no physical cue; `classical.6.md:17-24` has the physical cue and the practice method (melody alone, accompaniment alone, both) and no measure. Neither points to the other. A learner-facing split, not a contradiction.
6. **Prerequisites used before taught: none found** in the five lessons for what they assert. The stage 3 Alberti figure (`technique.6.md:18-19`) is taught at `3.6`. Sight-reading level 4 (`technique.5.md:46-48`) is a generator level, not a topic.
