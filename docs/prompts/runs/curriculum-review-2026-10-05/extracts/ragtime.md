## 3. Missing or weak abilities

### 3.1 Job 1: the bridge into full syncopated rags

1. **A level-appropriate two-hand MODEL between Greensleeves (3.6) and the Entertainer (7.1).** Ladder levels on rung 5: 3.6, 4.1, 6.8, 6.8, 7.0, 7.1 (`docs/generated/ladder.md:222`). The first step is one 16-bar piece in 3/4 whose left hand moves little (verified `song.folk.greensleeves.waltz` bars 1-15: A2 then C3+E3, E2 then G#2+D3); the next are complete 85-148-bar Joplin works that the 425 CSV already labels `EXCERPT_TRANSFER` or `TRANSFER_STRETCH` ("full 148-bar work is too large; use excerpt", "not the cleanest first ragtime texture model"). `ragtime.5.md:18-20` calls the leaps "the technical problem of the whole style" and `:51-52` tells the learner to get them silent on the marches, but the marches' left hands are bass-and-chord with modest hand shifts (Combination March bars 4-19: bass Bb2/F2/C3/Eb3/D3 against chords between F3 and Eb4); the octave-and-tenth reach is first written in the Entertainer's later strains and Maple Leaf. External evidence that a competent curriculum includes this stage: Matz (steady left hand plus a simple syncopated motif first), Wikipedia Ragtime and Cakewalk (the march/cakewalk oompah is the base), and the dossier's own section 5.3. Scope searched: ladder rung 5 options, stage-5 JSON, the four candidates below. Severity: foundational bridge, partly met (see 3.3).
2. **CONTROL for the leap.** Absent as a named option on rungs 5 to 8, present only at rung 9 (`exercise.oompah.c.tenth`, `exercise.stride.c`). Scope: `exerciseOptions` of the five ragtime lessons, read in full from stage-5 to stage-9 JSON. Severity: depth (data-only fix).
3. **A figure-specific rhythm CONTROL for 16th-8th-16th.** None of the generic rhythm options writes it (section 2). Severity: depth.
4. **INDEPENDENCE.** No rung asks the learner to choose, build or recover a left hand: every left hand is written out and nothing asks for an oom-pah from chord symbols (12th Street Rag, rung 5, is a one-staff lead sheet with 28 chord symbols but the lesson frames it as "a right hand that is one figure repeated", `ragtime.5.md:44-47`). Scope: `ragtime.9.md` read in full. Severity: depth.
6. **Stop-time** is named at `ragtime.8.md:30-34` as "not optional" with no score or exercise that contains it. Severity: enrichment (the lesson is honest about the Library copy; a promised concept has no station).

### 3.3 My verdict on the audit's "GREEN/AMBER" for `ragtime.5`

I agree with the staged workflow (left hand alone, then right hand alone counting sixteenths, then four bars at half tempo, `ragtime.5.md:38-40`; the success test is one strain). Where the evidence changed it: (i) the "oom-pah on its own" repertoire is four complete works levelled 3.6, 6.8, 6.8 and 7.0, so the learner meets the first leap-bearing material only at 85-148 bars; (ii) the Greensleeves option has almost no left-hand travel; (iii) the control exercises exist but are not on the rung. ## 4. Sequencing concerns

- **Leap named before it is written or drilled.** `ragtime.5.md:18-20` ("the technical problem of the whole style") and `:51-52` ("Get the leaps silent on one of those before you open *The Entertainer*") against: Greensleeves has no leap (bars 1-15), the three marches have modest shifts (Combination March bars 4-19), and the octave-and-tenth CONTROL exercise is a rung-9 option. The real leap is deferred to rungs 6 and 7 prose (`ragtime.6.md:44-56`, `ragtime.7.md:15-21`) without a drill.
- **School of Ragtime sits on rung 6 but does rung 5's job.** The shipped score (`song.ragtime.joplin-school-of-ragtime`, 33 bars, key C, 2/4, level 6.4) is, in bars 1-24, a syncopated right hand over steady repeated eighth-note chords in the left hand (bar 1 LH: four eighth chords, F4-C4-A3-F3 and D#4-C4-A3-F#3), and in bars 25-32 a single line doubled at the octave. It has no oom-pah leaps and no strain structure. `ragtime.6.md:70-72` says Joplin wrote it "to teach exactly this" (the rung's subject is multi-strain form and the leaping left hand). What the notation demonstrates is **syncopated right hand over a metrically stable left hand**, the exact job of rung 5. (The duet instruction at `ragtime.6.md:98-101`, "syncopation placed against a bass that is not also your problem", is accurate for bars 1-24.) The audit's "GREEN, authentic pedagogical source" is right about authenticity; the placement and the description do not fit what the notation holds.
- **Rung 7 is a topic list in its options.** The lesson teaches stride precursor, three non-rags (habanera *Solace*, waltz *Bethena*, slow drag *Heliotrope Bouquet*), edition comparison and the secondary rag, but the CONTROL for the stride and the secondary rag (`exercise.stride.c`, `exercise.secondary-rag.c.4bar`) are rung-9 options. The lesson text is a development; the option set is not.
- **Rung 9 is the first place the stride and secondary-rag studies appear** (`ragtime.9.md:28-37`), after the rags that need them (Maple Leaf at 7, Pine Apple at 8). The lesson says the left hand must be automatic first; the options place its studies at the memory stage.
- **Design versus lesson:** `docs/02-curriculum.md` D5 puts "The Entertainer (easy arr.)" at Stage 5 and the original at Stage 6; the shipped rung 5 offers the original 92-bar edition (`song.ragtime.joplin-entertainer`, `Moderato = 70`) on both rungs. The lesson is what ships, and it makes the one-strain scope explicit.
- The prerequisite of rung 5 is `4.5` only (stage-5 JSON). The duple bass-chord alternation is not taught in core: `3.6.md:28-31` is the waltz bass in 3/4, and the grep in section 0 finds an oom-pah named only at `4.7.md:48`, `holiday.4.md:29-31` and in the exercises. The "simple bass-chord accompaniment" the bridge is supposed to extend (dossier TRACK 8) is, in core, a 3/4 waltz pattern; the 2/4 or 4/4 version enters at ragtime.5 untaught except by prose.

- **B. Transposition recurring:** not touched. `ragtime.6.md:36-38` separates reading in flat keys from transposing into them; no ragtime transposition task (the generated oom-pah exercises exist in several keys, which is practice, not transposition).
- **C. Memory as structure plus retrieval:** touched at rung 9 (`ragtime.9.md:16-33`): structure (strains, key plan), three memory types, a mid-strain start test, Blind then Perform. Missing: seam practice and recovery after interruption (3.1 item 5).
- **D. Ear training with production:** weakly touched. `ragtime.9` lists `drill.ear.tune-long` ("play back a tune, eight bars", a generic G-major melody drill at level 8.4, no ragtime content, not mentioned in the lesson text). Rung 5 relies on the ear for straightness (self-check).
- **E. Score study before playing:** touched at rung 6 (`ragtime.6.md:25-32` mark the trio change and find the newly flattened note; `:65-68` trace the repeat route and say the letters) and rung 8 (find every occurrence of a named figure, `:17-22`). No reusable "before you play" task.
- **F. Performance and recovery:** touched at rung 9 (`ragtime.9.md:54-58` Perform mode, one pass, no restarts). Recovery after a mistake is not taught.
