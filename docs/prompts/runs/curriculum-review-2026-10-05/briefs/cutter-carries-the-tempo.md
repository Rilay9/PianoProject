**What governs:** FABLE §6 (the app never pretends) and the never-teach-wrong rule: a cut that plays at 96 where the page prints ♩ = 60 teaches a wrong tempo and sets Keep tempo's pass pair at 77, above the written tempo. Found by the latin.4 probe (Entry 241 in `docs/pending-review.md`; read its "Defects and gaps" paragraph first).

**Mechanism as reported, to be confirmed at the code before editing:** `tools/content/excerpts.py` removes every note of the unselected staff for `selection: left` or `right`; the tempo mark (`<direction>` with `<sound tempo>` or a metronome mark) sits on the upper staff of a grand staff, so a left-hand cut loses it and the converter's default 96 is written, with no `tempo-defaulted` tag and a catalogue label "authored, via the parent's".
