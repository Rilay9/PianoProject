# Brief: the chain record and its checker (FABLE.md §2 step 2, §3; 2026-10-06)

**What governs:** `docs/prompts/FABLE.md` §3 defines the record, its fields and the checker's rules; this brief does not restate them. Read FABLE.md whole first, then `ORCHESTRATION-CONTRACT.md` §1 (field meanings) and `MODE-SHEET.md` (the tool vocabulary and what each tool records), then `briefs/probe-latin4-bizet.md` lines 48-140 (the first record's content: the instructional chain, the failure route, the independence test, the completion treatment).

**Why this and not something else.** The teaching design of an ability must be data the build checks, because prose rules are followed only while they are the last thing read (the owner, 2026-10-06). Alternatives: keep the three brief headings and a reviewer's eye (rejected: no check fails when a builder forgets); a general curriculum schema with a validator over every rung (rejected: machinery before a second record exists). What would reverse it: the first record proving unwritable in the §3 shape, in which case the builder stops and reports the field, not a redesign.

**Finish condition (FABLE §2 step 2):** the checker runs in CI; the Bizet chain `docs/chains/A7c.1.yaml` is the first record and passes as a `draft` with its unresolved refs listed; one deliberately broken record fails for each rule; the brief lint rejects a major curriculum brief missing the three headings.

## Decisions taken here (FABLE §10: the brief states them)

- **Tool vocabulary.** The set of tool names is read from `MODE-SHEET.md`'s section headings (sections 1 to 30 and the sub-modes 7a, 7b, plus the Score-screen settings Blind, Loop, Ladder, Duet, Rhythm only, Perform as the sheet names them), normalised to the sheet's own words; the checker prints the vocabulary with `--tools`. One value outside the sheet is allowed and documented in the checker: `lesson`, for an explanation step on the lesson page, because FABLE §3's example and the probe's step 1 need it and MODE-SHEET has no section for the lesson page. Option not taken: forbidding it, which would make every chain's first step unwritable.
- **Ref resolution.** A `ref` resolves when it is a path that exists in the tree (with an optional `#anchor`), an exercise or song id present in `content/catalog.static.json` or produced by a family in `tools/content/family_contracts.json` (match by family id or by the id's prefix as the generators name items; say which), a family id in that file, or a CID present in `content/sources/pdmx.json`. A `draft` may hold unresolved refs; the checker lists them.
- **Scaffold subset.** Scaffolds are compared as sets of strings after trimming and lower-casing; the rule is exact wording, so the record's scaffold items are written as reusable tokens (for example `notation`, `cursor`, `click`, `count-in`, `tempo slider`, `app plays the other hand`). The A7c.1 record uses such tokens.

## Harness and boundaries

`operating-procedure.md` §14 is the harness. Python is `py -3.11` with `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`; tests run with `py -3.11 -m unittest tools.content.tests.test_check_chains`.
