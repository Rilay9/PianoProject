# Brief: a hold is part of the offer's material identity: a held run credits no requirement of the judging rung, and the card shows the held level (lane SR3; 2026-10-06)

**What governs:** `docs/review/responses/sr2-landing.md` §2 and §3 (read whole: the rule and the five acceptance cases are the specification, and "no broader evidence rewrite is authorized"); Entry 258 in `docs/pending-review.md` (what SR2 built: the unanchored daily read held to the learner's taught set, judged at the offered-from rung, the hold carried in the route and in the run's stored `material`); FABLE §6 (evidence honesty) and §10 (a ruled evidence rule, built under the ruling without another design round); `docs/prompts/operating-procedure.md` §10b, §13, §14. Nothing heard.

**The rule (the reviewer's words):** the judging rung may still define the evidence semantics, but rung-requirement credit is refused when the stored generation hold is below or different from that judging rung. Evidence belongs to the learner wherever observed; requirement credit does not cross the material boundary that made the observation easier or narrower. A hold is part of the learner-facing material identity for this offer, not an invisible generator detail, so the card shows the hold's level while held.

**Decision rationale (operating-procedure §10b).** Learner problem: five daily runs held at 1.1 (steps only) meet rung 1.5's `reads` requirement, its interval-reading "familiar" and one of its two exercise runs (Entry 258, OPEN 1), so the "Steps and skips" rung is mostly complete before the learner meets a skip; and the card prints "L1.5" over a phrase held at 1.1 (OPEN 2). Solution classes: (a) re-label held rows as 1.1 credit (refused by the ruling unless 1.1 independently asks for them); (b) **chosen, as ruled:** keep the learner's global skill evidence for what was observed; refuse rung-requirement credit (reads, exercise-run, skill requirements) for a run whose stored material says it was held below or different from the judging rung, immediately and retroactively; new unheld 1.5 reads count once the learner is at 1.5; the card shows the hold's level while held; (c) deleting or relocating the observation (refused by the ruling). What reverses it: the reviewer. Real problem: a learner-facing overcredit. Uncertainty: where the one place is that reads a run's stored hold for requirement credit (`rungState` and whatever pools runs for `reads`), and whether the stored `material` of SR2's held runs carries the hold in a form `rungState` can read without a new stored field (a new stored field or `DB_VERSION` change is a stop).
