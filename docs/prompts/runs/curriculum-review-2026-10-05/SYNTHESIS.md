## E. Owner decisions (three)

The packet's rule: ask only when two defensible paths change the curriculum promise. Three tracks meet it. My lean is given; the owner decides.

1. **Hymns & gospel.** Path A: narrow the name to "Hymns & spirituals" (the word gospel occurs once in the lesson prose; shout, choir, congregation, testimony occur in none of 109 lesson files). Lean: A, plus one singer-and-key station either way.
2. **Rock & metal.** Path A: keep it a texture mini-module, correct the name or description (it says five textures; the overview teaches four plus the reduction rule), add one self-checked reduction task and a pointer to chords-pop.9 and theory.9. Path B: rock.8 (transcribe and reduce from a recording) and rock.9 (personal arrangement project), with the learner supplying the source; the catalog has no rock-idiom piano score and the ledger rejected the four archive rock files. Lean: A.
3. **Jazz endpoint.** Path A: keep "soloing" in the promise: add minor ii-V-i, a chord-tone then approach-note soloing step (make `improv.6` a prerequisite of `jazz.9` or write it in), and rewrite jazz.9 as one standard taken through learn, memorise, comp, walk, solo, intro and ending, solo-piano pass. Path B: narrow to comping, voicing and accompaniment, strip "soloing" from the jazz.9 title and finder, hand soloing to improv and arranging to chords-pop.9. Lean: A, because the capstone already names it and improv.6 holds the guide-tone work.

Two further choices exist but need no owner today: technique's stated scope versus exam breadth (record recommends stating the scope); classical fugue (amend D1 unless a licensed fugue is sourced).
