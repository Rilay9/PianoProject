## 1. Promised endpoint

Track description, `content/curriculum/00-tracks.json:67-72`: "Mini-module: carols, and how to play for people who are singing." Opens at Stage 2, not default-active.

Last rung's own endpoint, `holiday.7.md:52-54`: "You can play the left hand of one of these alone, in time, at the speed you want the piece, and then play the piece at that speed without it getting heavier as it goes." That is a solo-piano orchestral-reduction goal, not carols and not singers.

What a competent developing pianist in this domain does: plays familiar seasonal tunes from a lead sheet, accompanies a group or a soloist (key and range for the voices, an intro that sets key and tempo, a turnaround or vamp, keeping a room together, simplifying on the fly), dresses a plain setting, plays a written carol arrangement and a seasonal concert piece. The dossier's honest endpoint (TRACK 12): "sight-read familiar seasonal music, accompany singers, transpose, create a fuller arrangement, prepare a performance, play a substantial winter piece."

Reasonable PianoProject endpoint, from the lessons as built: Stage 2 melody plus three chords; Stage 3 accompany a room (written key and a step lower); Stage 4 three finishing devices; Stage 5 a written carol arrangement; Stage 6 a concert setting played once through; Stage 7 a non-carol winter piece with a demanding left hand. This is a use-case ladder, as the dossier says; no Stage 8-9 is warranted and none should be added for symmetry.

Is the track narrower than its name? Yes, in two directions. The name "Holiday" is wider than the description (carols and singing); the lessons are wider than the description in Stages 5-7 (solo performance repertoire, one jazz waltz, two ballet pieces). The description is honest about Stages 2-4 and silent about 5-7. `docs/02-curriculum.md:709-711` also lists Hanukkah (Ma'oz Tzur, Sevivon, Hanerot Halalu) as `[AUTH]` holiday content; none ships (see section 3).
