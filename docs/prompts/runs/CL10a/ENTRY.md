**Eligibility before share.** Both texture detectors count complete printed bars where the upper tune and lower accompaniment actually overlap. Sounding intervals, not just attacks, establish the overlap, including a tune held from the prior bar. Silence, a lower line without a tune and an explicitly marked pickup are excluded. An introduction or coda lacking that two-part overlap is excluded for the same factual reason; a complete two-part introduction, ending or coda remains eligible. No structural label is invented and no failed bar is discarded because it contrasts. Repeated printed bars count once in the share; every performed repetition remains located for evidence.

**Share rule, in musical terms.** At least three quarters of eligible printed bars must carry the accompaniment pattern or the simple-quarter walk. This admits three patterned bars followed by one contrasting bar while retaining the explicit two-of-three refusal; it describes the prevailing accompaniment rather than an occasional figure. It is a declared, unverified musical judgement, constrained by those positive and negative examples, not a threshold selected to empty a table. A wholly patterned short excerpt can qualify; no absolute three-bar minimum is invented. Walking also needs a quarter on each quarter beat in simple metre without turning back to a departed pitch; three quarters in 6/8 are not quarter beats in simple metre.
