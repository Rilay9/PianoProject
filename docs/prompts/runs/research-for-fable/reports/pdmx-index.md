# PDMX public-domain / CC0 piano shortlist: observed characteristics

Research note, 2026-10-03. 245 PDMX scores were shortlisted from metadata and read with music21 10.5.0; 243 parsed. Nothing here assigns a level or says what a piece suits.

## Method

**1. Metadata shortlist (CSV claims only).** Source: `build/ct1/PDMX.csv` (254,077 rows). Every PDMX row carries `license` publicdomain or cc-zero. Filters:
- exclude `license_conflict=True`, `is_draft=True` and `has_paywall=True`;
- `n_tracks` 1–2, with every track's GM program in 0–7 (the piano family);
- `song_length.bars` 4–64;
- skip the MuseScore score ids the app's `catalog.json` already cites.

A row is shortlisted when one of these holds:
- **(a)** the composer/artist field matches a composer from the brief's pedagogical list, and the title text matches that collection's opus number or typical titles;
- **(b)** the title, song name or subtitle names one of the listed traditional tunes. For (b), each tune keeps at most 6 rows, ranked by `subset:deduplicated`, then `is_best_unique_arrangement`, then rating, then lower `complexity`.

Quality fields per file (`is_best_path`, `is_best_unique_arrangement`, `subset:*`, `is_original`, `is_official`, `is_user_pro`, `is_user_publisher`, `n_ratings`, `rating`, `license_url`) are kept in the JSON under `metadata`. Of the 245, 147 are `subset:deduplicated=True`; 98 are not (kept because the unique version was not otherwise available for that work). No file is `is_official`; 6 are `is_original`. Ratings are mostly 0 (unrated). Licence values are as the uploader declared them. PDMX's own `license_conflict` flag is respected, but no licence has been checked independently.

**2. Files.** All files came from one streaming pass over the Zenodo `mxl.tar.gz` (record 14648209) and sit in `pdmx-files/mxl/<a>/<bb>/<hash>.mxl`.

**3. Observed characteristics** (script `pdmx-work/pdmx_analyse.py`).
- **Staves.** Staff 1 is treated as RH and staff 2 as LH (the MuseScore grand-staff order). A single-staff file is "no LH part".
- **Counting.** Voices are flattened per staff. Grace notes are ignored. An "onset" is all notes starting together in one staff. The top line of RH (highest note per onset) and the bottom line of LH give the leaps and repeated-note runs.
- **Span.** Reported as max − min pitch per staff, in semitones and in diatonic steps. "Exceeds five-finger" means a span of more than 7 semitones. This is range only: no fingering is inferred, and a hand may shift position inside a small span or stay put across a larger one.
- **Out-of-key notes.** An "accidental outside the key signature" is a sounding pitch whose alteration differs from the key signature's. It is not a printed accidental. In minor the raised 6th/7th count; a separate field excludes them.
- **Mid-piece rests.** Counted only where no note in that staff is sounding and the rest is not hidden.
- **Bar lengths.** A bar whose length differs from the time signature is flagged unless it is the first or last bar, sits next to another short bar that completes it, or lies at a repeat/final barline.
- **LH texture label (heuristic, first match wins):**
  - Alberti-like: at least 30% of 4-note single-note windows run low-high-mid-high over a triad;
  - block chords: at least 40% of onsets have 3+ notes;
  - block dyads/chords: at least 50% have 2+ notes;
  - broken/arpeggiated: at least 35% of 3-note single-note windows are three distinct notes forming a triad;
  - held: at least 60% of onsets last a half note or longer;
  - stepwise: at least 60% of moves are 1–2 semitones;
  - otherwise mixed.

  The fractions behind each label are in the JSON.
- **Hands together.** For each RH onset: LH also starts, LH is still sounding, or LH is silent. The type is a threshold on those shares.
- **Root position** uses music21 `Chord.inversion()`.
- **Key estimate** is music21's Krumhansl–Schmuckler `analyze('key')`. It is a computed estimate, not a fact about the piece.

**4. Red flags** (automatic):
- default title or composer text;
- a staff with no notes;
- staves 1 and 2 identical;
- more than 2 staves;
- the LH staff in treble clef throughout;
- the LH sitting higher than the RH on average;
- a staff outside A0–C8, or spanning more than 3 octaves;
- inner bars that do not fill the time signature;
- chord-symbol spans in which under half the written note-duration is chord tones (when more than 30% of spans do so).

Files whose notes are byte-for-byte the same as another shortlisted file (same onsets and pitches) are cross-referenced as `identical_notes_to`. There are 25 such groups, covering 55 files.

**5. Edition / tune check.**
- **(a) pieces.** Openings were compared against **Mutopia Project MIDI files**. Mutopia is a volunteer edition, not an urtext, but it is an independent typesetting. The 23 works fetched were: BWV Anh. 113–121, 126–128 and 131; Schumann Op. 68 Nos. 1, 2, 4, 8 and 10; Mozart K. 2; Kuhlau Op. 20/1 i; Tchaikovsky Op. 39/16; Burgmüller Op. 100/2. For each file, the first 16 RH top-line pitches (and LH bottom-line pitches) are aligned to every reference at the same pitch or transposed, by longest common subsequence. Unrelated pieces score about 7–12/16 by chance. "Matches" therefore means at least 14/16 at the same pitch. Only pitch order is compared: rhythm, octave placement of the LH, ornaments and anything after the opening are **not** verified. Where no reference exists (Hook, Leopold Mozart, Türk, Gurlitt, Duvernoy, Lemoine, Spindler, Haydn, C.P.E. Bach notebook pieces, Mozart K. 1a–d/3, and others), the entry says UNVERIFIED.
- **(b) tunes.** The RH top line was searched (first 64 notes) for an opening interval pattern that I wrote down for each tune. This is a heuristic consistency check, **not** a comparison with an authoritative edition. Every "no match" was then read by eye and given a manual verdict: tune present with a variant, a different tune, or unclear. Tunes with no pattern written down are marked "no reference encoded".

**Limits.**
- Nothing was heard or rendered.
- Fingering, phrasing, dynamics, articulation and pedalling were not examined.
- Repeats and voltas are not expanded.
- Wrong notes after the opening 16 are not detected for any file.
- Composer and title fields are uploader claims throughout.
- The shortlist missed whatever the regexes did not catch. In particular, nothing usable for Beyer, Czerny (the listed opp.), Köhler, Duvernoy beyond one item, Diabelli, Clementi Op. 36, Biehl, Bertini, Concone, Streabbog or Heller survived the filters. Their PDMX rows were either absent, over 64 bars, multi-track, conflict-flagged, or already in the catalog. This was not separately audited.
- Non-Latin titles are mis-encoded in the CSV and were ignored.

**Observed vs claimed.** Every musical column below is observed, computed from the notes. Title, composer, opus and licence are metadata claims. Where an uploader names an edition or source collection (e.g. "Roose.0742", "WES.046", "JJo3.061" codes from tune-book transcriptions, or "Cecil Sharp"), the claim is quoted in the CSV title field in the JSON.

**Files.**
- `pdmx-index.json` holds one object per file: the full hash id, `pdmx_mxl` path, `musescore_score_id`, `local_file`, all observations, `red_flags`, `edition_check` and `shortlist_doubt`.
- The scripts are in `pdmx-work/`.
