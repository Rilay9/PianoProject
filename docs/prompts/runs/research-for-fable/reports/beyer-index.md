# Beyer, Vorschule im Klavierspiel, Op. 101: characteristic index

- **Source:** scan `scratchpad/beyer.pdf` (88 PDF pages), Internet Archive item `imslp-im-klavierspiel-op101-beyer-ferdinand`.
- **Edition:** Edition Peters Nr. 2721, plate 8033. The imprint on p. 86 reads "Lizenz-Nr. 415-330/217/66, Druck: VEB Messe- und Musikaliendruck Leipzig III/18/157", so this is a later East German printing of the Peters plates. The work itself is public domain. The licence of this particular scan was **not checked**.
- **Page mapping:** PDF page numbers equal the printed page numbers (checked on pp. 5–86).
- **Pages read for this index:** 5–86. Pages 9–11, 24–27, 29–32 and 38–39 were re-read, so every numbered piece has a row.
  - Not read: pp. 1–4 (cover and contents; the lead read them).
  - Not indexed: pp. 87–88 (Peters publisher advertisements).
- **What the book contains:** 109 Peters numbers, 1–109.
  - Nos. 1–2 are three-hand pieces: the teacher plays, and the pupil plays **one hand only**.
  - Duets (pupil plays the Primo with both hands): 3–11, 32–34, 41–44, 63–64, 86–87.
  - All other numbers are two-hand solo pieces.
  - Material with no Peters number is listed separately in §1: exercises, scales, the chromatic scale, the Appendix (its own numbers 1–34) and the 24 scales.
- **Skipped:** every Seconda (teacher) part. For duets, only the pupil's Primo is described.

**Conventions**
- **Labels:**
  - Unlabelled statements are *Observed* in the printed notation.
  - **[Pub]** marks the book's own printed headings and explanations.
  - No teaching judgement is added.
- **Pitch names:** c4 = middle C.
- **Clefs:** up to No. 53, the left hand is printed in the treble clef in every pupil part.
- **Abbreviations:**
  - q = quarter, h = half, w = whole, e = eighth, s = sixteenth, dh = dotted half, dq = dotted quarter.
  - rpt = repeat sign.
  - "5 3 1" etc. = printed fingering.
- **Bar counts:** "~" marks a count taken at a glance, not counted note by note.
- **Confidence:**
  - H: clearly legible and checked.
  - M: legible, but a detail is inferred from fingering or note shape.
  - L: partly illegible or not examined closely.
- **Chord names:** given only where the notes spell the chord outright. Otherwise the pitches are listed.
