HOW TO READ THE TRACE
---------------------
aheadFor runs in every pricing pass (priceWindowShape): once for the chosen shape and once for each candidate count
(shown 3, 2, 1), so every pass is four consecutive entries: [chosen, candidate 3, candidate 2, candidate 1]. That
structure was verified for every load (the entry count is a multiple of four and entries 2 to 4 have shown 3, 2, 1).
Only the chosen entry is tabulated below. Passes come from both chooseWindowShape (applied) and sheetsNeeded (not
applied); the log does not say which, and does not carry a timestamp or the ladder count.
Terms, as the patch names them:
  rowHeight       the window row's price: the piece's tallest system at the scale in use, plus the margin (the reserve)
  aheadHeight     the look-ahead row's price: min(rowHeight, drawnRowPx) once the fit at this zoom has drawn
  admissionPx     systems x rowHeight + aheadHeight + SLOT_GAP_PX x systems
  slotsHeight     the room the chooser measured
  drawnHere       the fit at this zoom has drawn (drawnAtZoom equals the zoom)
  drawnNow        the scale being drawn when this shape is the one on the glass, else 0
  drawnRowPx      the tallest row the last fit drew, with its margin
  currentSlots    the slot count on the glass when the pass ran
Derived by this check, and labelled as such: "over-by" is admissionPx minus slotsHeight; "gap/system" is the margin of
the sum that is not rowHeight or aheadHeight (24 in every entry, SLOT_GAP_PX); "window rows priced at the drawn height"
replaces rowHeight by the smallest drawnRowPx logged at that zoom with currentSlots equal to systems (only window rows
drawn), keeping aheadHeight and the gaps as logged.
