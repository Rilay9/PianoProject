[2.1] stage 2 — Hands together: the left hand holds
  level: very easy, beginner, first pieces hands together, C position
  must: C major; left hand whole notes or half notes; right hand melody; hands together; 8 to 16 bars
  avoid: chords in the right hand; moving left hand; 6/8; anything over 32 bars
  before: Ode to Joy (hands together) (Beethoven) and Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star
  after:  Ode to Joy (hands together) (Beethoven), Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star and Minuet in G major, BWV Anh. 114 (Christian Petzold)
[holiday] stage 2 — Carols you can play this year
  level: easy, carols, melody first, chords C F and G
  must: carol or Christmas tune; a melody that fits one hand position or close to it; chord symbols mostly I, IV and V; 8 to 24 bars
  avoid: orchestral arrangements; four-stave choral scores; keys with four or more sharps
  before: Jingle Bells (chorus) and Silent Night
  after:  Jingle Bells (chorus), Silent Night and Minuet in G major, BWV Anh. 114 (Christian Petzold)
