# Convergence disposition: who is allowed to know what

This is the finite authority disposition required by `docs/prompts/charter.md` after CQ1. It is **not** a new audit, implementation plan, or queue. It classifies the major authority mechanisms already known from the existing audits, CT1/content-truth work, the reuse census, CQ1, and accepted project decisions. No exhaustive rediscovery was performed.

The purpose is to make future work smaller. Every row therefore says not only who owns the truth, but what work the decision lets the project delete or avoid.

Disposition vocabulary follows the charter:

- **KEEP** — the current mechanism is already the simplest trustworthy owner for this responsibility.
- **DELEGATE** — use an established library/data source after semantic-fit and parity checks; the library does not get pedagogical authority merely because it exists.
- **CURATE** — make the teaching/content decision an explicit sourced record. Published syllabi and methods are evidence inputs, not an automatic universal curriculum.
- **VERIFY NARROWLY** — a generated-content assertion may stand only as sourced definition → generator contract → independent structural checker.
- **ADVISORY ONLY** — an inference may search, rank or flag candidates, but it grants no teaching, suitability, progression or credit authority.
- **UNKNOWN** — the app deliberately does not claim to know.

## Work this disposition deletes or freezes

These are not deferred subprojects waiting for a later agent. They are work the project should **not** do unless a future concrete learner-facing requirement reopens the responsibility under the charter.

1. **No CQ2+ universal named-figure detector series.** A named figure is verified only for a generated contract that needs it, curated on a real passage, or left unknown/advisory on arbitrary music.
2. **No universal accompaniment detector project.** `leftHandPattern` and related fuzzy texture inference are not promoted into teaching or credit authority.
3. **No detector-by-detector audit/perfection campaign.** Existing known misreads matter only when a live learner-facing decision still depends on that authority.
4. **No replace-everything-with-music21/Tonal/partitura migration.** Libraries are adopted only when exact semantic-fit/parity lets PianoProject stop owning a standard fact.
5. **No mass rewrite of the generator families.** Keep the working generator architecture; narrow or repair a family only when its concrete contract is wrong.
6. **No ABRSM/RCM/Faber averaging or automatic crosswalk.** Keep each source distinct and make explicit curriculum decisions where needed.
7. **No lesson-audit checkbox batch.** The existing THEORY/HISTORY/UNVERIFIED/JUDGEMENT findings are evidence. Objective learner-facing errors may later be fixed from sources; judgement stays curated; unneeded assertions can be weakened or removed.
8. **No requirement to semantically classify every arbitrary import.** Unknown is a valid state; imports remain playable and practicable.
9. **No automatic musical-quality or style-mastery credit from detectors or metadata.** Those claims are human/curated or unknown unless directly observed.
10. **No new architecture merely to preserve old audit machinery.** Claims/reports/tests may remain as diagnostics while product authority moves to the owners above; their existence does not create a migration project.

## What remains after this pass

This document authorizes **no implementation**. It is the review boundary before the project resumes product work.

After this disposition is reviewed, active work should be chosen from learner-facing product completion, not from old audit order. Keep at most ten active blockers. A candidate blocker must identify a real effect on what the learner is taught, offered, credited, told, or able to do; otherwise it does not displace one of the ten.

The preferred product convergence path remains the charter's target: Today/session chooses the next useful experience from trusted inputs; controlled generated drills isolate a need; real excerpts/repertoire provide transfer; Simon/Lab/Jam/Free Play/Score serve distinct practice intents; evidence records only what the activity actually observes.

If a future change would add more authoritative inference, more unresolved follow-ups, or another project-wide audit, stop and ask first: **should PianoProject own this responsibility at all?**
