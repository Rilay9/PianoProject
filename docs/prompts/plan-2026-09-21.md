## Goal 1 — Every style the app teaches reaches the learner as early as it honestly fits, and keeps going up the ladder

**True today.** Blues, classical, chords-pop, improv and theory run stages 3–9; rock 3–7
(its plan stops there); jazz 4–9; ragtime 5–8; holiday 2–4; hymns 2–3; latin 3 and 5;
jam 4–6. Early exposure on core rungs: riffs at 1.1/1.5, a swing pair at 2.2, pentatonic
at 2.5, a modal vamp at 3.3, tresillo at 3.6, blues and carols as options on 2.x–3.x.

**Gap.** The genre plans describe rungs at holiday 5–7, hymns 4–6, latin 4 and 6–8, jazz 3,
ragtime 4 and 9, jam 7 that do not exist. Most need music from the archive; 41 quarried
candidates await a decision and 136 more marked "in archive" were never extracted.

**Closes it.** T11 (quarry latin and hymns; a reader marks keep/reject from notation) →
T14 (build those rungs from the keeps, one item per call with its evidence line, lessons
written against the scores, claim rows added as each is written).

## Goal 2 — The modes the app has (lab, Simon, duet, free play, rhythm-only, ladder, chart, trading fours) are used wherever a rung is better for them, and each does what its lesson says

**True today.** Every rung carries `tools`; presets exist for the lab; Simon seeds from the
blues scale; the chord chart has doors (T10); the placement sets the plan (T10).

**Gap.** Trading fours does not exist (T2, running). The tempo ladder has no route (T6).
The lab explains itself nowhere in the app, and it runs one way only: *Jam it* plays a
bed under the learner, *Read it* opens the piece as a score. The owner wants the lab to
hold the chords while he plays the tune, and to play the tune while he comps. The
two-lab-button shape from T10 is a workaround for a schema rule and should become an
`unlock` field on the tool entry. And the owner does not yet trust any of these modes.

**Closes it.** T2 → T6 → **T18 (new): the lab both ways and documented** — a *Hold the
chords* mode (the bed voices the progression, the learner plays the melody, judged only if
it can be honest) and a *Play the tune* mode (the bed plays the generated melody, the
learner comps), plus a one-line explanation under every control and preset in the screen
itself and a page in `docs/04` §3c that matches → the `unlock` change (in T16) → **T17:
every mode driven in the browser, one spec each, judged for playability** (read-ahead,
swap timing, cues), which is where trust is earned or not.

## Goal 3 — Everything the app tells the learner is true: lessons, concepts, exercises, songs

**True today.** 98 lessons audited, 323 findings, 236 corrected, a sample of 72 second-read
(0 wrong, 4 overreached). Six of seven known bad score files confirmed by T9's checks and
the seventh shown not to be a fault. T10's restored sentences pinned by

**Gap.** 164 corrections not second-read and none of the 236 under test. 86 findings open

## Goal 4 — The MIDI converter turns a real recording from the keyboard into a score the app can use

with timing jitter. Its test harness was not committed.

**Gap.** Never run on a real recording. A one-track two-hand recording lands on one
staff. No hand split, no quantisation policy stated.

**Closes it.** T16: a hand split (pitch and voice-leading based, stated and tested), a
quantisation policy with its grid stated, the harness committed, and a run on a real
recording. **Three real recordings are on disk** (2026-09-21, `build/midi-real/`, from the
