# The plan forward, by goal (2026-09-21)

The owner's goals, restated without his words, each with what is measured true today and
the task that closes the gap. Tasks are in `docs/prompts/tasks/`. The order across goals is
at the end. **Nothing here has been heard; every "correct" below means the notation, the
curriculum data or the code says so.**

Rules that govern all of it (owner, 2026-09-21): he does nothing manually; readers and
build checks decide, with evidence written down. Promised features are built, not written
out. Briefs written by the previous session are read and judged before they are run, and
replaced where a better shape is obvious. Two agents at most, one per task, budget checked
before each; one report per finished task; nothing committed until he says.

---

## Goal 1 — Every style the app teaches reaches the learner as early as it honestly fits, and keeps going up the ladder

**True today.** Blues, classical, chords-pop, improv and theory run stages 3–9; rock 3–7
(its plan stops there); jazz 4–9; ragtime 5–8; holiday 2–4; hymns 2–3; latin 3 and 5;
jam 4–6. Early exposure on core rungs: riffs at 1.1/1.5, a swing pair at 2.2, pentatonic
at 2.5, a modal vamp at 3.3, tresillo at 3.6, blues and carols as options on 2.x–3.x.

**Gap.** The genre plans describe rungs at holiday 5–7, hymns 4–6, latin 4 and 6–8, jazz 3,
ragtime 4 and 9, jam 7 that do not exist. Most need music from the archive; 41 quarried
candidates await a decision and 136 more marked "in archive" were never extracted.

**Closes it.** T11 (quarry latin and hymns; a reader marks keep/reject from notation) →
T14 (build those rungs from the keeps, one item per call with its evidence line, lessons
written against the scores, claim rows added as each is written).

## Goal 2 — The modes the app has (lab, Simon, duet, free play, rhythm-only, ladder, chart, trading fours) are used wherever a rung is better for them, and each does what its lesson says

**True today.** Every rung carries `tools`; presets exist for the lab; Simon seeds from the
blues scale; the chord chart has doors (T10); the placement sets the plan (T10).

**Gap.** Trading fours does not exist (T2, running). The tempo ladder has no route (T6).
The lab explains itself nowhere in the app, and it runs one way only: *Jam it* plays a
bed under the learner, *Read it* opens the piece as a score. The owner wants the lab to
hold the chords while he plays the tune, and to play the tune while he comps. The
two-lab-button shape from T10 is a workaround for a schema rule and should become an
`unlock` field on the tool entry. And the owner does not yet trust any of these modes.

**Closes it.** T2 → T6 → **T18 (new): the lab both ways and documented** — a *Hold the
chords* mode (the bed voices the progression, the learner plays the melody, judged only if
it can be honest) and a *Play the tune* mode (the bed plays the generated melody, the
learner comps), plus a one-line explanation under every control and preset in the screen
itself and a page in `docs/04` §3c that matches → the `unlock` change (in T16) → **T17:
every mode driven in the browser, one spec each, judged for playability** (read-ahead,
swap timing, cues), which is where trust is earned or not.

## Goal 3 — Everything the app tells the learner is true: lessons, concepts, exercises, songs

**True today.** 98 lessons audited, 323 findings, 236 corrected, a sample of 72 second-read
(0 wrong, 4 overreached). Six of seven known bad score files confirmed by T9's checks and
the seventh shown not to be a fault. T10's restored sentences pinned by
`lessonClaimsAboutApp.test.ts`.

**Gap.** 164 corrections not second-read and none of the 236 under test. 86 findings open
(theory, history, judgement). 247 score-check rows unapplied (duplicate ids, wrong titles,
truncations, repeat faults). 1,183 generated exercises never checked as music beyond five
families. 110 of the 292 rung songs named in no lesson and never opened. The audit's
instrument (`dump_score.py`) hides tuplets, ties and grace notes, which produced one false
finding already.

**The owner's own test of this goal (2026-09-21):** he must never be told a G♯ is an F, never
meet a concept the app invented, and never be told an exercise or song teaches something it
does not. So three checks are non-negotiable in the check phase: every note name the app
prints (drill cards, Simon, answer sheets, the lab's chord names, lesson prose) comes from
one spelling function that is under test against the score's own spelling; every concept a
lesson names exists in `concepts.json` **and** the lesson's prose teaches it (the build
already refuses unknown ids; the taught half is T12's row per concept); and every sentence
of the form "this piece/exercise teaches X" is a claim row that reads the notation.

**Closes it, after the build phase so it is not done twice.** T15 (apply the 247 rows) →
T13 (an invariant test and a full-page picture per generator family) → T12 (every
correction second-read and made a test row; the 86 decided by a reader, UNSURE ones by a
second) → `dump_score.py` taught tuplets, ties and graces (in T13) → score checks wired
into the build as a gate → a last audit pass over the lessons of the rungs T14 built.

## Goal 4 — The MIDI converter turns a real recording from the keyboard into a score the app can use

**True today.** Rebuilt on 2026-09-19: chords from overlapping notes, spelling by key, a
self-check, refusal to overwrite. Tested only on MIDI rendered from eight catalog scores
with timing jitter. Its test harness was not committed.

**Gap.** Never run on a real recording. A one-track two-hand recording lands on one
staff. No hand split, no quantisation policy stated.

**Closes it.** T16: a hand split (pitch and voice-leading based, stated and tested), a
quantisation policy with its grid stated, the harness committed, and a run on a real
recording. **Three real recordings are on disk** (2026-09-21, `build/midi-real/`, from the
MAESTRO dataset, see its `SOURCE.md`): Scarlatti K. 525, a Bach BWV 885 excerpt, Grieg Op. 38
No. 7 — each format 1, one tempo track plus one note track carrying both hands, which is
exactly the case the converter fails today. A recording from the owner's own keyboard would
add a second instrument's quirks and is welcome but not required.

## Goal 5 — The record stays honest and the budget lasts

Stopping points: one report per finished task, with what is unverified stated first.
Budget: usage checked before each dispatch; heavy tasks start after a window reset via a
scheduled wake; no agent is resumed from a long transcript after a crash — it restarts
from its brief with a two-line note of what is on disk. Every task's entry in
`docs/pending-review.md` names the test that was red and the line that made it so.

Measured 2026-09-21/22: one 5-hour window carried roughly two medium agents (T10 at 535k
agent tokens, T2 at 279k, a 72-item second read at 359k) before reaching three quarters;
the weekly window stood at 10 % after that day. So: **size every task to at most half a
window** — T14 by track (holiday; hymns; latin; jazz and ragtime), T12 by batch file, T15
by check, T17 by mode — so a crash loses a short transcript and the handoff rule recovers
the rest. **Each wake schedules the next wake at the following reset before it dispatches**,
so no window sits idle. On a crash: one message asking the dead agent for a 40-line handoff
in its scratch folder, then a fresh agent continues from the brief plus that handoff.
