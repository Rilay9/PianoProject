# The master audit matrix: every substantive issue, mapped to evidence, status, decision, wave and verification

Rebuilt 2026-09-25 after the owner asked why the first backlog held so little of the
reviewer's conversation: the share page had rendered its last five turns and the capture
took them for the whole; the share's data holds 206 nodes and ten long audits never read
here. They are in `audit-2026-09-25-outside-full.md` (cited below as N-38, N-76, N-93,
N-96, N-99, N-117, N-170, N-180, N-184, N-192, N-205). The reviewer's own correction
(relayed by the owner the same day) set this file's shape and its rule:

> **This matrix is the control document.** It is what the project is judged against. Wave
> A diagnosed a subset of the architecture under this list; it did not address the list. No
> row is resolved because an architectural problem under it was diagnosed. Anything not
> yet audited gets a concrete audit task, never a silent drop. The goal is the original one:
> a genuinely good piano-learning application. **A technically correct feature is not
> necessarily a good teaching feature.**

**Columns.** *Evidence*: where the claim comes from (a trace with lines; the reviewer's
reading of the repo, N-n; the owner's words, O; the record, P21; a design judgement, DJ).
*Severity*: P0 wrong, P1 architectural limit, P2 content or design improvement, P3 polish,
or "—" for a proposal. *Status*: **resolved** (test, picture or reader closed it);
**diagnosed, pending** (mechanism known with evidence, implementation to come);
**investigate** (symptom on record, mechanism not established); **rejected** (decided
against, with the reason); **not audited** (nobody has looked; the *Decision* names the
audit task). *Wave*: B is running; the letters after it are `plan-2026-09-25.md`'s waves
after B (C0 vocabulary and schema design, C evidence architecture, D generators and
sight-reading, E repertoire corpus, F lessons, G identity and style, X the experience
beyond the score, H the final audit; T tooling inside its consumer wave). *Verified*:
pending until a test seen red then green, a picture opened and read, or a reader's line.

**A decision on a proposal row is a hypothesis, not a specification.** Where a row's
decision names a particular shape (a preview proportion, a phrase form, a ratio, a ladder,
a reader count), that shape is the first thing to try and the thing to measure against;
the wave that builds it may replace it when the learner-facing result says so, and says
why in the row. The rows marked *hypothesis* below are the ones the reviewer named on
2026-09-25; the rule applies to every proposal row.

**The audit tasks** named in *Decision* for "not audited" rows, so each has an owner wave:
AT-1 the lesson-content audit over every lesson and tip file against the teaching questions
(F); AT-2 every user-visible string categorised and flagged (F); AT-3 the fake first 30
days for three learners, traced day by day through the real selection code (C, repeated in
H); AT-4 the generator fitness sweep, thousands of seeds per family, structural, musical,
pedagogical, statistical (D); AT-5 the reader and converter information-loss audit over the
MusicXML and MIDI paths with a pathological-input fixture set (E); AT-6 the PDMX inventory
and excerpt-candidate analysis (E); AT-7 the navigation walk as a new learner, Today →
lesson → score → summary → next → plan (X, H); AT-8 the ear-training, theory and free-play
transfer audit (D, X); AT-9 the modes, jam and lab audit as musicianship, per genre (G);
AT-10 the phone score UX by density, both orientations, on the sheet cells (H); AT-11 the
timing and grading audit with real MIDI recordings as fixtures (C); AT-12 the curriculum
sequencing and prerequisite audit, lesson by lesson (F); AT-13 the repertoire-assignment
audit: does each rung's music reinforce its concept, and where should music simply be
music (E, F); AT-14 the state-machine inventory beyond the score screen (T, H); AT-15 the
test-suite inventory: every unit, e2e, state, tour, content and converter test classified
preserve / revise / delete / replace / add, starting from `docs/08-test-map.md` (C0, before
any wave rewrites what the suite encodes; repeated as a cleanup and rebuild pass in H).
