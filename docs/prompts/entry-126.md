## The mechanism

**The fault.** `ladderState` credited transfer on `firstContact && !shownOn.has(itemId)` and spared a failure on the same condition (`countsTowardsMovingDown`): a new seed of one generator is a different id and was never checked, a neighbouring excerpt was new, and nothing said on which dimensions a skill had generalised. The facts to do better existed (D4's relationship on transfer-offer runs only, G1a's `firstContact` on the header) but no attempt carried them, `EvidenceContext.firstContact` was written from `unseen`, and `transfer.ts` found the establishing records by calling `ladderState` once per history prefix plus once per probe.

**The change, on the mechanism** (the reviewer's fact path, item by item):

1. **Each attempt carries its own facts.** `EvidenceContext` gains `relationship?` and `demands?`, and `firstContact` becomes optional and is copied from the run header's `firstContact` — never `unseen`, absent where the header's is (`evidence.ts`). `recordRun` writes both new facts on every measured record of a run with a material (`progressStore.withAttemptFacts`): the relationship by `relationshipOf` against `shownOnRecords` over the rows stored before the run, the demands as every demand the run's records located; on a transfer-offer run the offer's relationship for its skill, as written. The candidate `relationshipOf` measures is the item **as played** (`playedCandidate`: its identity the run's material, its key the phrase recipe's where the material is a generator that fixes one, its hands the hands played, its demands the run's) — a reading row's catalogue entry has no key, demands or identity, so the bare row would have left every phrase's key, texture and rhythm unknown. Absent stays absent: no material, no catalogue, or G68's intent-only shape write no relationship. `recomputeEvidence` (the evidence job) carries both facts forward skill by skill and computes none.
2. **The establishing contexts come from the replay.** `ladderState` keeps `establishing` (each supporting full-standard record before proficiency, since proficiency was last lost) and exposes it with `established` (D4's `MaterialReference`s). `transfer.ts`'s `establishing` reads them from **one** ladder call; the ladder calls nothing in `transfer.ts` (spied: one call). D4's probes could not see a reset below proficiency and could include a record from before one; the replay sees the reset.
3. **One path, two thresholds.** `transferReading(skill, established, run)` → `{ verdict, on, why, differs, newDemands }`: contact (absent → unknown; `false` → not-transfer), relationship (absent → unknown; every establishing reference unknown → unknown; a new seed of established material → not-transfer), the claim (no `transfer` block → unknown; a skill dimension measured `true` and supported at the full standard → demonstrated, `on` the intersection; none true and one unknown → unknown; all false → not-transfer). `differsOn` is never read; `declared` only writes a disagreement into `why`. Promotion takes `demonstrated`; protection (`sparesFailure`) spares a failed full-standard attempt where first contact is `true` and a skill dimension measurably differs or the attempt carries a demand no establishing record carried (only where every side's demands are known).
4. **The scope beneath the summary.** `transferScope: { on, since }[]`, one entry per distinct set of dimensions, cleared with `transfer` on demotion; read by no screen.
5. **The skills' dimensions as data** (`skills.json`, spliced as text; the schema's `transfer` property; `validate.py` reads `DIMENSIONS` out of `transfer.ts` and refuses any other, and its report prints the skills without a block).
6. **The offer's contact** (`session.ts`): `BuildInput.contact` (encounters and pruned runs' summaries) and `contactOf(input, itemId, material)` — `contactIn` over the input's rows and that field — the session's one contact reader; `transferOffer` reads it. Today loads the field (`TodayScreen.ts`, outside the brief's files: without it the offer on the phone still read runs alone).

**The discriminating tests.** Each new or revised case was red first: the policy's 17 against the stub; the fact path's 11 of 14 on the code before the change (`red-fact-path.txt`); the offer's three (`red-offer-contact.txt`); the validator's five (`red-skill-transfer-py.txt`); and on HEAD's sources (`red-vitest-head-source.txt`, the changed sources swapped for HEAD's bytes and restored by sha256) 23 of 100 across the ladder, D4's case, the fact path and the offer; and in a browser, both new cases against HEAD's app (`red-e2e-g2-cases-head-app.txt`).
