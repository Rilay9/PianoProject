## The builder's calls, each with its reason

- **The per-demand useful-density minimums** (`content/sources/opportunity-density.json`): a located count *and* a count per bar for each of the nineteen demands, each with its reason and marked a hypothesis (the two every-bar detectors, left-hand pattern and walking bass, are not: presence already means every bar). Both, because a long piece passes a count by accident and a short one cannot reach one. One file read by the build (which writes `measurement.established`) and by the app (the import path); a Vitest case holds the two readings equal on every built item. A generated item may also establish a demand by its family contract where the contract states a density (a count per bar, or a count of two or more) — the family's own considered threshold, and a four-bar drill cannot reach a whole-piece count — and a presence-only rule establishes nothing, which is how the tie drill's one tie in four bars is not adequate practice (D0 finding 6).
- **`demands: "unmeasured"`** is the string itself, not an empty list beside a flag: `CatalogItem.demands` is `string[] | 'unmeasured'`, so every reader has to handle it or fail to compile, and an empty list can only mean measured-and-empty. The reason sits in `measurement.reason`. A runtime drill has no `demands` and `measurement.status: "runtime"`: its notes are made per phrase, and it is not a notated item.
- **The provenance record's shape**: `source`, `edition`, `composition`, `arrangement`, `converter` or `generator`, `facts` (each `{kind, via}` with kind one of measured, inferred, authored, reviewed, unmeasured, runtime), `review` (`score`, `teaching`, `null` = no person has decided), `quarryKeep` (PDMX only, neither bit), `physical` (the declared large-hand voicing). A learner's correction of the hands is `authored` with `via` naming the learner. A generated or runtime item's identity is its generator (family, version, seed, or the drill kind), and it names no composition: an exercise is not a work, and the inventory counts none.
- **What a rung claims**, for the report: its `skill` requirements; its concepts that are vocabulary skill ids; its concepts that name a notated fact a detector finds (a small table in `claims.py`: `alberti`, `waltz-bass`, `oom-pah-bass`… → the left-hand pattern; `walking-bass`; `skips`; `eighth-notes`; `chromatic`…); and the demands `taughtAt` names it for. Everything else it names is a claim the notes cannot establish, listed per rung with the review bit.
- **The demand tier's want is the demand the row's rung teaches** (`taughtAt` of the rung it was offered from, or the first rung listing it), not every opportunity of the row's target skills: with every piece measured, "also practises steps" would make the tier the catalogue by level, which is Part 23's trap by another road.
- **A rung's skill requirement is served only by what the evidence readers act on** (`want.for: 'requirement'`), because only those runs can meet it; selection's skill tier and skill step may offer a generated item whose notes establish the skill's opportunity, as practice, never as what the requirement counts.
- **The repertoire claim has no judging rung**, so the gate reads the evidence alone there: "your reads support them" stays true, and the old rule is unchanged but for the density.
- **The readiness floor stays `familiar`** (the brief's instruction unless a learner is locked out of everything; see the reruns).
- **D0's suite keeps its own run of the plan**, through the same bridge (one definition, two runs): it tests the generator in process, before any build and in its red trees, which the build's attach step cannot.
- **The reports** go to `docs/prompts/rung-claims.md` and `docs/prompts/inventory.md` from the default build only; a `--out` or `--quick` build writes the JSON under `build/` and leaves the documents alone.
- **The Today swap sheet reads the learner's skills as well as their rung.** `swapOptions` takes the stored runs (`rows`) and reads the skills from them as the session does (`skillEvidenceOf`), and `TodayScreen.ts` hands it the runs the card was built from — three lines in a screen file outside the brief's list, because without them the swap sheet would ask only half of the gate's decided first question (the lessons, not the evidence), and a learner whose reads show subdivision familiar would still be refused every sixteenth-note piece. No pixel of the screen changes.
