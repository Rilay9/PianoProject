## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

C6 composes a coherent lesson: a warm-up that isolates the rhythm, a real excerpt with the same demand, a piece to keep, an easy finish, each with a reason. The learner meets it as five independent app launches: *Start session* opens the first, Done returns them to Today, and they must inspect five rows, remember which is done, infer the next and work out why it follows. A teacher does not hand over five activities and disappear between them. X1 is the execution layer between C6's composition and the screens: where the learner is in today's lesson, what comes next and why, what changed because of what just happened, and how to pick it up after an interruption — with nothing in it ever counted as competence.

## What is decided

1. **The execution state.** `app/src/data/sessionRun.ts` owns one small durable record per day in the settings row (the offer snapshot's pattern; no store, no database version): `{ day, sessionId, version, activities: [{ index, slot (the composed slot's kind, item, claim and words), reason, contact?: { assumed: 'first-contact' | 'met' | 'none', rechecked?: 'held' | 'invalidated' }, state: 'pending' | 'active' | 'attempted' | 'completed' | 'skipped', adaptation?: { kind: 'skipped-redundant' | 'kept-here' | 'repurposed', why } }], current, startedAt, elapsedMs, endedOnPurpose?, detour?: null }`. Written when *Start session* is pressed from the card as composed at that moment (the identity and version from the composition, so a recomposed card is a new session, never a silent rebuild); read by Today and by every activity screen's end. `detour` is reserved for X19 and stays null. Session completion, skipping and adaptation are never evidence: no reader in `evidence/`, `rungState` or `progressStore` reads this record (a test holds it).
2. **The transition.** When an activity ends (the Score screen's run summary; a drill's or the lab's end sheet; the reader's finish), the sheet's closing action becomes the next step: "Next: *Minuet excerpt*, 4 min — the same eighth-note pattern, now in real music. [Start] [Skip or change]", the reason being the composition's own words for that slot (C6's claim and the slot kind's sentence in `04` §2), never new prose. *Start* opens the next activity through the router with the session's cursor advanced; *Skip or change* marks the activity skipped with the learner's word and returns to Today's card, where the next is highlighted. After the last activity: a finish line on Today ("Today's session done · 28 min · the rhythm held, the excerpt read, the piece kept") built from the activities' states and the run summaries' existing words — no judgement, no competence claim.
3. **Adaptation, visible.** Easy success (an activity passed at full standard on the first attempt where the composition marked the next slot as the same demand's controlled practice) marks that next slot `skipped-redundant` with "easier than expected — straight to the piece"; a failed activity keeps the learner where they are: the transition offers the same activity again with "still unstable, so we're not moving on" and *Move on anyway*; nothing else in the card changes after a run (no invisible rebuild; the reviewer's line). Fatigue, long-session trimming and diagnostic reshaping are X's later rows, not X1's.
4. **Resume.** Today shows "Continue today's session · 18 of 30 min · next: Minuet excerpt" above the card while a run is active for today, and *Continue* opens the current activity; after a reload, another tab, or the app closed mid-activity the state is the record's (U31's score-run persistence stays its own and distinct); a run from yesterday is closed as "not finished" without judgement when today's card is composed.
5. **L113, the gate on the rung's own list.** The composition's automatic paths — the rung's ask, a rung's listed option, an assigned import or PDF the rung lists — pass the canonical gate with its unknown-forbidden rule (`eligibility.ts`), so an unmeasured item never reaches the card as the rung's ask; explicit exploration (the Library's row, *Open anyway*) stays reachable with the unknowns named in words. A test with an unmeasured item on a rung's list and the same item opened from the Library.
6. **Contact recorded and rechecked.** Each activity's `contact.assumed` is written at composition from G2's one adapter (`session.ts`'s contact input; a sight-read is `first-contact`, an offered transfer piece `none` met, a repertoire piece `met`); at the activity's start the assumption is rechecked through the same adapter (G1's history: a phrase heard at noon from the Library invalidates a session composed at breakfast); an invalidated activity is repurposed with the reason said on the transition ("you heard this one at noon, so it is practice now, not a first read") and the run marks `repurposed`. No second novelty reader (the reviewer's seam); the reader row's evidence (`unseen`) follows the recheck as C4 wrote it.
7. **The small rows in Today's words:** U71 (the offer's reason cut at 342 px: the skill first, the invitation on the sheet), U73 (a failed snapshot write: the route not opened until the write settles, the error said on Today), U57 (the reader's "kept" line, X's voice), G61 and G62 (the jam slot and *Quick check* take a rung's options in list order: prefer chord-and-feel material and a judging drill by the slot's own condition, or say what the button really opens).
8. **Not X1's:** X19 (the practice episode: a detour and return; `detour` stays null); X15 and X16 (cues, auto-continuation); the composition's ranking and content (C6, E2); the transfer policy (G2, consumed); the lifecycle (G1b); the performance experience (X2); Plan as a map (X2's row); the daily read's phrase rule (C4).
