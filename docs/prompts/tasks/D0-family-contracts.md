## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The generator is a mechanically well-tested content factory: fifty-six families, every one checked for bar count, key, hands, register, span, spelling, rhythm and its own promise, with a mutation census proving the checks can fail. What it does not say is what each family is *for*. A parameter called `maxInterval` is written and never read back from the music; an open-voicing shape asks one hand for fifteen semitones and the suite exempts it; "canonical, variable, transfer" is a field the content model designed and nothing populates; a family is on a rung because a docstring says so. After D0, every family carries one contract stating its target skill, the opportunities it must provide at a useful density, the demands it must not bring, its physical constraints, its role and its promise (drill or music), and what the app can and cannot measure about it; every generated item is measured with the canonical detectors and passes the gates its promise requires; the physical gate refuses the open voicings as they stand; and nothing claims to be music that only promises to be a pattern. The sight-reading phrase, the study middle, the workbench and the distribution suite are the later D briefs; this one makes them possible.

## What is decided

1. **One family-contract table** (G1), data not docstrings, one row per maker, with: primary `targetSkill`; legitimate secondary target skills; prerequisites and assumed demands; required opportunities with the family's minimum useful density; forbidden and unintended demands; physical constraints where applicable; `role` (canonical, variable, transfer); the promise (`drill` or `music`); the assessment declaration (what can be judged, from which input, at what precision, what stays unjudged — G8); and what the family cannot prove, in words (the worked example's admission is the normal case). Target skills come from the vocabulary. A family may add a vocabulary skill only when all six hold (the reviewer's pre-dispatch constraint, 2026-09-26, so the ontology does not become generator-shaped): it is a learner ability, not a generator implementation detail; it is meaningfully distinct from existing skills and demands; an actual observable already exists; the evidence function can honestly attribute evidence to it under the existing semantics; at least one curriculum or teaching use exists beyond satisfying the family contract; and an adversary shows why the existing vocabulary cannot represent it. Otherwise the family's target is declared "not judged by the app" and the candidate skill is recorded for later vocabulary review. Every skill added carries a justification fixture — learner ability → observable → distinguishing adversary → family opportunity — checked by a build-time contract test and a unit evidence adversary. Zero additions is an acceptable outcome. Never a skill without an observable that claims evidence.
2. **Demands are measured, never declared** (G3, G4): the build runs the canonical detectors over every generated MusicXML and the contract is checked against what was measured; presence, useful density and overconcentration are three distinct checks with family-specific thresholds; one universal percentage is forbidden.
3. **Four gates** (G23), each applied as the promise requires: structural (the existing suite, preserved); pedagogical (the required opportunities present at density, the forbidden demands absent, the combination appropriate to the rung the item sits on); physical (simultaneous span, fingering where printed, repeated-note solution, thumb crossings, register, leaps, velocity at the stated tempo, duration and endurance); musical, only for families whose promise is `music`, and in this brief only as the hook the later sight-reading and study briefs fill — D0 does not write the musical evaluator. A drill family's repetition is never a defect; a `music` family's random legal notes are never accepted.
4. **The open voicings** (G38) fail the physical gate as they stand, red first; the resolution is one of the reviewer's three — redistribute the voicing between hands, a narrower physically appropriate arrangement, or an advanced large-hand voicing declared with its physical prerequisite and an alternative — chosen with the reason written in the contract. The suite's exemption is deleted.
5. **Roles are data** (G25): every family states which of canonical, variable and transfer it can honestly provide; transfer is never inferred from a different id or seed; a family that cannot establish transfer says so and the curriculum supplies transfer from elsewhere (an unfamiliar phrase, an excerpt).
6. **Every family is classified** drill or music, in the table, with the reason; the groove and style families (clave, tumbao, montuno, boogie, stride, comping, walking bass, rag, vamp, riff, ostinato) are `music` and marked "unheard" until a person hears them (G32, G29).
7. **Generated objects are named by what they are** (G11): a I–V–vi–IV loop, a i–♭VII–♭VI–♭VII vamp, son clave, a tumbao pattern, a ii–V–I progression; every title, docstring and catalogue fact that states a genre universal is rewritten to the pattern's name, and the contextual claim is left to F and G (T42–T45). `genre: ["technique"]` and `["drill"]` leave the genre field (G33).
8. **Identity** (G21): family + recipe + seed + generator version; the version changes when a recipe changes; the catalogue records it.
9. **The round-robin stays** (G18); nothing in this brief changes rung placement or selection. Note the consumer: `tieredAlternatives` (`app/src/curriculum/selectors.ts`) and the session's skill tier read `targetSkills` at runtime, so populating the field would switch those tiers on across the corpus. The reviewer's ruling (2026-09-26): D0 populates truthful `targetSkills` — that is a central output of the contract, and withholding it until E would make the contract documentary — and D0 also owns the activation safety of writing them. The gate is narrow: new target-skill metadata must not silently change learner-facing selection merely because C6 holds a dormant skill tier. Implement it as an explicit capability or eligibility condition at the C6 consumer boundary (`tieredAlternatives` and the session's skill step), never as fake metadata, empty strings, special skill names or test-only suppression; do not build E's needs-versus-taught machinery here. Acceptance: truthful target skills populated and the swap and session behaviour not widened; C6's constructed tests still exercise the skill tier explicitly; later activation deliberate and test-visible; E extends this one boundary with needs-versus-taught and measured-demand checks rather than adding a parallel selector — record that ownership in `docs/02` so nobody later writes a second readiness check beside it. Report how many items now carry target skills — the contract table is what a later brief reads to build canonical → variable → transfer sequences.
10. **Nothing is rebuilt**: the census and its mutation census, the fingering tests, the sight-reading promise and absence tests, reproducible seeds, the vocabulary, the roles, the reader's one dimension at a time, the honest refusal of impossible combinations. `generate_exercises.py` is split only where a split creates clear ownership of facts, recipes, realisation, validators or contracts (G13), never for size.
