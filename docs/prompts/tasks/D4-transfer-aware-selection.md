## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The ladder can say a skill is proficient, and the app has nothing to offer next on purpose: transfer today is whatever first read of a different reading row happens to come along. D0 gave items roles, D3 declared per recipe what a transfer study differs in, E1 brings approved excerpts with a durable identity, and Part 26 says transfer is three facts — has the learner met this exact material, how it relates to what established the skill, and a skill-relative claim — of which the claim belongs to a later evidence task. After D4 every run stores the exact versioned identity of the material played, reusing the identities the build and D2 already own; the evidence context carries it with the role and the relationship facts; contact novelty is read from those identities and never from an id alone; when a skill is proficient the session offers, deliberately and labelled as transfer-intended, a candidate that passes the one gate, differs from what established the skill along declared or measured dimensions, and this learner has not met — a generated transfer item or an approved excerpt; and nothing anywhere says a run demonstrated transfer: the ladder's v0 reading stays exactly as it is until the post-E policy reads the facts.

## What is decided

1. **One material identity, the build's.** `attach_provenance` writes `provenance.identity` on every catalogue row as D2's `Identity` (`review.current_identity`: the generator family, version, seed and recipe for a generated item; the built file's sha256 for a notated one, an excerpt's cut included; `none` for a placeholder), so the app never recomputes it. A runtime phrase's identity is the **complete** D2 generator identity — family, version, seed, the exact `ReadingRecipe` the phrase was drawn from, and the base tempo where D2's contract carries it — built from the `generator` triple D1a stores plus the recipe and tempo the run already knows; the triple alone is not an `Identity` and is never called one. Equality is proven: reopening the same version, seed and recipe compares equal; a changed version, seed or recipe does not. If tempo is found not to belong to sight-reading material identity, the builder does not drop it silently from D2's type: they define one shared discriminated material identity with explicit equality rules and adapt D2 to it, never two structures under one name (the reviewer's required change). No new identity is invented; an excerpt's chain key stays in E1's `excerpt` block and its `material` is its cut's file identity.
2. **Persisted on every run and in every evidence context.** `RunResult` and the stored row gain `material: Identity` (from the catalogue row, or from the phrase for a runtime run), `role` (the item's, where it has one) and `intent` (`'transfer'` where the run came from a transfer offer, else absent); `EvidenceContext` and its stored pick gain `material` and `intent`, the readers unchanged. Optional fields, no migration: a row without `material` is a legacy row. E1's excerpt context field is this `material`, not a second one.
3. **Contact novelty, from identities, conservatively.** `contact(item, material)` reads the store **by material identity across every item id** — a `byMaterial` index on the stored row, or a scan of stored identities; `sessionsForItem` alone cannot see a renamed id and is not enough — and by `itemId` for legacy rows: `met` where any row carries the same identity (exact: family, version, seed and recipe, or the same sha256); `met-by-id` where only legacy rows share the `itemId` — prior contact proven, the material unknown, so **never offered as transfer** until a row with `material` exists, and never read as `unmet`; `unmet` only where no row of either kind exists. A renamed id with the same identity is `met` (the identity, not the id, is compared where both sides have one); a new seed or a new generator version is new material (`unmet` as material) even where the id was met — reported as `unmet` with `metById: true`, so the later policy can read both. Never a store-side verdict: the function returns the facts.
4. **The offer, deliberate and labelled.** When a skill's ladder state is `proficient` (and not beyond), the session's `new` slot — after the rung's own new material, at most one transfer offer a day — proposes a candidate for that skill: an item with `role: transfer` whose `transferOf.skill` is the skill (D0's pentatonic items today; D3's studies only once a per-item affirmative teaching-use decision admits them through D3a's gate), or an excerpt whose measured demands establish the skill's opportunity **and** whose `provenance.review.teaching` is `true` on its current cut identity (E1's workbench approval establishes the cut and its boundary, not its teaching use; a measured opportunity and an approved cut are not enough; D3a's promise check does not reach an excerpt, so this is the same admission rule stated for the other candidate form, never a bypass) — that passes `eligibleFor` for the skill want, that is `unmet` for this learner, and whose relationship to the items the skill was shown on differs on at least one dimension the contract declares or the provenance measures (family; key; texture; hand pattern; rhythm; generated → authentic). The offer is its own transfer-intended `SlotClaim` with its reason text; it never displaces an unmet rung requirement, and it never reads v0's `transfer demonstrated` as proof that this relationship has been tested (the offer depends on `proficient` alone). The offer's copy says what it is for ("a fresh piece for a skill you have shown: it should feel different") and never that it will prove or has proved transfer. The swap sheet's tiers are not changed.
5. **The relationship, recorded as facts.** The offer and its run record `relationship`: the identities the skill was shown on (`shownOn`'s materials, as references — where the establishing evidence is a legacy row with only an `itemId`, an *unknown historical reference* carrying that id, never the catalogue item's current identity put in its place), and the dimensions on which the candidate differs from them — declared (`transferOf.differs`, `notMeasured` carried as such) and measured (family, key signature, texture and hand pattern from the provenance's measured facts). Nothing computes a distance; nothing writes a verdict.
6. **The ladder untouched, the words honest.** `ladder.ts` is not changed; its v0 condition may still turn `transfer demonstrated` on a first read of another reading row, and no screen copy ties that state to a D4 offer. Where the Progress screen or the sheet names the state, it keeps C7's words ("shown on different material"). A test holds that D4 changes no ladder state on any constructed history.
7. **Adversaries held at the selection layer** (Q6's, the ones D4 can answer; the rest are the evidence task's): a new seed of the family that established the skill is not offered as transfer (same family; it may still be a variable offer elsewhere); a `role: transfer` item with an untaught demand is refused by the gate; a duplicate or renamed id with the establishing identity is `met`; a regenerated item under a new version is `unmet` as material with `metById`; an unreviewed study is refused (`unreviewed`, D3a); an approved excerpt of a composition the learner played whole is `met` only where its identity was played — otherwise `unmet` with the composition relationship recorded; a sight-reading row is never a transfer offer (a reading drill, its own slot); a legacy history with `itemId` only yields `met-by-id`, never `unmet`, and the item is not offered; an excerpt with the right measured opportunity and an approved cut but `teaching: null` stays out, and the same excerpt with a current `teaching: true` may enter subject to the gate and the relationship checks; the same identity under a renamed id is `met` through the material index; a runtime phrase of the same version, seed and recipe is `met`, a new version of the same seed is not.
8. **Not D4's:** the transfer policy (the post-E evidence task: reading novelty, relationship and evidence together, replacing v0); runtime studies (deferred, per-item admission); Part 27's encounter model (D4 persists the minimum and the model consumes or migrates it); any change to what the readers credit; the chooser's ranking (E2); any lesson text.
