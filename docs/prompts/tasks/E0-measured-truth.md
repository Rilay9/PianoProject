## What is decided

1. **Demands are measured on every notated item at build time** (R38's evidence, Part 12 §5): the build runs the canonical detectors through the bridge D0 made real over every bundled score (authored, PDMX, Kern, MuseTrainer) **and every generated item** and writes `demands` on the catalogue entry, cached on the file's bytes like `attach_notation`; D0's build-time suite (`tests/planned.py`, `test_measured_demands.py`) measures the generated plan once per process today — after E0 there is one measurement, the attach step's, and the suite's tests either read the catalogue's ids or keep their own run for the red lines (the builder's call, said in a line, never two definitions); the located counts from `measure_opportunities` are what the density judgement below reads; an imported score gets the same at import time in the app through `detect.ts` directly (the one implementation), and again whenever the learner corrects the hand split (R34's truth half: the correction is source truth for rendering, demands, difficulty and eligibility, never a visual override). Measured on the arrangement in the file, never on the title, the genre, the level or the whole composition's reputation. Where a detector cannot read a file (an unknown notation, a part it cannot separate), the entry carries `demands: unmeasured` with the reason, never an empty list that reads as "no demands".
2. **Provenance on every content object** (R35, R15's levels, R11, Part 21 §B): a `provenance` record on every catalogue entry and import — source kind (authored, PDMX, Kern, MuseTrainer, imported MIDI, imported MusicXML, generated with D0's identity); the source or edition identity and, for compositions with several arrangements, the composition and arrangement identities as R15 defines them; the converter or generator version; for each fact, whether it was measured (the detectors, with the evidence-definition version), inferred (the converter's tempo default, a hand split, a key guess), authored, or human-reviewed (the two review decisions of R42 as separate bits: a usable score; a good teaching use). `tempo-defaulted` becomes one inferred fact among these and a tempo-sensitive demand is marked untrustworthy where the tempo is inferred. Inferred, measured and human-supplied facts are never flattened into one field.
3. **One gate** (R38, Part 23, Part 25 layer 6): `eligibleFor(candidate, learner, want)` in one module the whole app calls — never a second readiness check beside D0's activation boundary: the gate takes over the boundary's three curriculum readers (the swap tier, the session's skill want and its skill fallback), which call `eligibleFor` and nothing else. **The two evidence readers (`ScoreScreen.ts`, `evidenceJob.ts`) stay at `SHIPPED_SKILL_ACTIVATION`** — the nine reading rows — until D4 teaches the ladder to read role and family, because a new seed of one family would otherwise read as transfer (L102, D0's one deliberate boundary). E0 never widens what earns evidence. Two questions, answered from the facts the candidate has: *can the learner cope* — every measured demand of the candidate is one the learner's skill state supports (the ladder's `familiar` or above on the demand's `copedWithBy` skill, the rule the repertoire slot already uses) or is taught at or below the judging rung; *does it provide the opportunity* — the wanted demand or target skill is present at a useful density (D0's contract density for generated items; for notated items a documented per-demand minimum, never one universal percentage), told apart from incidental presence. Missing information is not false: an item with `demands: unmeasured` is eligible for exploration and project work and ineligible for any claim of skill practice, and the gate says which. Ranking is not this function's job (Part 25 layer 7); it only says eligible, ineligible with the reason, or eligible-for-exploration-only.
4. **C6's dormant tiers go live through the gate** (Part 23's activation condition, L36): `tieredAlternatives`'s skill and demand tiers, the session's skill and demand steps and the repertoire slot's demand claim call `eligibleFor` and nothing else; a candidate that shares a demand incidentally, or shares the target and adds an untaught demand, is refused; level orders eligible candidates and rescues nothing. The swap sheet's reason states the strongest fact known ("Also practises skips, with the other demands you have met"), never "similar difficulty" from level alone.
5. **The rung-claims report** (R38, Part 12 §1): a build-time report listing every rung option whose lesson claims an opportunity (the rung's `targetSkills` and the demands its requirements name) that the option's measured demands do not establish — the PDMX placements on `jazz.7`–`.9`, `latin`, `hymns`, `technique.4`–`.7` first — with the claim, the measurement, and whether a human review bit exists; the same report carries D0's 71 untaught-on-rung combinations for the generated items (`fixtures/untaught_on_rung.json`, L101), so placement reads from one report, and it marks the three detector readings E22 records as known misreadings (`walkingBass` on the stride left hand and the clave's pulse; a one-line second staff read as the bass staff) rather than fixing the detectors here. The report is written to `docs/prompts/` and to the entry; **nothing is removed from a rung by this brief**: the authored lists stay authoritative during the migration, as the reviewer decided, and the report is what F, G and the owner rewrite from. The validator gains the check as a warning, not a failure, until the reviewer says otherwise.
6. **The inventory** (R39): adequacy reported in usable experiences, not rows — distinct works, distinct arrangements, demand coverage by rung, texture, key, range and coordination coverage, whole piece against excerpt, measured against unmeasured, human-reviewed against not — as one generated table in `docs/prompts/`, regenerated by the build.
7. **Never changed here**: the vocabulary's demands (adding a demand is the vocabulary's own task, with a detector and a test); the rung requirements; the excerpt model; the chooser's purposes and ranking; any screen beyond the swap sheet's reason words.
