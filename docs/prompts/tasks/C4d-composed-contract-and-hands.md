## What is decided

1. **The contract covers composed recipes the reader can reach** (S29; the reviewer: not the Cartesian product). Walk the legal reader transitions from each served rung's row — every step up in the taught-at order, the step downs, the easy reads, and representative accumulated combinations at every rung the diaries reach (2.2, 2.5, 3.1 at least, and every core rung that offers a row) — and for each reachable recipe, over a run of seeds: every promised demand is detector-confirmed present, every demand taught later than the rung is absent, and nothing arrives that the controls did not declare. Where a composed recipe cannot be honoured reliably, the generator says so (`unrealisable` extended to compositions, or a declared list), and the reader treats it as unavailable: it does not offer it and the line says the next step waits, exactly as for a single impossible move. Silent substitution is the fault; a declared unavailability is not.
2. **The generator honours composed promises where it can.** C4c's hypothesis is that the redraw budget runs out with five promises at once; count redraws per promise set and say whether that is the mechanism before changing it. The five seeds in `KNOWN_BROKEN` (47615, 87210, 101, 39696, 71372) and day 28's missing tie are the red; when fixed, `KNOWN_BROKEN` empties and the diary's day 28 carries its tie.
3. **The hands-together opportunity means coordination** (L72). Redefine the detector's opportunity around meaningful two-hand events — a step where both hands sound a new onset, or where the left hand changes under the right — not every right-hand note over a held left-hand note. Add regressions that tell the two apart: sustained accompaniment (whole-note roots under a melody: the opportunities are the onsets where both hands move or the root changes, not every melody note) against genuine simultaneous or changing coordination (Alberti or broken patterns under a melody: many opportunities). Legitimate hands-together evidence must survive: the two-hand learner who plays both hands cleanly still earns it; the skip confound goes.
4. **The reruns** (the reviewer's list): the skip learner's thirty days, the two-hand skip learner (now singled out without needing a one-hand read: assert the day), the ambiguity adversary in both halves, and the composed-recipe contract. `docs/prompts/checkpoint-2026-09-27-diary.md` is regenerated and its diff read as a teacher: say which days changed and why.
5. **L76 is not built here.** The diagnostic probe (persistent ambiguity → isolate one competing demand while holding the others → ordinary evidence → adapt only if the probe discriminates; a neutral line) is designed in the matrix row and built after C5. If S29's work exposes a generic mechanism that makes it essentially free, say so in the report and stop there.
