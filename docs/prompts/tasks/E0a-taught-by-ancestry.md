## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The curriculum is not one line. From Stage 5 the tracks run in parallel, and a learner on the jazz track has never met what the blues track teaches. E0's gate asks "has this rung taught the demand" and answers from the position of the teaching lesson in the JSON file: because `blues.5` is stored before `jazz.5`, a walking bass reads as taught at `jazz.5` and at every later rung on every track, so material can look copeable to a learner with no evidence and no lesson behind it. After E0a the predicate answers from the rung's actual ancestry — the lessons reachable backwards through `prerequisites`, the rung included — so a demand taught on track A is not taught on track B, and is taught for a learner who reached the teaching rung on their own path. Nothing else about the gate changes; the three learners' mornings are rerun so the change is seen where it lands.
