## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The catalogue has drills, which repeat a pattern and are honest about it, and repertoire, which is somebody's music; it has nothing in between that a learner can read as a small piece written around the one thing they are learning. D3 adds that middle: a generated study of eight to sixteen bars in two four-bar phrases (or four), built around one target skill with only the supporting demands its rung has taught, with a left hand that accompanies rather than doubles, a beginning that establishes, a middle that varies or develops, and an ending that resolves on a cadence — chosen from many valid candidates by a musical evaluator that exists once for every Python family that promises music, judged by the same four gates as every family, measured by the same detectors, identified by the same family-version-seed-and-recipe, and put in front of a person through the microscope before any rung relies on it. Not a random-melody machine, not one golden template, not a claim of musical quality the record cannot back.

## What is decided

1. **One family, `study`, one contract row, promise `music`.** Its recipe: a target (a vocabulary *skill* where one exists — `interval-reading`, `position-shift`, `subdivision`, `syncopation` — and otherwise a vocabulary *demand* the study provides as an opportunity — `texture.hands-together`, `metre.compound` — using the existing identifiers and never inventing a skill to label a recipe, the reviewer's constraint), a key, a metre, a length (8, 12 or 16 bars), a left-hand texture (one of the accompaniment families' patterns: blocked chords, broken, Alberti, waltz bass, or a sustained root), and the rung whose taught set bounds every demand (E0a's ancestry, read the way `claims.py` reads it). Roles: `variable` (another key, texture or seed of the same recipe) and `transfer` (a study in a different key, texture and register from the drill family that teaches the same skill — D0's rule: transfer differs in surface and context, declared per dimension and measured where it can be). `heard: false` until D2's record says otherwise. Identity: D0's `drill.generator` triple plus the recipe (seed, key, metre, length, texture, target), as D2 found the identity really is.
2. **The realiser, on the architecture, not beside it.** A study is composed as: harmony first (a progression per phrase from a small grammar — I–V–I, I–IV–V–I, I–vi–IV–V–I and their minor forms — with a cadence at each phrase end and an authentic cadence at the close); a melody over it by constrained draws (hard layer: the target demand present at the family's density, the rung's taught set, range within the texture's hand, the leap cap from the skill's level, no forbidden demand; the same "valid first, then scored" rule as D1, with D1's fail-closed rule: a recipe that yields no valid candidate within its budget is refused with a reason at build time and never ships a draw it did not check); the left hand realised from the chosen texture over the progression; interval-correct spelling throughout (D0a's policy). A grammar and a distribution of plausible behaviours, never a template: the builder states the grammar in `docs/02` E2 and the entry.
3. **One musical evaluator for the Python families that promise music** (G9's second half): `family_contracts.musical_gate` stops being a hook for `study` and becomes an evaluator with parts mirroring D1's — beginning, arrival and cadence, contour, motif with variation, rests, harmonic agreement, leaps — each a pure function over the written notes with its own unit cases and its weight stated with its reason. **One definition, not two:** the builder either calls D1's TypeScript scorer through a bridge over the written file (the pattern `demands.py` uses for the detectors) or ports the parts to Python and holds each part equal to its TypeScript twin on a shared fixture of constructed phrases — the builder's call, with the reason. **The semantics adapt explicitly** (the reviewer's constraint): D1's scorer models four-bar phrases in major keys and infers the harmony from the left hand's first note in each bar; a study is longer, has several phrases and may be minor, so the evaluator's arrival, cadence and harmonic-agreement parts are defined for a multi-phrase form (an arrival per phrase, the authentic cadence at the close, the harmony read from the study's own progression rather than inferred) and for the minor forms, with unit cases for each — numerical parity with D1 on major four-bar fixtures alone proves none of that. For the groove and style families the gate stays "not evaluated: idiom needs hearing" — the evaluator judges phrase shape, not idiom (Part 15 §17).
4. **The four gates, applied.** Structural: the invariant suite covers the new family (bar count, key, hands, spelling, rhythm, MusicXML). Pedagogical: the target present at density, nothing untaught on the rung, the forbidden absent, overconcentration judged (a study is not a drill: exact repetition of a bar beyond the grammar's own restatement is a fault here). Physical: `physical_faults` on every item. Musical: item 3's evaluator, with a floor score below which the candidate is refused. Every gate red first on an adversary (a random-walk melody with the right demands; a study whose middle repeats its opening bar four times; a cadence on a non-chord tone).
5. **The plan, and no placement.** A fixed, seeded set: for each of the first targets, the study in its canonical key and texture and in two variable realisations, sixteen bars at most, listed in the Library and in the contract — **and on no rung** (the reviewer's required change, `responses/2c80472.md`): a study can meet the detector and density checks and still read as an exercise with an accompaniment, and a measured claim is not a teaching-use judgement. D3 instead writes a **candidate-rungs report** beside the entry: for each study, the rungs whose taught set contains every demand it carries and whose claims its measured demands would establish, with the exact claim and demand evidence from the merged build's report — the material a later placement decision uses together with a resolved `goodTeachingUse` in D2's record, that decision being F's and the owner's. `exerciseOptions` are not touched.
6. **Measured, identified, reviewed.** The studies go through E0's attach step like every item (the bridge, the cache, the provenance); the bridge regression gains one hand-read study; the identity pins gain the family; the microscope's queue puts the studies in its first tier beside the grooves, unheard; the builder walks three through the microscope and records `usableScore` from the notation with `goodTeachingUse` left undecided (a person's, by hearing), as D2's first line did.
7. **The distribution suite, in Python** (G22, `test_study_distribution.py` or the builder's name): over a seed set per recipe, report and bound arrival, contour, motif recurrence, cadence correctness, notes per bar, rest density, repeated notes, leaps, opportunity density of the target, near-duplicates, the rejection rate and the refusal rate; the bounds justified per metric and red on an adversary generator (the random walk), with the table in the entry.
8. **Not D3's:** wiring transfer into selection (D4); runtime unseen studies (D4, on the versioned identity); the grooves' idiom (a person, through the microscope); any lesson text; any change to the vocabulary or to existing families' notes (the evaluator may *report* on them, it changes nothing until their owners act — the entry lists what it would say about each music family, as information).
