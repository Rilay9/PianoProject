## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

Today a learner's rung is decided by counting passed items over the rung's lists, and a pass credits every rung that lists the item; sight-reading phrases get passed, mastered and a review date like pieces. Beside that, since C3 and C4, a second machinery reads evidence into skill states and chooses the daily read. The reviewer's rule for C5 is that these two must not run in parallel: after C5 there is one path — the rung's requirements are predicates over the evidence they name, the learner's rung state is derived from that evidence, `nextRecommended` reads it, a run's evidence is the learner's skill evidence everywhere while only the decision that a rung's requirement is met is scoped to the rung that judged the run (no item credit crosses rungs), and generated sight-reading carries no piece semantics. The old counting path is retired, its tests replaced, and what a learner sees on Plan, Progress and the lesson page is the derived state.

## What is decided

1. **A rung's requirement is a predicate over evidence.** `mastery.custom`'s terms and `exercisesRequired`/`songsRequired` become requirements stated over the vocabulary's skills and standards (design §4(c)): a rung is met when each named skill's ladder state (C3) over the learner's evidence is at or above the requirement's standard, with the two-day and support-share rules the ladder already has. The content declares it (`curriculum.schema.json` gains `requirements`; C2's `requirementTerms` interim mapping becomes the real field and is deleted); `validate.py`'s gate refuses a requirement no run can evidence, and the three waivers (1.5's first-attempt rule, 3.4's five reads, 4.6's continuity) become either a predicate the evidence can meet or an explicit "not yet measurable" the lesson's words honour (T2).
2. **One derivation.** A pure `rungState(rows, curriculum, vocabulary, today)` in `app/src/evidence/` returns, per rung, met / in progress (with the skills that hold and the ones that do not) / not started, from the stored evidence and the ladder; `nextRecommended` and placement read it; `lessonComplete`, `PassRecord`-based counting and `lessonForItem`-as-credit are deleted, not bypassed. `rungForSlot` (C4c's interim) reads the derived state.
3. **Evidence belongs to the learner's skills; requirement satisfaction is scoped to the judging rung** (L8; the reviewer's clarification, 2026-09-27). A run's evidence about a skill is evidence about that skill wherever it was observed: interval reading shown during a 2.2 assignment informs the learner model everywhere, including other rungs' predicates that name that skill, and a run from outside the curriculum updates the skills it declares. What is scoped to the record's judging rung (C1's `lessonId`, C3's `?rung=`) is the decision that *this rung's requirement* is met by *this run*: the requirement is checked against the run's evidence under the rung's standard, and no item-level credit exists at all — playing an item listed on three rungs never completes any of them by listing; each rung's predicate reads the skill evidence. Never make general skill evidence owned by the rung where it was observed.
4. **Sight-reading carries no piece semantics** (S8): generated phrases have no `status`, no mastered, no review date; their rows are observations with evidence, read by the reader and by `rungState`; `reviewQueue` skips them; the daily sight-read's done-day stays (it is a habit, not a mastery).
5. **The recompute job** (L78, L66): a background pass, on open and after a definitions change, re-reads rows whose evidence stamp is not current, regenerates the phrase from item and seed (or loads the file) and stores the current evidence through `recomputeEvidence`; progress is visible on the storage report; until a row is recomputed it contributes nothing, as decided. The owner's real rows from before C4a are the first test.
6. **What the learner sees follows the derived state**: the lesson page's lock line and "how you'll know" (T2, T26), Plan's map, Progress's skills read `rungState` and the ladder; the words in `help.ts`; the Progress screen's history keeps C1's lines.
7. **The reruns**: `firstThirtyDays`'s other two learners for the reading strand (the returning intermediate, the experienced musician, L31) through the real code, asserting their rung state is derived and moves for evidence and never for a count; the reviewer's sixth verification as a test: no item-completion path exists that can contradict `rungState` (a grep-level test that the deleted functions are gone, and a behavioural one that a pass on an item listed by two rungs meets only the one it was judged by).
