## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The one gate asks a candidate's coping question against the rung judging it: every demand the item asks must be supported by the learner's evidence or taught by the rung's ancestry. On the shipped curriculum 387 of the rungs' own authored options fail that question at their own rung — the rung assigned them, so the curriculum says they are teachable there, and the gate says they are not. The reviewer ruled that no refusal may read those 387 until the classification is corrected at whichever truth owns the error, and that a rung assigning material is itself a teaching relationship. This seam finds which truth owns each of the 387 and corrects it there, measured, so that the coping question and the curriculum agree — after which L113's split can be revisited on facts rather than on a mismatch.

## The causal model, with the tests that tell the causes apart

Four mechanisms can put a rung-own option among the 387; they are not exclusive, and the first thing built is the table that says which apply to each reading.

- **H1 — a claim gap.** The ancestry does teach the demand, in the lesson's own words and music, but no lesson `introduces` or `teaches` the concept that maps to it, so `taughtAt` never names an early rung. Evidence for it already: `steps` and `skips` are first claimed in Stage 1 while Stage 0's rungs assign stepwise songs (*Hot Cross Buns* at 0.3 asks `interval.step`); `eighth-notes` first in Stage 2 while Stage 1 songs carry eighths. **Test:** for each of the 387, whether a lesson at or below the rung mentions the concept's word in its text or names it in `concepts` under another spelling. If yes, the claim is missing, and the fix is the lesson's claim (the F claim rule then wants an option establishing it — the very option does).
- **H2 — an incidental demand read as asked.** The measurement tells *established at a useful density* from *incidental presence* (`eligibilityCore.ts` line 18, `measurement.established`); `demandsAsked` (about 117–118) returns the catalogue row's `demands` list for a measured item, so the question is what the build writes into that list — every demand the detectors found present, or only the established ones (read the demands step that writes it); if presence counts, a Stage 1 song with one ornamental sixteenth is `untaught` for sixteenths at every rung. **Test:** for each of the 387's demands, the measurement's verdict (`established` / `incidental` / `absent`) on that item; the share of the 387 that would not exist if only `established` demands were asked.
- **H3 — no concept exists for the demand.** Nothing in `CONCEPT_DEMANDS` maps to `rhythm.sixteenths`, `rhythm.triplets`, `range.beyond-position` or `rhythm.shorter-than-quarter`, so no rung can ever teach them and every option carrying one is `untaught` everywhere: about two hundred of the 387 are `rhythm.sixteenths` alone (the probe's demand counts). **Test:** the list of demands the 387 name that no concept maps to; for each, whether any lesson teaches the thing in its text (then H1 with a new table row) or the curriculum never teaches it (then the demand cannot be a refusal ground for authored options until it does — a rule, not a placement).
- **H4 — a genuine misplacement.** The option asks, at a useful density, a demand the ancestry does not teach in any lesson's words: the rung's author placed music beyond the rung. **Test:** what remains after H1–H3; each such option read as a learner meets it at that rung (the score, the lesson's text), and the nearest rung whose ancestry does teach it.

After L120b the report's count is zero or every remaining line has a recorded reason, and the validator's warning becomes an error.
3. **Not L120's:** L113's split (revisited only after L120b, on the facts), assigned imports (the coping refusal stays), the measurement's detectors, the claim rule (Q75), the session.

## Questions for the reviewer (the gate)

1. **Is an incidental demand asked?** If the measurement says a demand is present but not established, may the coping question ignore it for a rung's own options (H2), or is any presence a demand on the learner?
2. **A demand no rung teaches.** For `rhythm.sixteenths` and the others with no concept: add the concept where a lesson does teach it, or rule that such a demand is not a refusal ground until the curriculum teaches it? Both keep the 387 from refusing; they differ in what the curriculum claims.
3. **The order of truths.** Claims first (H1), then concepts (H3), then the reading (H2), then placements (H4) — is that the order you want, so that a placement is moved only when no truth above it explains the reading?
