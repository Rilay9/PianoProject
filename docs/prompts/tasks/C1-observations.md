## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

Every run leaves one record of what it measured, by its own definitions, with every channel it did not measure marked as not measured rather than zero or absent. Nothing the engine computed for the sheet is thrown away any more: per-step outcomes, timing per note when it was timed, the technique measures, hands, what the keys showed, the input, the context that opened the run, the range played. The two runs T40 stopped recording are recorded again, flagged, so practice is never lost and the flags keep them out of the evidence they cannot support. The keyboard's guide is off for sight-reading drills, and whether it was on is on every record. Nothing reads the new fields yet except the Progress history and backup; that is C3's and C4's job.

## What is decided

1. **The observation shape** is the design's §3, extended onto `SessionRow` and `RunResult` rather than a new store: the header (item and `range`; the opening context: rung, Today slot, route; mode; tempo asked, measured or not, base tempo and whether it was written or defaulted; hands, and whether the app played the other; what the keys showed: strip, guide, fingers, names; grace notes judged; input source, tolerance window; `seed`, `unseen`, `demonstrated`; performance, rhythm-only, self-report; a `definitions` version) and the measures (pitch with its definition and denominators; wrong, missed, early; per-step outcomes; timing per matched note, or not measured; technique measures with "not measured" kept; pedal, rolled and lenient chords, loops; duration and time). Where a field cannot be filled from what the engine has today, it is stored as not measured, never invented, and the report lists it. Continuity is not invented ahead of its measure.
2. **T40's two drops are reversed** (reviewer decision 3, design §11 item 6): a sight-read heard before its first run is recorded with `unseen: false`; a performance with a demonstration inside it is already recorded without the performance flag (T40; `scoreSummaryTruth` "Hear it part way") and now also carries `demonstrated: true`. T40's sheet sentences stay (the run is not counted as sight-reading or as a performance); the minutes, the attempt and the day's tick count as practice. A run nothing heard (T40 case 1) stays unwritten unless answered: nothing was measured.
3. **The keys guide is off for sight-reading drills by default** (reviewer decision 5), the setting still the learner's for everything else, and the record says what the keys showed on every run.
4. **The sessions cap** (Q26, L48): counts measured runs, is raised to what the storage report shows is affordable (measure it: the report screen already sizes the stores), and prunes by compacting old per-step arrays to per-bar after the retention window rather than deleting rows; `sessionRetention`'s "prunes without being asked" test that prunes itself is revised. State the numbers as relationships in the tests.
5. **The Progress history** prints what was measured: a Wait run's line does not say "88 % at 70 %" (L41); a self-reported run prints the answer (L43); a run with `unseen: false` or `demonstrated: true` says so in a word.
6. **`lessonId` is no longer filled from the first listing rung** when nothing opened the screen; a run from nowhere records no opening rung and is judged by Part G's defaults (design §10 C1).
7. **Backup** carries the new fields and restores them; the version note in `01` §4.5 says what changed and why no store changed shape (or bumps `DB_VERSION` only if an index changes, with the upgrade keyed on `oldVersion`).
