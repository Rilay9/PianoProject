## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

D3a made the rule for generated music: no affirmative teaching-use decision, no automatic offer. D3b and D3c carried it to the card and the rung page. E1's excerpts are notated, so the one predicate lets them through, and only the fact that none is placed keeps them off the glass. The reviewer's rule is that an excerpt is music whose teaching suitability is not established until a person says so, exactly like a study. After E1a the same predicate refuses an excerpt whose current cut identity has no `teaching: true`, so every consumer that already reads it — the gate's verdict, the card's filter, the swap sheet's tiers, the rung page's picks, and D4's transfer offer to come — refuses it with no second rule; the Library lists it and exploration opens it; a decision recorded on an earlier cut of the same definition admits nothing, because the bit is filled only on the current identity.

## What is decided

1. **The predicate, extended once.** `unapprovedMusic(item)` (or the helper `admittedForTeaching` reads) returns the stored bit for an item that is an excerpt (`isExcerpt`, the type) as it does for a music-promising generated item: `null` or `false` when `provenance.review.teaching` is not `true`, `undefined` (admitted) when it is. `eligibleFor` therefore refuses an unadmitted excerpt for `skill`, `requirement`, `demand` and `equivalent` as `teaching-use-not-approved` with the bit carried, and passes it for `exploration`; `session.usable()` and every path through it refuse it; the rung page's picks (D3c) refuse it through the same call. No excerpt-specific chooser, no branch in any consumer, no second reading of the fact or the bit (the D3b test that `session.ts` reads neither is kept, and one like it holds `LessonScreen.ts` once D3c lands).
2. **E1's reader exclusion stays, and stops being the only guard.** `selectors.ts`'s rule that an unplaced excerpt is offered by no whole-catalogue tier remains (an unplaced excerpt is a Library candidate, not practice), and the admission now holds also for a *placed* excerpt: a rung that lists one offers it only once a `yes` exists on the cut.
3. **Staleness.** The build fills `provenance.review.teaching` from a decision on the item's *current* identity only (D2, `fill_reviewed`); for an excerpt the identity is the cut file's sha256, so a `yes` recorded on an earlier cut of the same definition (a moved endpoint, a changed parent) leaves the bit `null` on the new cut and the excerpt unadmitted. E1a proves it: a record with a `yes` on an older cut's sha256 and a rebuilt cut with a new sha256 — the bit `null`, the admission false, the gate's verdict `teaching-use-not-approved` (`test_review_record.py` or `test_measured_truth.py` for the build side; `eligibility.test.ts` for the app side on a constructed row).
4. **The regressions, red first:** (a) `eligibility.test.ts`: an excerpt with otherwise eligible measured demands (the built Anh. 113 cut, or a constructed one) refused for skill, requirement, demand and equivalent wants at `teaching: null` and at `false`, eligible for exploration, and admitted to the existing gates at `true` (still refused where the learner does not cope); the Q8 case revised so the excerpt's eligibility is shown with `teaching: true` set on the constructed row and its refusal at `null` stated — the old assumption ("the excerpt is eligible at classical.3") becomes "eligible once approved, refused until then", classified as a revision with the reason; (b) `gateAtTheConsumers.test.ts`: a rung-listed excerpt (a constructed rung listing the cut) refused by the card's `usable()` and by the swap sheet's lesson tier at `null` and `false`, offered at `true`; the whole-catalogue sweep extended to excerpts; (c) `excerptItems.test.ts`: the Library still lists every excerpt and exploration opens one at `null`; (d) the staleness case of item 3; (e) mutants: excerpt admission removed from the common predicate (every case red), and from a direct consumer by bypassing the predicate at `usable()` for excerpts (the card cases red).
