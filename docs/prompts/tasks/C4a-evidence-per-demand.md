## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The evidence must keep what happened at each demand's opportunities, so that a reader can see that the skips went wrong while the steps went right, without the evidence ever saying "the skips caused it" where a wrong note sat under several demands at once. And the evidence must carry its own version, because this is the first change to its shape and the observation's version is not the evidence's. Nothing about the rule against inference changes: presence is never evidence, an unmeasured channel yields nothing, a heard phrase is refused, and the counts say "right and in time under these conditions" and no more.

## What is decided

1. **Per-demand counts on the evidence.** `MeasuredEvidence` gains `byDemand: { demand, n, right, steps }[]`: for each demand in the skill's opportunity list that the played passage contains at measured steps, `n` is the number of that demand's opportunity steps that were measured, `right` the number right by the channel's definition, `steps` the opportunity step indices (so a later reader can audit). A step that is an opportunity for several demands is counted in each. The skill-level `n` and `right` stay what they are (the ladder reads them). No field says which demand caused a miss.
2. **The overlap is a fact on the evidence, not a judgement.** For each entry, `overlap: { demand, steps }[]` lists which other demands (of any declared skill in this run) share its opportunity steps, so a reader can tell an isolated failure from a mixed one. Where a demand has no measured opportunity in the passage it is absent from `byDemand`; the skill-level `no-opportunity` refusal stays only when no demand of the skill had one.
3. **Demand readings, pure, in `readingState.ts`.** `demandReadings(rows, vocabulary, today)` returns, per demand, the recent opportunities and rights over the reading strand's evidence and one of three selectivity facts computed from the data: `isolated` (most of its wrong steps are not opportunities of another failing demand of the same skill), `pattern` (over at least two phrases its share is below the skill's support share while the skill's other demands' shares are above it), or `ambiguous` (neither). The arithmetic and the thresholds are named constants marked hypotheses, printed in `05` §9b, with the three worked examples from Part 8 as tests: the skip learner (five reads, skips fail, steps hold → `pattern` for skips); one wrong note with four properties (`ambiguous`); and the reviewer's **mixed-demand ambiguity adversary**: two phrases whose wrong notes are all events that are skips and eighths at once (`ambiguous` for both), then contrasting reads — skips in quarters succeed, steps in eighths fail — after which eighths become `pattern` and skips do not. The readings must discriminate when the observations allow it and refuse when they do not. The reader (C4c) will act only on `isolated` or `pattern`; you provide the facts, not the move.
4. **The evidence's own version** (L66): `EVIDENCE_DEFINITIONS` in the evidence module, stamped on every stored evidence result as `evidenceDefinitions`; `storedEvidence` accepts only the current stamp; the observation's `definitions` stays the observation's. A pure `recomputeEvidence(row, played, vocabulary)` returns what the record call would store today, so a later job can refresh old rows given the model; the trigger is not built (say so). Bump the stamp in this task, since the shape changed.
4b. **Evidence is keyed by musical demand, never by a reader's control** (Part 8 §4 of the fourth message): `byDemand` and `demandReadings` speak in the vocabulary's demand ids; nothing in the evidence module knows the six dimensions or any generator option. C4c maps supported demand evidence to whatever control the generator offers.
5. **Restraint kept**: `evidenceFor`'s decision order (target → channel → conditions → opportunity → precision → attribution) is untouched; every refusal still cites the record; the self-assessed class is untouched; nothing reads `byDemand` for the ladder.
6. **The adversarial cases** (Part 8 point 7 of the second message, the evidence half; the reviewer's list): accurate steps with inaccurate skips (single hand, so the skips are `isolated`); accurate pitch with poor rhythm (pitch demands high, timing demands low); accurate right hand with a poor left hand (the hands-together opportunities fall, the right-hand-only demands hold); a difficult passage performed accurately; an easy one performed poorly (`ambiguous` where every demand fell together); a passage containing a demand with no measurable opportunity for it (absent from `byDemand`; the skill refused or not per today's rule); a heard, demonstrated or re-read passage performed perfectly (refused, as today); no input (nothing). Each case asserts the evidence and the demand readings; C4c asserts the selection.
