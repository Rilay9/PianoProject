## What is decided

1. **The correction rule, for every sentence in item 2.** A sentence says only (a) what the app does, verified at the line or read from the built catalogue; (b) what the notation shows, read in the built file; or (c) a teacher's heuristic said as one — "a common starting point", "one way", "often", "try" — never as a law. No invented cause, no ranking of causes without a source, no superlative or count the evidence does not carry, no count put in a count's place (F1's rule: no "usually" or "most" standing in for "almost every"), no historical claim without a source. A source is cited only from F0's list (S1–S9) or when the builder has read it and names the section; never from memory. The advice under each sentence is kept: the activity, the concept, the check at the end of the lesson. *Never teach wrong*: where the only correction would be a musical judgement nobody here can make, the claim is removed, not replaced by a guess. The builder writes every replacement; this brief supplies no wording.
