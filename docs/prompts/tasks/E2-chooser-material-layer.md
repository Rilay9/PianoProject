## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

The gate answers one question well — can this learner cope, and does this item provide the opportunity wanted — but it answers it only for catalogue items, from a `Want` that names a skill or a demand, and it knows nothing of what a candidate's source can and cannot prove. Part 25 says the chooser must ask the material question before the source question, must let each source prove only what it can, and must treat missing information as a limit on the claims allowed rather than as false. After E2 there is one candidate contract that a bundled score, a generated item, an approved excerpt, a learner's import and an external recommendation all satisfy; a material-requirements object that states the target opportunity, the allowed, supporting and forbidden demands, range, hands, duration, novelty, completeness and physical limits, and which the gate reads as it reads a want today; a validity function per source kind that says which of those requirements the candidate's facts can answer and which it cannot; and an import path whose stored scores are measured once, versioned by the converter that inferred their split and tempo, corrected by the learner where the inference was wrong, and never evidence by assignment. The session's purposes and experiences, ranking and composition are not touched: E2 gives them the material layer to stand on.

## What is decided

1. **The candidate contract.** A `Candidate` is one of: a catalogue item (bundled, generated, excerpt), an import (through `importToCatalogItem`, as today), or an external recommendation (a title, a source, an estimated level and demands with a confidence, a provenance saying they are estimates — I14, never a `CatalogItem`, never playable). Each carries its source kind, its `provenance` where it has one, and its measured facts where it has them. The type lives in a new `app/src/curriculum/candidates.ts`; nothing in the app constructs a recommendation yet (X's screens do); E2 constructs them in tests and in one seed reader.
2. **Material requirements as data.** `MaterialRequirements`: the target opportunity (a skill or a demand, as `Want` names today), allowed and supporting demands, forbidden or unprepared demands (the learner's `uncoped` set, read at the gate), range and hands, duration bounds, novelty (`unmet` by D4's `contact` reading once D4 lands, or familiar), musical completeness (a whole piece, a phrase, a section), physical limits (D0's). `eligibleFor` gains an overload that takes a `MaterialRequirements` and a `Candidate`; the existing `Want` calls are expressed as the simplest requirements and keep their verdicts byte for byte (the E0, D3a and E1a cases are the regression). No caller changes in E2.
3. **Source-specific validity.** `validityOf(candidate)` says, per requirement, whether the candidate's facts can answer it: generated — the family contract and the four validators; notated — measured demands and the two review bits; an excerpt — the parent's provenance, the cut's demands, the boundary decision and the admission; an import — conversion provenance, the corrected inference, measured demands; a PDF — nothing about the notes, a duration and a title only; an external recommendation — estimates with confidence, never measured. The gate reads validity before its questions: a requirement the source cannot answer makes the candidate `exploration-only` for that requirement with the missing fact named (E0's `missing`), never eligible by silence and never ineligible by absence — Part 25 §6, Q8's external recommendation valid for exploration and ineligible for a claim of syncopation reading.
4. **The import workflow's store side.** (a) E26: the app's constant moves into `app/src/import/midi/convert.ts` so the converter owns it (`importStore.ts` re-exports it, callers unchanged); `tools/midi-cleanup/midi_to_musicxml.py` gains its own, written into what it emits (the MusicXML encoding block, or a comment if the schema forbids it — say which) and read back by the import's provenance; the provenance's `converter` block stays its shape. (b) E25: on the next launch every stored import whose provenance carries no measured demands (`facts.demands` absent, or `measurement.status` not `measured` for a reason that no longer holds) or a `converter.version` older than the current is measured once through `measureImport`, in the background, the result written to the row through the store's own update path, the screens reading the measured truth — a migration with a test on a fixture store. (c) The assign sheet's flow stays what T52 made it (assignment yields no evidence and meets no rung). (d) An import is a candidate like any other: eligible for a demand it measurably provides once its inference is corrected or trusted, `exploration-only` while a tempo-sensitive demand rests on an inferred tempo (E0's `untrusted`).
5. **The seed list (E28) as a proposal reader.** A small `content/sources/teaching-repertoire.json` naming public-domain compositions per concept, read by `finder.py`'s queries and by `excerpt_proposer.py` to order its parents; nothing in it admits anything (a proposal source, the reviewer's E28 ruling).
6. **The twelve adversaries at the material layer** (Part 25's list; each test holds the fact E2's layers supply and names the later layer that finishes the case): (1) a generated transfer study and an authentic excerpt compared for a transfer requirement, each carrying its own source kind and validity, neither pretending the other's origin; (2) a PDMX excerpt the learner has seen: eligible for practice, refused for a first-contact requirement (novelty from D4's `contact` when it lands; a fixture contact reading until then); (3) a corrected import's validity equal to a bundled score's for measured demands; (4) the same import ineligible before its hand correction and eligible after, the requirement unchanged; (5) a PDF candidate's validity answering nothing about the notes, so it is `exploration-only` for every measured requirement and never a competence claim (the evidence grammar is L24's); (6) an external recommendation eligible for a discovery requirement, `exploration-only` for a measured one; (7) an isolating generated exercise and an authentic candidate under a completeness requirement — E2 holds the completeness validity fact; the choice is ranking's (layer 7, X); (8) a musically excellent excerpt refused for an untaught demand (E0's gate, a regression); (9) nothing satisfying the requirement returns `none` with the unmet requirements named, never a weakened gate — changing the experience is the teacher layer's (X); (10) one piece under two requirements on one identity, the purpose records elsewhere; (11) a sight-reading requirement met by a generator phrase, an approved excerpt or suitable imported notation alike — sight-reading never implies the generator; (12) interest is not a fact validity or eligibility reads — a tie-break is ranking's.
7. **Not E2's:** the session's purposes and experience contracts, ranking and composition (X's, L32); any screen (X3 builds the import experience on this); the chooser's use of external recommendations on Today (X); any change to what the readers credit; `db.ts` and its schema (D4 is changing it now; the migration uses the store's existing update path, and if a schema change proves necessary, stop and say why); D4's offer (it consumes the contract when it lands, or E2 adapts to D4's `material` and `contact` names if D4 lands first — read Entry 106 if it exists when you start, and say which).
