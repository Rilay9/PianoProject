## The goal, in the orchestrator's words

E0 made every bundled score honest about the demands it carries and refused any assignment the notes do not establish. The report that fell out shows it in numbers: nine rung claims are kept by no whole piece, and a whole piece is the wrong unit for most of them — the four bars where the leaps are, the phrase where the walking bass starts, the passage in the Minuet a Stage 3 learner could read while the whole Minuet is beyond them. The repository has one notion of a bar range today, `teaching.sections`, which names bars of an item for the Score loop and carries nothing else. After E1 an excerpt is its own content object: cut by the build from the parent's built file into its own score file, so E0's measurement, D2's identity and record, the eligibility gate and the screens all read it unchanged; identified by the parent's bytes, the printed range, the hand selection and the cut version, so a moved boundary is a different excerpt, a renamed one the same, a parent whose title changed the same and a parent whose notes changed a stale one; proposed by a miner that scores windows for the requested opportunity and for musical completeness and never for the lowest difficulty; approved by a person in the microscope with the bars before and after the cut on the page and in the ear, a boundary moved by a bar being an ordinary review action; placed on no rung (a candidate-rungs report, D3's ruling); and never evidence that the whole piece was played. Sections stay what they are. PDMX is one producer of parents among the bundled scores; the object serves imports later (E2, X) without being built for them now.

## What is decided

1. **The object is a catalogue item, `type: 'excerpt'`, with its own score file.** Its id is derived from its definition and nothing else: `excerpt.<parent id without its leading `song.`>.b<from>-<to>` with `.rh` or `.lh` appended for a one-hand selection (`excerpt.classical.bach-menuet-bwv-anh-113.pdmx.b1-8`); the schema's id pattern and `type` enum grow by one each; `excerptOf` names the parent; `hands` is the selection; `level` is estimated on the cut by `difficulty.py` (`levelSource: estimated`), never the parent's; `tracks` and `source` (licence, edition notes) are the parent's — a cut of a CC BY-NC edition stays under `--allow-nc`, a personal-build parent's cut is personal-build, and `--strict-license` refuses the cut where it refuses the parent; `concepts` are the targets' concepts where the vocabulary maps them, else none; `title` is the row's label or "<parent title>, bars a–b", display only. `teaching.sections` is not touched, not read and not written by anything in this brief. The repertoire lifecycle, retention and the "performed" reading keep to songs: an excerpt run enters none of them (adversary 10).
2. **The definition lives in `content/sources/excerpts.json`, written by the merge and read by the build.** One row per approved excerpt: `of`, `fromBar`, `toBar` (printed bars, 1-based, the pickup as bar 1, exactly as `sections.json` counts and `validate.printed_bars` checks), `selection` (`both`, `right`, `left`), `targets` (existing vocabulary skill or demand ids the cut was approved for — never an invented name), `label`, `note`, and the approving event (`event`, `by`, `at`) from the workbench. `validate.py` checks each row beside the sections check: the parent exists and is a notated item with a built file, the range lies inside the parent's printed bars, the selection is valid for the parent's staves, the targets exist, the derived id is unique and no two rows share parent, range and selection. A range crossing a repeat sign, a volta or a first-and-second ending refuses at build with the bars named (the cut would mean something else unrolled; the proposer never proposes one).
3. **The build cuts it: a new step after the parents' files exist and before score checks, demands and provenance.** `tools/content/excerpts.py` cuts the parent's *built* file (never the archive, never the raw source) by printed bar numbers with music21 as `bisect_render.py` already does, carrying in the clef, key, time and tempo state at the cut, keeping a pickup bar the row includes, severing a tie into the first bar (a plain note) and out of the last (dropped), keeping both staves for `both` and dropping the unselected staff for a one-hand cut through `drop_silent_staves` (a single-staff score, which the loader accepts), and writing `scores/excerpts/<id>.mxl` with a **normalised header** — the excerpt's id as the work title and none of the parent's credits — so the cut's bytes depend on the notes and the definition alone. The header carries no credits for byte stability, and nothing else may lose them: the excerpt's catalogue `source` (name, url, licence, `editionNotes`) is the parent's, the provenance's `parentEdition` names the edition, and every path that shows an item's attribution — the Library's Source and Licence rows (`LibraryScreen.ts` 651–652), the lesson row's source label (`LessonScreen.ts` 228), the folder library's source (`folderLibrary.ts` 408) — shows the parent's for an excerpt; a test holds an excerpt's `source` equal to its parent's and the Library rows to show it (the reviewer's constraint, `responses/bf2666a.md`). Then `score_checks`, `attach_demands` (E0's bridge and cache, so the demands are the cut's; nothing of the parent's is copied), `attach_notation`, `attach_provenance` and the render check treat it as any file: excerpt-local measurement costs no new code path (R7, R38).
4. **Identity is a key over the parent's bytes and the definition, and D2's file identity does the rest.** `attach_provenance` writes `source: 'excerpt'` with an `excerpt` block — `of`, `fromBar`, `toBar`, `selection`, `cutVersion` (a constant in `excerpts.py`, bumped with the cutter), `parentSha256` (the parent's built file at cut time), `parentEdition` (the parent's edition identity) and `key` (sha256 over `parentSha256`, the range, the selection and `cutVersion`) — and carries the parent's `composition` and `arrangement` identities down the chain (R15, R35). The cut file's own sha256 is D2's identity for the record and the microscope, unchanged in shape. The consequences are the adversaries, each a test (below): the same bars with the other hand are a different key, id and measurement; an endpoint moved one bar is a different key, id and bytes, and every decision on the old one is the old one's; a parent whose catalogue metadata changed leaves the cut byte-identical and the key unchanged; a parent whose file changed gives a different key and bytes, and the old provenance's `parentSha256` no longer matches — stale by provenance, detectable by the build and named in the validator's warning.
5. **Mining proposes and creates no truth.** `excerpts.py propose --for <skill or demand id> [--of <parent id>] [--rung <rung id>] [--bars 4..8]` runs over the merged build's catalogue and writes `build/excerpts/candidates.json` and its builder-only projection `app/public/dev/review/excerpts.json` (D2a's `dev/` root, gitignored, out of the precache). It never cuts a window to score it: the bridge (`demands.py` and `demandsOfFiles.test.ts`) is extended to write, beside each demand's located count, the printed bar of every located place (the note's `sourceMeasureIndex`, which `sections.json` and the loop count from; `DemandAt` carries only the unrolled measure today) — positions `detect.ts` already computes and the bridge drops — once per parent, cached with the counts. Windows of 4 to 8 printed bars (2 to 16 allowed by flag) are scored from those positions and from the parent's notation by pure functions, one per signal, each with unit cases and its weight stated with its reason: the target's opportunity at a useful density inside the window; no demand the judging rung has not taught (E0a's ancestry, E0b's lists, read as `claims.py` reads them) and none the family contracts forbid; a phrase start (bar 1, after a rest or a long note, at a double bar, a rehearsal mark, a repeat sign, or where the bass changes chord on a strong beat) and a pickup included when the notes before the nominal start belong to its phrase; an ending that resolves (a cadence read from bass root motion or chord symbols, a long note or a rest) or is marked "leads onward"; texture and hand continuity (both hands sounding where the parent has both; no window that begins on one hand's rest and ends on the other's); a useful length; physical plausibility (D0's `physical_faults` over the window's spans); enough occurrences of the target to practise it. A candidate's score is a sum the reviewer can see part by part; **nothing ranks by lowest difficulty**, and a mutant that does is red. The proposer joins the PDMX package's workflow as a step after `commit.py` and before the build, not a fork of it: its candidates read the catalogue the quarry committed, and the reviewer's approval lands in `excerpts.json` the way a quarry keep lands in `pdmx.json`.
6. **The workbench is the microscope's excerpt view, and the reviewer sees and hears outside the cut.** A route beside the item view (`#/dev/microscope/excerpts`, or the builder's) lists the candidates by target with their part scores; for one candidate the parent is rendered with the window marked and a margin of bars either side, played from before the cut to after it with the existing renderer and playback (the Score session's loop with a margin); the window's measured facts and the parent's sit beside it; the boundary moves a bar at a time by a control, re-scored live from the positions (the cut itself stays the build's); the decision is approve (targets and label), adjust-and-approve, or reject with a reason, kept on the device and exported as a file like D2's decisions, merged by `excerpts.py --merge` into `excerpts.json` idempotently by event id (a re-merge appends nothing; an approval of a range already present is refused with the row named). The built excerpt then enters D2's queue like any item, and `usableScore` and `goodTeachingUse` are decided on the cut, on its own identity, in D2's record unchanged.
7. **The gate and the evidence read the excerpt as the object it is.** `eligibleFor` needs no excerpt branch: the cut's measured demands are what it reads — Q8's case on the real corpus is the acceptance: the whole Anh. 113 refused for a Stage 3 want and its excerpt eligible, with the parent's demands unchanged by the cut (adversaries 1 and 2). A run of an excerpt writes the excerpt's `key` into the run's evidence context (an optional `identity` on `EvidenceContext`, stored beside `itemId` and `seed`, read by nobody yet — Part 26's versioned fingerprint), marks neither the parent performed nor the excerpt as repertoire, and yields skill evidence only through the existing readers at the nine reading rows (E0's constraint (a), kept: the measured opportunities are in the provenance the context points at, not a new credit). Every reader of `item.type` is reviewed for its meaning, not made exhaustive by the compiler: the entry carries a table — reader, what it does with a song, an exercise and a drill, what it must do with an excerpt and why — for `selectors.ts` 204 and 227 (`excludeSongs`), `session.ts` 671–672 and 768 (the `songs` filter), 763 (the requirement pool), 1027, 1525–1526 (an equivalent's kind), `LessonScreen.ts` 534, `SkillsScreen.ts` 130, `LibraryScreen.ts` 72 (the filter union) and whatever else `tsc` lists; an `else` branch that would treat an excerpt as a song or an exercise by default is a fault, and each reader whose treatment of an excerpt differs from its default gets a unit case (the reviewer's constraint, `responses/bf2666a.md`).
8. **The first excerpts, and no placement.** With the combined branch built (the reviewer's constraint; the committed report is never the input), the builder runs the proposer for the claims the rung-claims report says no option keeps — 1.5's leaps, 3.1's notes outside the key, technique.5's syncopation, ragtime.5's oom-pah, the walking bass on blues.5, blues.6 and blues.8 — and for Q8's Anh. 113 case, walks the candidates through the excerpt view, and approves as **unplaced Library candidates** only those on which every defined signal is met by the rules — a phrase-start signal fired at the start, a cadence or lead-on signal at the end, the pickup present where the signal calls for it, the target at density, nothing untaught for the judging rung, nothing forbidden, the physical gate passed — a first handful of three to six where that many exist and none where none do. The builder records what the signals and the source-backed rules establish and claims no musical-ear or teacher authority: `usableScore` from the notation with its category, `goodTeachingUse` left undecided, and the uncertainty stated on the item ("boundary by rule, unheard"); the entry lists each approved candidate with its signals and each rejected or moved one with the signal that decided. Every excerpt is on **no rung**: a candidate-rungs report beside the entry names, per excerpt, the rungs whose taught set holds every demand it carries and whose claim its measured demands would establish, from the combined build's report (D3's precedent, `responses/2c80472.md`); placement is F's, on a stated non-owner gate: a rung option is added only where the candidate-rungs line is established on the combined build with nothing untaught **and** D2's record holds a current `goodTeachingUse: yes` on the cut's identity by a named reviewer stating their basis; until both exist the excerpt stays in the Library unplaced and no learner assignment reaches it through a rung. The owner supplies neither musical review nor placement (`responses/bf2666a.md`). `exerciseOptions` are not touched.
9. **The density rules, read per window.** A whole-piece `min` cannot be met in six bars, which is the density file's own point. The proposer and `attach_demands` read one rule: `perBar` reached and at least the file's stated window minimum for the demand (a new `minInWindow` per rule where the whole-piece `min` exceeds what an eight-bar cut can hold; two occurrences where the file says nothing), each value marked a hypothesis with its reason, as E0's are; the report shows every excerpt a rule refuses so a threshold that rejects a good cut is visible. The values stay the builder's and the reviewer may set them otherwise; a threshold met is never proof of teaching quality, and every candidate a rule refuses is reported.
10. **Not E1's:** the chooser and any ranking among eligible excerpts (E2, Part 25); the encounter and transfer models (Parts 26–27; the identities are written for them and read by nobody); excerpts of a learner's imports or projects and the workbench over them (E2 with X; the object and the cutter must not assume a bundled parent, and the entry says what an import would need); sight-reading from an approved excerpt (the chooser's); any rung, lesson or `sections.json` change; the evidence readers; the PDF path; PDMX genre, which stays useless for selection and enters no score.
