# Owner direction — learner experience variety (2026-10-06)

The curriculum must not become a sequence of technically correct but monotonous Score/Keep-tempo lessons.

Variety is not a quota and every chain does not need every mode. The rule is to choose the tool or mode because it is the best learner action for that step, not because Score is the easiest implementation path.

Across the MUST abilities, the finished curriculum should make real teaching use of the app's different experiences where they fit: Simon, Lab, Jam, Free Play, Duet/one-hand work, listening/hearing, Rhythm only, generated CONTROL and generated musical material, real excerpts, full repertoire, chord-chart work, improvisation and other packet experiences. Each has a distinct honest job under MODE-SHEET; none gets credit for evidence it cannot establish.

Review the curriculum across abilities for accidental monoculture: repeated Keep-tempo/Score use where another existing experience would teach the learner better, major modes or surfaces that never get a real consumer, improvisation or ear work existing only as prose, or generated content always being mechanical drills. Preserve recognizable/fun repertoire and engaging musical actions where they serve the learner need.

Do not force superficial variety. Reuse the chain records as the experience map: ability → learner action → content source → tool/mode → why that tool → scaffold removed. Review this at cluster boundaries and in the final representative learner journey. The purpose is to catch missing or underused teaching experiences, not to create another framework or a mode-count target.
