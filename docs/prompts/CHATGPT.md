# CHATGPT.md — outside reviewer durable notes

This file is for ChatGPT's cross-chat reviewer continuity. It is **not** a governing builder contract and does not replace `FABLE.md`, the owner's newest word, or the immutable review handoff. Read it when resuming outside-review work so recurring reviewer concerns do not disappear between chats.

## Learner experience variety and engagement

The curriculum must not become a technically correct sequence of mostly Score/Keep-tempo lessons.

For each learner-facing ability, review the tool/mode as a pedagogical choice, not as a convenient implementation surface. Ask:

- What is the learner actually doing?
- Why is this tool or mode the best fit for that action?
- What support does it provide now, and what support is removed later?
- What does the mode really measure or record, and what can it not establish?
- Is this experience meaningfully different from nearby work when a different experience would teach the skill better?

Do **not** require artificial variety or force every chain to use every mode. Variety is curriculum-wide, not a per-lesson quota. A Score-heavy chain is fine where Score is genuinely the right tool.

Across the shipped MUST abilities, watch for a healthy distribution of real teaching jobs across the app's experiences:

- **Simon:** hear/reproduce short ideas, ear-to-hand connection, memory with visible help progressively removed; never pretend visual copying proves unaided hearing or durable memory.
- **Accompaniment Lab:** supplied pattern -> reduced notation/chart -> choose or invent accompaniment; fade lit chord tones, supplied bass/voicing and other supports where appropriate.
- **Jam / backing contexts:** maintain groove, respond inside a harmonic frame, trade ideas, improvise under progressively looser constraints; do not turn an unstored frame into fake mastery evidence.
- **Free Play:** exploration, improvisation, riffs, chords, transposition and self-directed use where scoring would be counterproductive.
- **Duet / one-hand practice:** let the learner own one musical role while the app supplies another, then fade toward independent/both-hands performance where that is the target.
- **Listen / Hear / Wait / Keep tempo / Rhythm only:** use each for its actual scaffold and measurement semantics; do not collapse them into interchangeable Score modes.
- **Generated CONTROL / SIGHT-READING / NAMED-PATTERN / MUSICAL material:** use generation where exact isolation, variation, unseen material or a verified musical construction is the best teaching source.
- **Real excerpts and full repertoire:** use them for authentic transfer, integration and recognizable/fun musical experience, not merely because real music is presumed superior.
- **External material / listening / score study:** retain them when they are the best route for a learner need the app should not fake measuring.

### Curriculum-wide anti-drift check

After the first vertical slice proves the chain architecture, maintain a small experience map over the MUST abilities:

`ability -> learner action -> content source -> tool/mode -> why this tool -> support removed`

Use it diagnostically, not as another large framework. It should expose patterns such as:

- most abilities defaulting to Keep tempo/Score;
- Simon, Lab, Jam, Free Play or Duet existing in the product but having no real teaching consumers;
- improvisation or ear work existing only in prose;
- generated content being only mechanical drills and never verified musical/unseen material where appropriate;
- too little recognizable/fun repertoire or authentic transfer;
- repeated experiences that are valid individually but monotonous as a curriculum.

When such a pattern appears, fix the **next relevant chains**, not by retrofitting arbitrary mode quotas into every lesson.

### What counts as success

A varied curriculum is not one with the highest number of modes. It is one where different learner needs produce appropriately different experiences, and where the learner alternates among explanation, controlled practice, generated variation, listening/ear work, interactive modes, authentic excerpts, full pieces, improvisation/creation and independence as those jobs genuinely require.

The reviewer should therefore ask both:

1. **Is this individual chain pedagogically coherent and honest?**
2. **Across the curriculum, are we accidentally converging on one repetitive interaction pattern?**

If several consecutive abilities are essentially Score lessons despite other modes being a better teaching fit, treat that as a direction warning even when each individual chain passes its local checks.
