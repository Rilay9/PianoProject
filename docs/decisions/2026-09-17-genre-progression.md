# The genre progression, designed across every stage (2026-09-17)

**What this is.** A decision per cell of the track × stage grid — all fifteen tracks, all
ten stages — so that the early-stage work and the upper-stage work are one design rather
than two. The owner asked for exactly that: *"I don't want to have to ask you to come up
with new stuff for the new stages and then it's in a vacuum as opposed to taking into
context the stuff you have beginning."*

**What it supersedes.** `build/genre-plan.md` (2026-09-16) in three ways, each because the
owner corrected the brief it was written from:

1. Its rock section is built around seven named songs. Those were a *style* the owner
   named in conversation, not repertoire he wanted taught: *"I just meant that style of
   music… you shouldn't cater lessons to them."* `rock.8` is dropped and the rock ladder
   is textures, not songs.
2. It treats "personal build only" as a constraint. The owner has decided the public
   deploy should carry the personal items too, so a few friends can beta-test.
3. It answers "new rungs or options on existing rungs?" as a choice. It is both, plus a
   third thing the plan did not consider: **the modes built between 2026-09-15 and
   2026-09-16 are rung material**, and they are what makes genre content possible below
   Stage 4 at all.

Its archive research — the wants list, the index measurements, the per-genre inventory —
is sound and is reused rather than redone.

## 2. The principles this design follows

**P1 — A rung is not only songs and exercises.** Between 2026-09-15 and 2026-09-16 the app
gained duet (`?hands=`), rhythm-only, blind, the tempo ladder, Simon with three help
levels, free play and the accompaniment lab. Any of those can be the thing a rung asks
for. This is what makes genre possible early: a Stage 2 learner cannot read a stride bass,
but can play half of one in duet mode while the app takes the other hand.

**P2 — The earliest *honest* point, not the earliest stage.** The owner: *"of course
there's not going to be any content there, that's still like figuring out what notes
are."* Each genre element enters where the skill it needs has just been taught, named
below per cell, and not before.

**P3 — Options on a core rung before a rung of its own.** Where a core rung already
teaches the skill a genre uses, the genre arrives as an extra option beside the folk song.
It costs no structure, and it is how a learner meets variety without choosing a track.

**P4 — A new rung only where a genre carries a concept nothing else covers.** Swing feel,
the clave, the open fifth, the riff over a pedal bass. Everything else is P3.

**P5 — Nothing on a rung may claim something the music does not do.** `blues.3` said "one
of these is in the minor" when none of its three songs was. The check is mechanical and
must be run per rung: every claim a lesson makes about its own options is verified against
the catalog before the lesson ships.

## 5. Part C — The modes as rung material

Every one of these exists and is used by no lesson today.

| mode | what it teaches that nothing else can | rungs that use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Duet** (`?hands=`) | takes a two-hand texture apart — the app plays the hand you are not | `ragtime.4` (oom-pah alone), `rock.4`, `blues.4`, `jam.6` |
| **Rhythm-only** | feel without the reading load: swing, clave, syncopation | `jazz.3`, `latin.3`, `ragtime.4`, core `4.5` |
| **Blind** | whether the *form* is in your head, not on the page | `blues.5` (the turnaround), `jazz.4` |
| **Tempo ladder** | "not fast" — Joplin's own instruction — and the shuffle you cannot yet swing at speed | `ragtime.5`, `blues.4` |
| **Simon**, seeded from a genre's scale | ear before theory before name (`02` Part A item 7), which no genre rung honours today | `jazz.3` (blues scale), `latin.3`, `improv.4` |
| **Free play** | harmonic discovery — it names the chord you stumbled onto | `chords-pop.3`, `rock.5` |
| **Accompaniment lab presets** | harmony you play over rather than read, ungraded | `chords-pop.3`+, `jazz.4`, `blues.3`, `latin.3` |

### The lab needs presets, and that is a small change

The lab is six pickers from scratch. A preset sets them, locks the ones the preset is
*about*, and says in one line what it is for — reached as `#/lab?preset=<id>`, the idiom
the Score screen already uses for `?mode=`, `?loop=`, `?tour=` and `?hands=`.

First presets, all on progressions the lab already has: **pop four-chord** (I–V–vi–IV,
block chords), **jazz comping** (ii–V–I, shells), **blues shuffle** (12-bar, walking),
**ballad** (I–vi–IV–V, broken), **minor vamp** (i–♭VII–♭VI, held roots — needs the numeral
reader's flat prefix, which the lab already has).

A rung then offers *open the lab on this preset* beside its songs. That is what makes
harmony teachable at Stage 2: nothing is judged, so nothing has to be played accurately.

### One thing worth building: trading fours

The app plays two bars over the drum bed, the learner answers two. It is the teaching
device for blues and jazz and both halves exist — the `call-response` drill kind and
`backingLoop`'s bed. Rungs: `jazz.4`, `blues.5`, `jam.5`, `improv.4`.

## 6. Part D — What must be generated, and what must be re-levelled

**Re-level, not regenerate.** Every Phase 1 family is on no rung, so changing its level
breaks no band. Current levels are constants in their makers (`level = 4.4 if with_pulse
else 4.2`), not measurements. Against the core path's own ladder — quarters 1.1, mixed
values 1.2, 3/4 1.4, eighths 2.2, dotted 2.4, 6/8 4.5 — the proposals are:

| family | today | proposed | because |
|---|--:|--:|---|
| `clave` (no pulse) | 4.2 | ~2.6 | five strokes tapped on one note; harder than dotted, far short of 6/8 |
| `clave` (with pulse) | 4.4 | ~3.4 | two parts against each other is the new difficulty |
| `power-chord` | 4.1 | ~2.4 | two notes and an octave, left hand, no reading load |
| `oompah` | 4.2 | ~3.6 | a bass note and a chord — the family core `3.6` teaches |
| `ostinato` | 4.4 | ~2.8 | a repeated figure over a held bass is core `2.1` |
| `comping` (intro tier) | 4.4 | ~3.2 | triads in rhythm, which is core `2.3` plus a pattern |
| `walking-bass` (intro) | 4.5 | ~4.0 | genuinely Stage 4; a small move |

**NEEDS A MUSICAL EAR.** Every number above is an argument, not a measurement. They should
be checked before they are written.

**New families**, all small, all at levels nothing currently occupies: a five-finger riff
(~1.2), a drone/pedal (~1.4), a swung-eighth pair against a straight one (~2.2), the
pentatonic and blues scale as 5-finger shapes (~2.6), a modal-minor vamp i–♭VII–♭VI
(~3.0), a habanera bass (~3.4), a 6/8 ballad figure (~4.2).

**Rock from progressions, not from transcriptions.** Chord progressions are not
copyrightable. The textures of the bands the owner named — minor ostinato over a pedal,
power-chord bass, sus2/add9 open voicings, a 6/8 half-time feel — are generatable, are
stylistically accurate, and raise no licence question at all. This is a better answer than
hunting an archive that has zero rows for one of the three bands.

## 8. Part F — Sequencing, and what will break

**Order.** (1) Re-level, and generate the new families — no archive, no browser. (2) Part A,
the core-rung options — no new music. (3) The lab presets and the mode wiring. (4) The 17
rungs. (5) One quarry run. (6) **One** verification pass over everything: render every new
family, open every new rung, one list of faults, one batch of fixes.

The owner asked for that last point explicitly: not a render-and-review loop per edit on
material that is still moving.

**Tests that will break, from the earlier plan's own survey plus this design's additions:**

- `planNoUnobtainableRungs.test.ts` asserts `rock` is exactly `['rock.overview']`. The rule
  it protects — no option whose licence starts with `copyright` — survives; the assertion
  is rewritten. This is no longer a reopened decision: the owner's 2026-09-12 deletion was
  of *song-specific briefs with no music*, which this design does not create.
- `lessonShape.test.ts` — any lesson stating a count must offer exactly that many songs.
  The `holiday`, `hymns`, `latin` and `jazz.5` lessons all state counts and all four change.
- `curriculumIntegrity.test.ts` — renaming `latin` → `latin.5` (and `holiday`, `hymns`,
  `jam`) means the markdown filename, its front matter, and the curriculum's `textFile` and
  lesson id agree or none do.
- `placementTargets.test.ts` — its eighth item's `failUnit` is `blues-boogie.4.1` and its
  ordering check asserts the eight never go backwards. Leave the blues item alone.
- `simonDrill.test.ts`, `folderAssign.test.ts`, `planHierarchy.test.ts` all read the built
  curriculum. Run the content build before vitest.
- New: any lab preset needs a test that the preset's settings are ones the lab can build,
  or a rung will link to a screen that opens on an error.

**Risks.**

1. **Musical correctness is the top one and it is not hypothetical.** Within one week the
   generator shipped a "clave" that struck the downbeat of both bars, a "12/8 slow blues"
   that was a C major scale, and power chords that printed no symbols — all three passed
   every mechanical check. Everything generated here gets rendered and looked at.
2. **Re-levelling is safe today and will not be later.** It is free only while the Phase 1
   families are on no rung. Do it before step 4, not after.
3. **The quarry needs the owner's machine and an idle port 4173.** Steps 1–4 do not.
