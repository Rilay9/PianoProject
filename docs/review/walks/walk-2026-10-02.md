## Judgement

The loop works mechanically end to end. Today composes a session, the session carries the learner from activity to activity, a run is judged, the pass is recorded, and the next day's card reflects it. A Keep tempo run interrupted by the phone pauses, says how long the learner was away, and gives a count-in before it carries on.

The product shape is wrong where the parts meet:

- **The session asks for work the defaults cannot deliver.** The rung counts a run only in Keep tempo at 80 % of the written tempo. A piece opened from Today with the piano connected starts in Wait for me at 70 %. Neither setting can produce what the session item is for, so the learner finds the criterion only by failing it twice.
- **The same pass is told four ways.** The lesson page (collapsed) says it counted. Today's card says "not counted yet". Progress leads with minutes and says no skill moved. The next day's card says nothing is due for review.
- **Leaving goes through history, not intent.** No screen offers "stop here", and the back gesture reopens the previous activity.

On top of that, the owner's upright phone size draws the stacked read-ahead rows into each other on most loads (finding 2), and a teacher would refuse that page.

A piano teacher would say two things. First, the app never tells the student *before* the run what today's run of this piece is for and how it will be judged; it says so afterwards, partly, and only on one of the two failures. Second, the warm-up, the review exercise and the piece are all marked *done* or *played* on the card, while only one of them counted for anything.

## How it was driven, and what that cannot show

- **Input:** the MIDI mock from `tests/e2e/fixtures/midiMock.ts`, with notes struck from inside the page on the run's own animation frames (the method of `fixtures/playInTime.ts`, extended to Wait and to partial runs). Its timing is frame-perfect, as no person's is.

- **Backgrounding:** a forged `visibilitychange`, the specs' method. No real suspension, no audio context suspended, no screen lock.
- **The learner's history:** the placement was imported, not walked. The tour was skipped, not walked. The warm-up drill was ended, not played.
- **Repetition:** the walk ran once per size for the kept set. The 360 × 780 path ran three more times before that while the script was being corrected. Pictures from the first and third of those were read; the second's were discarded unread. They are the source of "three of the four" in finding 2. The probes (`scripts/walk-probe.spec.ts`) ran once each.
- **Not checked:** no app code, style or committed test was changed. The scripts under `scripts/` are copies of the walk's own files from `app/build/walk/`, kept so the walk can be repeated. Copy them back to `app/build/walk/` to run them: their paths are relative to that folder. They are not part of the test tree.
