## Anti-drift dispatch checklist

No new build brief should be dispatched unless it states, in order:

1. **Learner deficiency** — exact Fable/track finding being closed.
2. **Station** — CONTROL, MODEL/TRANSFER, MUSIC, or INDEPENDENCE.
3. **Vehicle/mode** — why this lesson/mode/activity can honestly serve the station.
4. **Content source** — generated exercise, real excerpt, lead sheet, full piece, or external/self-directed task, and why that source type is best here.
5. **Exact artifact** — score CID/bars or generator family/parameters.
6. **Published definition** for every named style/pattern claim.
7. **Verification boundary** — what an independent parser/checker proves versus what remains notation review/self-check/unverified-as-music.
8. **Learner-facing completion condition** — what becomes possible after this seam that was not possible before.

If items 1 and 2 cannot be answered, stop: the work is infrastructure/maintenance looking for a curriculum reason.
