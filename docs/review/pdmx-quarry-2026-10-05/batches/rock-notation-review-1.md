- later sections convert the material into repeated dyads/fifths, block attacks and broken/arpeggiated figures;

## Muse — New Born
CID: `QmXQNZvfM491cT1Xxn4AK3s2Vgvjn4CEtCtWNUCPQmKoi2`

Observed:
- 212-bar full ensemble score with electric piano, acoustic piano, guitars, bass, drums and clarinet;
- electric-piano opening is an exceptionally transparent repeating broken-chord/ostinato pattern in both hands: E-minor-family shapes alternating with neighboring harmony while retaining the same eighth-note cell;

Disposition: **ADMIT / HIGH-PRIORITY** for rock texture work.
Likely role: rock **MODEL → MUSIC** chain for arpeggiated ostinato, preserving a cell while harmony changes, and expansion from piano opening to dense band texture. Selected opening bars are more useful pedagogically than the entire 212-bar score.

## Relationship to Hysteria

The four strongest rock findings now have distinct jobs:
- **Come As You Are** — concise riff → fifth/chord texture and ensemble-to-keyboard reduction.
- **New Born** — repeated broken-chord piano ostinato preserved across harmonic change, then major density expansion.
- **Starlight** — simple driving bass groove under sectional build.
- **Hysteria** — technically denser defining sixteenth-note riff expanded into a full arrangement.

Do not collapse them into four generic 'rock songs'. The curriculum should keep only the ones whose distinct job survives the ability-map review.
