**What a learner meets.** Practising a loop in Keep tempo, each lap is now scored on its own, so a wrong key on lap two costs a note even after a clean lap one. **The one judgement to check:** a partial lap at Stop is not scored, so a learner who stumbles in the unfinished lap and stops is judged on the last whole lap. The builder chose this as the honest unit, a lap being the loop's pass; the alternative (the partial lap) would save a near-empty population after a boundary.
