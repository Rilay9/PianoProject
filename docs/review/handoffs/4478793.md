## What changed

- **One predicate, one filter.** `admittedForTeaching(item)` is exported from `eligibility.ts` as `unapprovedMusic(item) === undefined` — the same reading `eligibleFor` uses for its verdict — and `session.usable()` reads it. `usable()` is the single filter every card slot chooses through (the `runs`, `done` and `measure` offers, the ladder's `choose()`, the exposure rule) and, contrary to the brief's premise, the jam slot already filtered by it; no path is display-only. `session.ts` reads neither the fact nor the bit (a test holds it).
- **Pool and offer.** A want's `pool` is never chosen from; every row comes from `pick(want.offer)`. The builder left the pool unfiltered on purpose: a want whose offer is empty after admission produces no row, keeps the ask unmet and routes the slot down the fallback ladder. Filtering the pool would drop the want and let the card claim the next lesson's ask as if this one's were met — the `pool` mutant shows exactly that.
- **On the built catalogue** (a fresh learner at every one of the 109 rungs, every track on, four lengths): the committed code put an unapproved music-promising item on 146 rows of 59 cards — 44 warm-ups "asks for it", 50 reviews "more from …", 52 jams; D3a's 23 rows are inside that. After: none, every row filled by the ask's next admitted exercise, the rung step's next option or a reached jam rung's next option, none dropped; 116 cards change with 16 knock-on rows. The per-path mutants bring back exactly 44, 50 and 52.
- **On the phone** (`jam` and `holiday.5`, 30 and 60 minutes, 342 × 740, DOM equal to the unit probe on all four cards): the warm-up moves from the A-minor ostinato to the E major arpeggio under the same "Holiday asks for it" line, a level and a half higher, every other row unchanged.
- **Two asks the card cannot fill until a decision exists:** `latin.3` (every listed exercise a clave or the tresillo) and `latin.6` (tumbao, montuno, Latin groove). No row says they are met or waits for review (your answer); the new slot takes the song ask, the warm-up a prerequisite drill, the review a piece.
- **What the grooves used to hide:** the jam slot and the rung step take options in list order, so "Chords, form and feel" is now a transposition page at `chords-pop.7` and an ear drill at `theory.8` (G61).
