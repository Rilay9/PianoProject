## What changed

- **Q39, proven.** Replayed notes are stamped on the script's schedule but delivered by `setTimeout`; the harness ticks the engine from its own timer. A stall past a window's close lets the tick close the window as a miss before the stamped note arrives, which is then judged wrong. On the engine's injected clock a stall from 90 to 400 ms gives "expected 1 to be 2"; in the browser a 400 ms busy main thread failed 3/3, unstalled passed 3/3. The browser case now runs on Playwright's clock (installed before load, paused, run forward so timers fire in order) with its five assertions word for word; a new unit case runs the same replay on the engine's `FakeClock` and asserts the statistic exactly.
- **Q37, proven.** Levels 6 and 7 of `sightReadingPromises` take 0.64–0.84 s alone and 11.2–13.5 s beside two full unit runs, past the 5 s default in 8 of 10 case-runs. A local 60 s budget on those two cases, with the reason at the case; 16/16 under the same load.
- **Q34's density case, proven on a page slowed 32× through the DevTools protocol.** The case's own waits said 60 s but the 30 s test timeout cut them off (3/3 with the C6 chain's message). The 2.5 s sleep is replaced by the fit's `.score-view[data-settled]` mark and the file has a 120 s timeout; 3/3 at 32×.
- **Q44's reload case, proven at 32×** (the chain's exact `.screen h1` message once, the test timeout once, of three). Every `openMidiScreen` case waits for the mounted `[data-screen="midi"]`; the reload case after the reload too, with a 60 s budget for its two cold boots; 10/10 at 32×.
- **Not reproduced, made self-naming:** the MIDI "unsupported browser" case (3/3 even at 32×; goes through the revised helper) and the Duet door (first row in 0.2 s quiet, 1.0–1.6 s under load, 6.8 s at 32×; the chain's lost minute unexplained). `findRow` now waits on the list's count line or the status line's load-failure sentence, so a failure says which.
- **Every assertion kept:** the `expect(` count in each of the four specs is unchanged (43, 67, 71, 11), checked by the orchestrator against the parent commit. No tolerance on a measured value was widened; the three budgets bound how long a wait for published state may take, and each carries its reason at the case. Whether that reading of the brief's "no broad timeout" rule is right is question 1.
- **A product finding, U66:** the Q39 mechanism applies to live notes by the same path — a render stall longer than a note's remaining tolerance would mark an in-time note wrong. Reasoned from the code, shown only in tests, never seen on a device; recorded for X, not fixed.
