## What changed

- **The object.** An approved row in `content/sources/excerpts.json` becomes a catalogue item of `type: 'excerpt'` with its own file under `scores/excerpts/`, cut by a new build step from the parent's built file by printed position (clef, key, time and tempo carried in; a pickup kept where the row starts at bar 1; ties severed at both edges; a repeat sign at an edge neutralised; a range across a repeat or ending refused with the bars named; the unselected staff of a one-hand cut silenced and dropped; a normalised header — the excerpt's id as the title, no credits, and the archive's inner entry renamed so two cuts of one definition are byte-identical wherever written, a found fault). It is measured on the cut through E0's bridge with a window rule (`minInWindow` per demand in the density file, each a hypothesis with its reason), carries the parent's `source`, attribution and licence tags (a strict build refuses the cut where it refuses the parent), and its identity is a key over the parent's bytes, the range, the selection and the cut version, with the cut's own sha256 as D2's identity. The validator checks the rows; the score checks read `excerptOf` as they read `variantOf` (a found fault: build 2 flagged every cut as a truncation of its parent).
- **The proposer** scores windows from per-bar positions the bridge now writes (each located place by printed bar and staff; the every-bar detectors asked of each bar alone; each hand's range per bar; key-relative demands scored 0 after a key change — the second hypothesis failed for three detector kinds and each is handled), by seven signals with stated weights (opportunity, occurrences, phrase start, ending, pickup, texture, length), three gates (untaught by the rung's ancestry, forbidden by a generated parent's contract, physical by D0's limits) and nothing reading a level (the lowest-difficulty mutant is red). The first hypothesis was tested against the editions' own marks on ten parents: half the starts and six of ten ends agreed, and the signals were changed before any weight was set. Parents whose upper staff the detectors misread as treble (74) are skipped, after one approved cut had to be withdrawn.
- **The view** at `#/dev/microscope/excerpts`: candidates by claim with part-by-part scores, the parent drawn around the cut with the bars outside dimmed, playback from two bars before to two after, a boundary moved a bar at a time and re-scored, approve, adjust or reject, exported and merged idempotently (22 appended, then 0). A found fault: it opened on the cut's own page, hiding the bars before while saying they were on screen; page placement is now a pure function with unit cases.
- **The gate needs no branch:** the whole Anh. 113 is refused at `classical.3` (sixteenths, triplets) and its excerpt of bars 25–32 is eligible there and for the key signature, on the real catalogue. A run of an excerpt writes `material: {{ kind: 'file', sha256 }}` of the cut into the evidence context and marks the parent neither passed nor performed. Every reader of `item.type` was reviewed for its meaning (the compiler was no census: the readers compare with `===`), each with a case where its treatment differs: an unplaced excerpt is offered by no whole-catalogue tier, left out with songs, never "the same kind" of an exercise, not listed as skill practice, its own Library filter and title, the parent's source and licence on the detail sheet.
- **Five excerpts, on no rung**, one per claim where a window met every signal: Anh. 113 bars 25–32 (the key signature at classical.3, Q8), Hark!
