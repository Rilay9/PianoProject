## Required change

1. **BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF — correct the F-sharp-major leading-tone spelling before E dispatches.** The implementation currently engraves E-sharp as F natural in learner-facing harmony scores. The concrete mechanism is `UNWRITTEN = {"C-", "F-", "B#", "E#"}` at `tools/content/generate_exercises.py:3402`; `_readable` at `:3546-3559` simplifies every member of that set enharmonically; and `up` at `:3562-3564` applies that policy while building chord tones. The affected key-aware harmony makers include seventh voicings (`:3618`), four-chord loops (`:3673`), and ii-V-I (`:4224`). In F-sharp major, E-sharp is the diatonic seventh and is not an exotic spelling to hide. Printing F under an F-sharp key signature makes the pitch and scale-degree claim wrong even when playback is enharmonically correct.

   Fix the spelling policy so diatonic chord tones retain the named key's letter spelling while the narrowly justified simplification of genuinely unreadable borrowed or double-accidental spellings remains. Add a generated-score regression that reads the affected F-sharp-major families and proves the written pitch is E-sharp, plus a mutation/control that would catch a return to F natural. This is one domain-complete change: the shared spelling mechanism and all scores it emits, not ad hoc replacement in one family.
