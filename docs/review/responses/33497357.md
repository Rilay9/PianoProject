## 1. T7 — the current whole-piece voice-home rule is wrong on real scores; HD2 precedes CD1

**Ruling: yes, HD2 must land before CD1.**

The disagreement is in the model, not in `habaneraCell` / `tresilloCell`. `voiceHomeStaves()` counts a voice over the whole piece, then `extractScoreModel` assigns every note of that voice the home staff's hand. That makes a reused voice number a stronger fact than the note's explicit staff. The CD1 dumps show the consequence directly:

- *The Crave* bar 40: the long staff-1 inner line is voice 2 and is emitted as `L/x`, while the actual left-hand notes are staff 2 / voice 3.
- *Solace* bars 22, 26, 30 and 32: the same shape — staff-1 voice-2 notes are emitted `L/x` alongside the real lower-staff voices.

Those are not harmless detector differences. `hand` drives hand focus, hands-separate playback and every hand-sensitive detector. A whole-piece majority of one voice id is not sufficient authority to relabel an explicitly staff-1 passage.

At the same time, the existing `cross-staff.musicxml` fixture is a legitimate counterexample to the opposite naive rule. Its voice 5 is a lower-staff line that makes a local excursion onto staff 1 and returns; that staff-1 note must remain the left hand's. So **do not replace voice-home with `staff 1 = R / staff 2 = L` unconditionally**.

### HD2 semantic boundary

For a two-staff piano score:

1. **The note's printed staff is the default hand fact.** Staff 1 defaults to R; staff 2 defaults to L. This is a product mapping from the score's two piano staves, not a clef inference.
2. **Cross-staff is a local notation relation, not a whole-piece voice property.** Override the printed-staff default only for the specific note(s) that a bounded local gesture shows have crossed staves — e.g. the same voice makes a staff excursion inside the measure and returns, or a beamed/chord group itself spans the staves. A bare voice id being mostly on the other staff elsewhere in the piece is not enough.
3. A voice changing staff only across a measure boundary is not, by itself, cross-staff evidence. This is the live Crave/Solace failure mode.
4. **HD1 stays separate and unchanged.** In a one-staff score, an authoritative catalogue declaration still supplies the semantic hand exactly as Entry 244/246 settled it.
5. `ScoreNote.crossStaff` must mean a locally established cross-staff note under this rule. It must no longer mean merely "printed staff differs from this voice's whole-piece histogram home".

I am not requiring one particular OSMD property or heuristic implementation. I am requiring this evidence hierarchy. If a local cross-staff case cannot be established from the parsed notation, keep the printed-staff reading and record the limitation rather than guessing from the voice's history.
