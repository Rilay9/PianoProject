# Reviewer response — U119 (Entry 199)

## Verdict

**APPROVE WITH ONE REQUIRED CHANGE**

The H1 mechanism is the right base fix: the left group must not paint over or intercept the controls, and the real unforced-click grid proves the fault is removed across the measured sideways cells. Keep the group-level clip as the final safety boundary.

The residual behavior is not acceptable as shipped, because the clip can now turn one truthful line into a different apparent fact: `bar 12…` can read as `bar 1`, and `Paused…` can read as the imperative `Pause`. Finish the same U119 lane so constrained layouts degrade without changing meaning.

## Required change — protect semantic minimums before the safety clip

Use **option (c)** for Question 1: teach `fitBarControls` the sideways left group’s required minimum so the existing overflow order moves **Hands behind `⋯` before Back or the complete `bar n / m` location is clipped**.

The minimum is semantic, not the whole natural width of the left group:

- Back remains usable and identifiable;
- `bar n / m` remains complete — never a cut number that can be read as another bar;
- controls remain one row and remain tappable;
- title and ordinary status are the yielding content;
- the group-level clip remains as the last fence so no text can ever cover a control.

Do not add the proposed global/sideways threshold merely to rescue some cells. The measurements already show H2 alone does not solve the invariant and would shorten labels in cells that do not need it. The existing per-control overflow machinery is the domain-complete place to make a control yield when the required left-side minimum and fixed controls cannot coexist.

## Question 2 — flush status cut

**A flush mid-letter cut is not acceptable. A truncated ordinary status must visibly read as truncated.**

Once the required-minimum change above has allocated the left group, the ordinary status may yield as aggressively as needed, but if it is not shown whole it must end with an ellipsis (or an equivalently unambiguous truncation affordance), not a partial word. In particular, `Paused — ▶ to carry c` and `Pause` are failures.

Do not solve this by weakening the refusal rule: U105d’s refusal text still stays whole. Do not sacrifice Back or the complete bar location to buy the ellipsis. The builder may use the smallest measured CSS/layout mechanism inside the U119 bar ownership that gives the ordinary status a real truncation boundary; if that requires a new product trade beyond those invariants, stop.

## Verification for the fix-forward

Keep the existing measured grid and add the residual adversaries explicitly:

1. 568 × 320 at 115% on the wider face with `bar 1 / 4`;
2. the three-digit/201-bar case at 568 × 320 on both faces and at 640 × 360 on the wider face;
3. a constrained ordinary paused line proving any truncation ends unambiguously rather than mid-token;
4. real, unforced clicks on every visible control; one row throughout;
5. U105d’s refusal cases unchanged and whole.

This is a fast-path required change within U119. No new brief is needed unless satisfying the semantic minimum forces a different control-priority/product choice.

Once that lands, U119 may close.
