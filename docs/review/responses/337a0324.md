## Findings

### BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF — closed; X1 may consume the G2 policy

The required G2 correction is implemented at the correct fact boundary. After contact, relationship presence, legacy-reference handling, and the same-generator-seed rule, `transferReading` now reads `relationship.composition.playedAs` before any skill-dimension comparison. A non-empty `playedAs` returns `unknown`, with no credited or differing dimensions, while preserving separately known new demands.

This keeps the four facts independent:

1. exact-material first contact;
2. the measured relationship dimensions;
3. declared dimensions, which remain non-evidence;
4. the composition relationship, which currently lacks the arrangement/section distinction needed to judge independence.

The boundary cases are correct: `playedAs: []` proceeds to the dimensions; false contact remains `not-transfer`; unknown contact remains `unknown`; and the same material facts without the composition relationship can still demonstrate transfer on a measured skill dimension.

Both ladder consumers use the same reading. Promotion requires `demonstrated`; challenge protection receives no manufactured `differs` from a composition-gated reading. The new policy and ladder cases, three caught mutants, and the merged-tree targeted browser run support that mechanism. G2a therefore closes the required change from `responses/030ce744.md`.

### CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF — keep the built challenge-protection rule

The built behavior is the correct answer to the handoff's open question: a failed reading of another cut of a known composition counts against the skill unless it carries a separately known new demand.

Do not use the attempt's differences from the skill's establishing phrases to spare that failure. Those comparisons do not establish how the attempted cut differs from the already encountered cut, so retaining them for protection would silently treat an unresolved composition relationship as a known stretch. That would reintroduce the same unsupported inference that G2a removes from promotion, only through the downward path.

This remains a hypothesis in the ladder policy, but it is internally honest: unknown relationship facts do not become protection evidence.

### CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF — arrangement and section remain relationship-owned facts

Keep the current fail-closed result until the relationship owner carries per-played-item arrangement identity and section/bar relationship. Adding those facts must not directly choose a transfer verdict. Once the facts exist, the transfer policy must separately decide which relationships qualify as an independent context.

### LATER WAVE — related-material offers

Before notated repertoire becomes evidence-bearing, reconcile the transfer offer with this policy. The offer may currently describe another cut of an already played composition as “something new,” although G2a correctly prevents that run from demonstrating transfer. The eventual offer should either exclude such material or describe the related context honestly.

### PRUNE/MERGE — none

Do not split challenge protection into a second composition-specific policy or preserve dimensions only for that consumer. The single `TransferReading` is the intended shared truth.
