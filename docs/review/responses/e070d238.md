# Review response: U122b (`e070d238`)

**Verdict: APPROVE WITH ONE REQUIRED CHANGE**

The state-by-state correction is the right product shape. U122b does not reopen c6; it supplies the missing state rules that the static U122a decomposition could not express. The probe is discriminating rather than decorative: under the app's existing fold/count behaviour, the learner loses a direct pause during count-in/armed/playing, loses direct resume after a pause, the count overlays entrance notation, and the padded row reaches notation in the dense 780 x 360 case. Under the per-state treatment, the listed 24 landscape-phone cells keep the music fixed and clear while the moment's needed action remains directly reachable.

The three main rules may carry into the c6 build:

1. fold by the learner's current task/state, not merely because a run object exists: count-in/armed/playing keep the row reduced to a direct pause; paused/refused reopen the controls;
2. the count-in does not occupy or wash the notation stage;
3. the bottom row's painted/background area ends at its controls rather than covering notation with unused padding.

Also carry the fixed-height top band at rest, the refusal replacing the title while it stands, and the absence of stale folded run chrome over the finished sheet. U110 still owns the separate upright row-overlap defect and U35/CL07 still owns the freeze's two outcomes. Do not duplicate either inside the Score-chrome implementation.

## One required change before the c6 build brief is treated as ready

**Reframe the finished-state acceptance around the learner's actual outcome plus next action, after X46, rather than hard-coding U122b's pre-X46 sheet ordering.**

U122b's B arrangement moves all actions directly under `Run finished`. That makes every action reachable, but in the tight 568 x 320 / 115 % cells the first result figure is outside the visible part of the sheet. `Run finished` says only that the activity ended; it is not, by itself, the learner's outcome. This matters more now because X46 is changing the completion sheet precisely so it can say whether the run was judged/counted and what criterion or next attempt follows.

Therefore the c6 build brief must be based on the post-X46 current sheet and require, in the initial finished view:

- one truthful learner-facing outcome/verdict for this run (whatever X46's current model says: judged/not judged/counting result/criterion state), and
- the primary next action that follows that outcome.

Secondary figures and secondary actions may scroll below. Do not require every metric in the first view, and do not let this layout seam overwrite or reorder X46's outcome semantics merely to satisfy the old probe. Re-run only the narrow finished cells against the merged/current X46 sheet; this is an acceptance update, not a new design lane.

The folded `bar n / m` chip remains wrong over a finished sheet and should disappear there.

## Count-in form

Use the **large count numerals beside the direct pause control, without the wash**, as the build target.

That follows the existing purpose of the count: make the entrance beat obvious on a phone on a stand even when sound is low. The small top-line form proves a safe fallback location but gives up salience for no demonstrated need. The only observed failure of the large form is the existing spacing running off the left edge in 6 of the 12 568 x 320 cells at 100/115 % text; the numerals themselves and the row location are clear of the music.

Do not add another probe lane or preserve the current `6vw` gap as doctrine. In the c6 build, fit the spacing and prove the tight discriminator: **568 x 320, 115 %, wider face**. Acceptance is relational, not a new magic gap: all count numerals are whole inside the viewport, the current beat remains visually distinct, the direct pause keeps its tap floor, neither count nor pause covers notation, and the music does not move. If that cannot be satisfied without materially shrinking the count or the pause, the already-proved top-line count is the fallback. No owner hold is needed for this implementation choice.

## Paused notes richer than generic `Paused`

**Yes: the existing cause-bearing paused note may replace the piece name in the top line while it stands. Generic pause status does not.**

Keep this boundary semantic, not string-based:

- a normal learner-initiated pause already communicates itself through the stopped music, the open controls and direct ▶; do not move `STATE_TEXT.paused` into the top line;
- when the existing state carries a `pauseNote` because something changed the run under the learner — e.g. a demonstration handed it back at a bar or an option restarted it — that note answers a current question and may temporarily take the title's place;
- when the page being away caused the pause, the away explanation may do the same. The proposed short form, `Paused — you were away N s. ▶ to carry on`, is sufficient; the secondary Start-again instruction remains one tap away in ⋯.

Keep `bar n / m` beside the note where it fits. Do not invent a generic status-to-top-line pipeline, a new pause taxonomy, or special cases based on parsing the message text. Use the existing state/cause signals that already produce these notes.
