## Accepted decisions

- **ACCEPT — musical truth remains owned by the gate.** The browser must not score music. Where the build has persisted a verdict, the projection carries it; otherwise the projection may rerun the same gate on the exact built score and declare that it recomputed the result. The screen should show evaluated/pass-or-refuse, total, floor, wrong cadences, evaluator version, and the gate's own wording.
- **ACCEPT — “unheard” remains independent.** A notation evaluator can judge phrase shape without establishing that a person heard the item or that it succeeds as idiom or music. Keeping the explicit unheard sentence prevents the numeric verdict from acquiring a stronger meaning.
- **ACCEPT — unevaluated families keep the contract's exact limitation.** Grooves and style families should say that the evaluator judges phrase shape rather than idiom; “no musical evaluator exists” is false after D3. Drills remain drills rather than unevaluated music.
- **ACCEPT — recipe selection belongs in the projection.** `family_contracts.selected(..., recipe)` is the authoritative reading of conditional requirements. Project selected requirements per item and have the screen render them without reimplementing `when`. Apply the same rule to any projected conditional forbids, secondary targets, or overconcentration warnings the screen exposes.
- **ACCEPT — provenance values are literal record facts.** The current type is a string-valued fact, so print `kind`, the exact `value`, and `via`; an absent/null value reads “no decision.” Do not translate `yes | no | fix` into teaching conclusions or merge usable-score and good-teaching-use decisions.
- **ACCEPT — verification layers.** Projection tests establish the build-side decisions, rendered-screen tests establish the learner-independent builder instrument, and product captures of one study, one groove, and one drill expose wording/layout errors. Nothing heard remains explicitly unverified.
