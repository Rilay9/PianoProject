## Findings

1. **BLOCKING — BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF (D2).** The record must keep each judgement's evidence and supersession semantics separate. D2 item 2 gives an item two independent decisions, `usableScore` and `goodTeachingUse`, but gives the line one shared `basis`; its verification then says “the strongest basis wins.” Those are not one fact. A reviewer can establish score usability from rendered notation while leaving teaching use unheard, and can later hear the item and revise only teaching use. In an append-only file, “strongest basis wins” also does not say whether a later correction can supersede an earlier current-identity judgement.

   **Required mechanism:** make basis, reason, category, reviewer, and timestamp belong to each decision dimension, or make each JSONL event address exactly one dimension. Define current-value resolution explicitly: triage never participates; stale identity never participates; a later valid human event for the same identity and dimension supersedes the earlier one (with an explicit `supersedes` id if the builder finds that safer). Do not let `heard` automatically override `notation` for the other dimension. Add tests for: score reviewed from notation while teaching use remains null; a heard teaching-use decision without changing score review; a later correction superseding an earlier decision; stale and triage lines filling neither bit. This is the smallest contract that preserves E0/R42's two separate review truths.

2. **ACCEPT — CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF (D2).** Keep the microscope in the app and builder-only in navigation. The existing `#/dev/<id>` routing and `DevScoreScreen` premise support using the actual renderer and playback path. The implementation must still ensure the microscope is absent from learner navigation and cannot mutate learner evidence, progress, or session state.

3. **ACCEPT — CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF (D2).** The export-and-merge path is preferable to adding a write-capable dev server. Export loss is visible and recoverable; a server endpoint would expand authority and deployment surface. The screen should retain unexported decisions locally, mark export/merge status plainly, and export stable event ids so rerunning `--merge` is idempotent rather than duplicating lines.

4. **ACCEPT — CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF (D2).** A `heard` basis must mean playback of the complete material under the experience being judged, with both hands sounding and the item's intended tempo/context. Hand-isolated playback is inspection support, not by itself “heard” for teaching-use or musical-quality review. If playback is partial, the record should remain `notation` and free text may say what was auditioned.

5. **ACCEPT — CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF (D1).** Keep S26 hard and the phrase qualities soft. The existing attempt sequence and promise budget are the right boundary: generate valid candidates first, score their musical shape second. The scorer must not rescue a candidate that violates taught material, requested promises, range, metre, or tie constraints.

6. **ACCEPT — CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF (D1).** The proposed levels 1–4 arrival rule is acceptable as a first explicit hypothesis: the final event begins on the last bar's felt beat 1 or sustains at least one beat as the arrival. State it unambiguously in the tests so “there” cannot be read as any long final note anywhere. It is a scored quality, not a universal cadence law; the distribution report and teacher read may revise its weight without weakening hard validity.

7. **ACCEPT — CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF (D1).** Version the generated phrase identity before changed phrases can reach encounter history. If the existing runtime identity/history schema cannot carry the generator version without migration, stop at that boundary as the brief says; do not silently reuse an old seed identity for new notes.

8. **ACCEPT — LATER WAVE.** D1's distribution suite is the correct verification form. Bounds must fail the committed generator for contour and phrase ending, be justified per metric, and avoid a single golden form. Hearing the results remains D2/D3 work; rendered teacher reads do not become evidence that the audio or musical feel is good.

9. **ACCEPT — PRUNE/MERGE.** Neither brief should add skills or demands, rewrite rungs, change learner-facing words, or absorb E1/D3/D4. D2 records judgement; D1 improves one phrase generator; later seams consume those truths.
