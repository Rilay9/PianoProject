# Reviewer response — G1 encounter model `b48342f`

Implementation HEAD: `b48342f`

## Verdict

**APPROVE WITH ONE REQUIRED CHANGE: separate general first-contact truth from sight-reading `unseen` before G2/X consume it.**

The encounter model itself is accepted:
- durable `encounters` rows record factual contact by visit and action;
- the `contacts` summary preserves pruned run contact in the same pruning transaction;
- summaries are evidence-free projections, not a second competence state;
- passage scope, excerpt-parent mapping, composition contact and legacy id-only contact remain distinct;
- backup/restore carries both durable stores;
- imports use stored-content identity where the bytes are actually available;
- D4 contact keeps its conservative `met | met-by-id | unmet` semantics and gains honest `how` facts;
- first-contact is derived from history and rechecked before the run is stored.

The retention adversary is the important proof: deleting old runs no longer deletes the factual truth that the learner encountered that material.

## Required change — G1a: stop overloading `unseen`

At this HEAD, `RunHeader.unseen` now has two meanings:

1. for a generated sight-reading phrase, it is an **evidence condition**: whether that phrase was genuinely first sight;
2. for ordinary notation/import runs, it is a **general encounter fact**: whether this material/passage was first contact.

The implementation then has to introduce `isPhraseRun` guards in `recordRun`, rung state, history and the evidence job so the old sight-reading consequences do not accidentally apply to ordinary pieces.

That works mechanically, but it is the wrong long-term truth boundary. It makes one boolean mean two domain concepts and requires every future reader to know which interpretation is legal.

Before G2 or X begins consuming general first-contact from runs:

- keep `unseen` as the sight-reading phrase/evidence field;
- add a separately named run fact for ordinary/general encounter truth, e.g. `firstContact`;
- generated sight-reading runs may carry both when useful:
  - `firstContact`: factual encounter relation;
  - `unseen`: the sight-reading evidence condition derived from that relation and the visit rule;
- ordinary pieces/imports/excerpts carry `firstContact`, not `unseen`;
- legacy phrase rows keep their existing `unseen` semantics;
- do not create a new competence/evidence state from `firstContact`.

Acceptance:
- repeated ordinary pieces still pass/meet rungs exactly as now;
- phrase evidence still refuses a heard/previously seen phrase;
- history wording for phrases remains correct;
- a general-contact consumer can read `firstContact` without first calling `isPhraseRun`;
- rows written before G1 remain readable without manufactured contact.

This is a narrow schema/reader cleanup, not a redesign of the encounter store.

## Question 2 — who wires durable contact into the transfer offer?

**G2 should consume the G1 encounter model; X1 should not independently reinvent that wiring.**

The transfer offer's novelty/contact question belongs to the encounter/contact consumer layer. G2 may wire the session input to use encounters + summaries through the one contact adapter, including heard-only and pruned contact.

X1 still owns its separate session-selection obligations such as L113 and snapshot/composition behavior. If G2 must touch a shared X1 file, keep the ownership seam explicit: G2 supplies/defines the contact input and test contract; X1 must consume that same truth rather than a second contact calculation.

Do not create two session-side novelty readers.
