### 1. Correct the pitch-range teaching in wave 1(b)

The brief's own fact says the exact G target uses **D4 through D5**: the G five-finger position plus the D below. The proposed learner text then says:

> “The G five-finger position holds all of it”

That is false. A conventional G five-finger position, G–A–B–C–D, does not contain the lower D. It also makes “same fingers” too absolute across the whole theme.

Keep the exact 63/63 event target and the hidden-page check, but rewrite the instruction to teach the actual move/range: most of the tune sits in G position and the learner must reach or shift to the lower D where the original C version drops to its lower G. The acceptance and test should preserve the exact target without claiming a fixed five-finger position covers it.
