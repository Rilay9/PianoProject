### Required change — BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF / dispatch

Close the `equivalent` side door and name the verdict by the fact it actually represents.

A music-promising generated item with `provenance.review.teaching !== true` must be refused for `equivalent` as well as `skill`, `requirement`, and `demand`. An authored `alternatives[]` entry is still an automatic learner-facing teaching offer: `selectors.ts:200-207` sends it through the same gate, and E0's contract explicitly says that same-lesson options and explicit alternatives receive “provenance, never immunity.” Authorship establishes the relationship, not that unheard generated music is fit to teach. Keep only `exploration` (and direct Library browsing, which does not call this offer gate) exempt.

Revise the consumer regression accordingly: an authored alternative naming the unapproved study must not appear; after `teaching: true`, it may appear subject to the existing gates. Include a positive exploration/Library case so the exemption stays deliberate.

Do not call the reason `unreviewed`. D2 maps `no` and `fix` to `teaching: false`, so those items have been reviewed. Use a truthful reason such as `teaching-use-not-approved` (wording may differ), covering `null` and `false` without collapsing their stored provenance. Learner-facing text should likewise avoid “not yet reviewed” for a reviewed rejection or fix request.
