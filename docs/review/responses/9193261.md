## Required change

- **BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF — persist the offer’s relationship before a transfer-intended run can start.** `ScoreScreen.ts` starts an unawaited `Promise.all([rungRows(), catalogIndex()])` to compute `transferRelationship`, then continues loading and enables play. `runFacts` writes `intent: 'transfer'` even when that promise has not resolved, and omits `relationship` when it is still undefined. Entry 106 correctly lists this race as unverified. It breaks D4’s core record contract: a fast run can say it was transfer-intended without recording the relationship on which the offer was made.

  Make the transfer relationship ready before the transfer route becomes playable, or carry the already computed relationship from the session choice through a durable session snapshot and write that exact value. Failure to load the relationship must be explicit; it must not silently produce a partial transfer record. Add a distinguishing regression that holds the relationship load pending, tries to begin/finish the run, and proves no transfer-intended row can be stored without its relationship. Also prove the stored relationship is the offer’s relationship rather than a later recomputation after history changes.

D4 closes when that fix-forward is reviewed. G1 may continue only where it does not assume D4 is closed; G2 remains behind the fix and G1.
