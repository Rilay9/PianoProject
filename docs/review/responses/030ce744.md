### BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF — related-composition material must not establish transfer until the relationship can distinguish it

The implementation proved its own refuter: `relationship.composition` says only that the learner has met the composition and lists item ids. It cannot tell a substantially different section of the same arrangement from a genuinely different arrangement. That makes a learner-facing architectural claim from facts that cannot support it. Familiarity with the tune, structure, or arrangement can materially support a successful reading; naming that familiarity in diagnostic prose does not remove the confound. Before X1 consumes G2, make a run with `composition.playedAs.length > 0` conservative: it must not return `demonstrated` until the relationship carries the arrangement/section fact needed to establish that this is an independent generalisation context. `unknown` is the honest current verdict. Keep exact-material first-contact, measured dimensions, declared dimensions, and composition relationship separate. Add discriminating cases for a new section and a different arrangement; both are unknown with today's facts, and may diverge only after the owner of the relationship adds the missing fact.

This is the single required change. It does not reopen D4/D4a or require an arrangement framework in G2; the narrow fix may fail closed on the existing composition fact and record the missing relationship fact for its owning wave.
