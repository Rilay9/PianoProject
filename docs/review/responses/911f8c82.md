## 1. Question 1 — accept c6 as the base whole-Score model

**Accept c6 over c1.**

The trade is justified by the measured product outcome:

- c6 keeps the piece name and `bar n / m` available in all 86 sideways cells measured at rest and paused, instead of c1 losing the name whenever paused;
- c6 removes the 22 px fold/reveal movement without reducing run-time stave size;
- the next music remains in view in every measured sideways cell;
- the at-rest cost appears only in 16/86 height-decided cells, is under 10% for c6, and the ordinary at-rest cells remain above the reading floor;
- unlike c4/c5, c6 keeps the selected mode, Hands and tempo visible rather than turning readable run context into hidden setup state;
- unlike c3, c6 does not rely on a supposedly-clear score corner that the probe showed is not clear.

`summary.txt` supports the core comparison: c6 is smaller at rest in 16/86 cells, unchanged in a run, loses no next music, and does not put an ordinary rest-state corner over the notation. `stability.txt` shows c6's music top does not jump at fold/reveal. The CL07 discriminating cells also do not reverse the decision: the upright and tablet cases are unchanged by the landscape-phone decomposition.

This accepts the **top-context / bottom-controls decomposition**, not every detail of c6's current transient-text placement.

## 2. Question 2 — neither “cut in the row” nor “whole on a bottom strip over music” is the right first choice

The evidence itself refutes both offered answers as the primary design:

- In c6 the ordinary status can become extremely short in the row's spare width at narrow cells. `texts.txt` shows examples where the paused line is reduced to only a small prefix. That repeats the original failure in miniature: controls win the row and the meaning is squeezed into whatever is left.
- A permanent/transient strip above the bottom row keeps the sentence whole, but the handoff records it over the paused music's foot in 36/86 cells. Covering the notation is not an acceptable price for preserving prose, especially after c3 was rejected for the same class of mistake.

### Required c6 transient-state probe

Reuse the **top context band** that c6 already pays for:

1. Ordinary state: piece name left, `bar n / m` right, as c6 already measures.
2. Actionable transient state (for example paused): the **title yields first** and the left/top context becomes a short state/action message; retain `bar n / m` if the discriminating cells show both can coexist without cutting the actionable core.
3. Refusal: try the refusal in that same top message surface before allocating a second line anywhere else.
4. The message contract is semantic, not “preserve the current 72-character sentence at all costs”: the whole **actionable core** must fit — current state + what the learner can do next, naming a control that is actually visible/reachable.

Measure this only on the cells that can falsify it: U120's 568×320 refusal, U121's narrow paused case, the owner's 780×360 case, and the narrow/wide face needed to distinguish the text fit. If it works, it is strictly cleaner than either offered bottom treatment: no new music-height cost beyond c6's line and no notation overlay.

If it fails, report why. Do not respond by layering another allocator onto the bottom bar.

## 3. Question 3 — never choose overlaying the score's foot as the fallback

If a refusal cannot fit honestly in the c6 top transient surface, **refit/reserve the stage around the refusal while it stands rather than overlay notation**.

The U122a data already shows the c6 refit trade is bounded: the closest measured refusal case is about 22.3 px, still above the current 22 px floor, whereas c4 is the candidate that falls materially under it. That makes temporary c6 refitting a defensible fallback.

Apply the same product invariant while paused/running: a transient message must not cover notes/fingerings the learner may need to read. If preserving the frozen run makes a bottom refusal strip overlap the score, solve the placement/reserve of the transient surface rather than declaring the overlap acceptable.
