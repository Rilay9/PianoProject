## Finding

### The common admission predicate does not cover excerpts

**Classification: BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF**

E1 keeps an unplaced excerpt out of whole-catalogue search tiers, which protects the five current rows. That is process-dependent, not a closed learner-facing contract.

On the combined current tree, `admittedForTeaching()` delegates to `unapprovedMusic()`, and that function checks only `provenance.facts.promise.value === 'music'`. An excerpt is a notated item and therefore returns admitted regardless of `provenance.review.teaching`. If a future rung lists an excerpt before a current affirmative teaching-use decision, ordinary rung, fallback, jam, swap, and lesson-page paths can offer it automatically. Measured demands, an approved boundary, and authored placement do not establish teaching use.

Required E1a change:

1. Extend the one shared teaching-use admission definition so an excerpt is admitted to any automatic teaching offer only when its current cut identity has `provenance.review.teaching === true`.
2. Keep explicit Library/exploration access available and truthfully labelled.
3. Do not create an excerpt-only chooser or a D4-local bypass. D4, session-card selection, swaps, and D3c's lesson-page selectors must consume the same predicate.
4. Test `null`, `false`, and `true` for an excerpt with otherwise eligible measured demands, both unplaced and rung-listed.
5. Prove a stale review on an older cut identity does not admit the current cut.
6. Add mutants that remove excerpt admission from the common gate and from a direct automatic consumer.

This required change is about admission only. It does not require a musician to approve any of the five excerpts.

## Answers and constraints

### Q59: unreviewed excerpts and swap tiers

**Classification: CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF**

Treat an excerpt like other music whose teaching suitability is not established: measured notation may support discovery and explicit exploration, but every automatic teaching offer requires a current affirmative teaching-use decision. “Not placed” is not the admission rule; it is only why today's five happen not to surface.

### Q58: bar numbers

**Classification: CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF**

Keep the cut's own bar numbering on the excerpt page. The excerpt is a first-class playable object, and its renderer, playback, evidence, and navigation should remain local to that object. Continue naming the parent's printed range in the title/source line, as the implementation already does. Do not make the cut's internal bar indices impersonate the parent's score.

### D4 dispatch

**Classification: BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF**

Do not wait for a musician to approve a candidate. Do wait for the E1a common-admission fix to be accepted. D4 can then implement and test transfer-aware selection even if the production catalogue has zero admitted excerpts; its positive case may use a fixture with a current `teaching: true` decision. This keeps D4 from owning a second admission rule.

### Density and detector limitations

**Classification: LATER WAVE**

The `minInWindow` values remain hypotheses, and the recorded left-hand-pattern/walking-bass false positives show why they are not teaching-quality proof. Keep the candidate-rung dagger and refused-candidate evidence. These limitations do not block the object architecture because automatic use remains gated.

### Chooser scope

**Classification: PRUNE/MERGE**

Do not add a general excerpt chooser in E1a. Cross-source ranking and experience selection remain with the later chooser work.
