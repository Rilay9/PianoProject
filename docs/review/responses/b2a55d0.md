# Review response — U67 brief, Hear it after a reload

Brief HEAD reviewed: `b2a55d0`

## Verdict: APPROVE WITH ONE REQUIRED CHANGE

U67 may dispatch after the brief makes the live audio dependency one complete binding: the context **and** its destination must be refreshed together at the user gesture. The current brief repeatedly names only the context, while `ScoreScreen` captures both `audioEngine.contextOrNull` and `audioEngine.masterGain` before the first gesture and `ScoreSession` retains both construction options. A provider/setter that refreshes only `audioContext` would fix scheduled piano notes while leaving the metronome routed through the stale null destination. Name the real owned file, `app/src/score/ScoreSession.ts`, at the same time; `app/src/engine/ScoreSession.ts` does not exist.

With that correction, the proposed mechanism is the right seam. The learner's tap must await `audioEngine.ensureStarted()`, then bind the resulting context and current master destination before any `start()` or relevant `resume()`. Whether that binding is exposed as a provider or a setter remains the builder's call. A setter carrying `{ context, destination }` is the smaller fit for the existing synchronous session API; a provider is equally valid only if the gesture handler still performs the asynchronous `ensureStarted()` first and the session reads the pair atomically at every playback/metronome boundary. Do not make `ScoreSession` create or own the app's AudioContext.

## Findings and classifications

- **BLOCKS NEXT BRIEF — complete live audio binding:** revise U67 item 1 and its owned-file list so the implementation refreshes `audioContext` and `destination` together, at the tap, in `app/src/score/ScoreSession.ts`. This blocks U67 dispatch, not D1a, E0b, E1 or D3.
- **CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF — resume paths:** the two existing `session.resume()` sites do not require a second AudioContext owner. A demonstration can resume only after Hear it has established the live binding. The ordinary Play path should continue through the same app-level gesture start contract; if the red-first test proves otherwise, route that gesture through the same binding rather than adding a timer or a second engine.
- **CONSTRAINS NEXT BRIEF — regression observation:** the count under the existing `__pianopath` test hooks is acceptable and preferable to a browser-internal scheduled-source spy, provided it counts calls at the real `Piano.start` scheduling boundary, is reset/read-only for the test, and is absent or inert outside test hooks. Assert greater than zero after the first Hear-it tap from a direct URL; retain the prior-tap case as the control. The unit test must separately prove a session constructed cold and bound live later, including the destination pair.
- **PRUNE/MERGE — no X expansion:** this is an audio-lifecycle defect, not a Score-screen redesign. Do not absorb latency, practice-engine audio, microscope changes, or other screens into U67.
