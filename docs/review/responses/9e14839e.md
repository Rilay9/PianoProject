### Question 1 — reshape the proposed “pass loop” lane

**Do one design/read lane, but do not define it as “what a pass is for every item kind.”** Many session items are warm-ups, exploration, review, unmeasured/self-reported work or other activities for which “pass” is the wrong universal concept. That framing would risk building another ontology around the symptom.

The lane's product question is:

> **For one composed session item, what does the learner need to know before, during and after it so the item has one coherent purpose/outcome story?**

It should derive the smallest contract that covers the current contradictions:

1. **Purpose / why now.** What is this item in today's session for?
2. **What can count.** Is it measured, self-reported, preparatory, exploratory, performance, or evidence toward a specific requirement/skill? If nothing can count, say that rather than borrowing pass language.
3. **Reachable opening state.** If the item is meant to satisfy a criterion, its default mode/tempo/configuration must be able to satisfy it **or the UI must truthfully frame the opening activity as preparation and provide a direct route to the criterion**. The current Wait-at-70% -> Keep-tempo-at-80% example fails this.
4. **Outcome.** Distinguish “activity completed/played” from “evidence counted,” “requirement advanced,” and “mastery changed.” Do not make `done`, `played`, `passed`, `started` four accidental answers to one learner question.
5. **Next action.** The completion action should do what the explanation recommends. If the sheet says Keep tempo is next, the primary/direct continuation must be capable of taking the learner there rather than silently moving to another item.
6. **Consumer consistency.** Lesson, Today, completion, Progress and the next day's composer may present different views, but they must be views of the same stored/session truth. A frozen session card may remain a snapshot without asserting a current falsehood such as “not counted yet” after the run counted.

Use findings **1, 3, 6 and 7** as the discriminating cases. Start by tracing the current truths and consumers; if the existing distinctions already compose into this contract, the deliverable may be a small consumer reconciliation rather than a new persistent model.

Finding **4** (no honest stop / Android back reopening the previous activity) belongs beside this lane as a **session-lifecycle/navigation acceptance case**, not inside the definition of pass/evidence. The learner must have an intentional stop/return path, but do not make navigation semantics part of the evidence model just because the walk found them together.

Finding **10** stays an **onboarding/composition observation** for now. “Untouched checklist reads as finished” and “Stage-0 learner receives L1.5 sight-reading” may have different mechanisms. Trace them against the current first-session/composer rules before deciding whether either belongs to this session-item contract. Do not absorb them merely to make the lane comprehensive.
