# D4 scoping review — transfer-aware selection and runtime unseen studies

Scoping handoff: `docs/review/handoffs/c7995b0.md`, carrying `docs/prompts/tasks/D4-scoping.md`. Reviewed at branch HEAD `7c61fd46575290bcd4e8c7c03d23f120f5c406e7`.

## Verdict: APPROVE WITH ONE REQUIRED CHANGE

D4 should own deliberate transfer material selection and the durable facts describing what was offered and played. The post-E evidence task should continue to own the rule that turns those facts into “transfer demonstrated.” Runtime studies remain deferred under D3a’s per-item teaching-use rule. D4 should wait for E1’s excerpt identity and first approved excerpts before wiring learner-facing transfer offers, so the first implementation covers the intended generated-to-authentic relationship instead of establishing a generated-only special case.

### Required change — BLOCKS THE D4 BRIEF

The premise that contact novelty can be read “from what the store already holds, by identity” is not true for general content.

- `progressStore.sessionsForItem(itemId)` queries the `byItem` index and compares only `SessionRow.itemId`.
- `EvidenceContext` currently carries `itemId`, optional seed and `firstContact`; it has no versioned material identity, role or source relationship.
- `SessionRow` carries a generator identity only for sight-reading phrases: family, version and seed. A built generated study, excerpt or ordinary file run has no general recipe/file identity on the stored row.

The D4 brief must therefore define and persist a durable material identity before it claims identity-based novelty. Reuse the identities already owned by D1/D2/E1/D3 rather than inventing another identity: generator family/version/seed plus recipe where applicable; parent file fingerprint, printed range, selection and cut version for an excerpt; file fingerprint for file material. Define how legacy rows with only `itemId` are read. An `itemId` match may conservatively prove prior contact, but its absence cannot prove first contact after an item’s identity has changed.

Do not solve this by adding a second transfer decision to the store. Store the facts; the later evidence policy reads them.

## Answers to the three scoping questions

1. **APPROVE, with a boundary correction — CONSTRAINS THE D4 BRIEF.** D4 selects candidates and records stable material, role and relationship facts. It does not replace `ladder.ts`’s v0 transfer condition. The brief must not present the current `transfer demonstrated` state as a reliable completion gate for this new selector: v0 can become true from first contact with another reading row and cannot inspect role, family or relationship. Until the post-E policy consumes the new facts, D4 may describe an offer as transfer-intended and record its result, but it may not claim that the run demonstrated transfer.

2. **Per-item admission; defer runtime studies — CONSTRAINS THE D4 BRIEF.** A family marked `heard: true`, one heard item, the four gates and an evaluator floor do not establish teaching use for every future seed. That would turn one item’s review into a family-wide musical-quality claim, contrary to D3a’s current-identity rule. The sight-reading rows are a different contract rather than an exception or a fault: they are runtime reading drills with no `music` promise fact, and D1 gates each phrase for that bounded reading activity. If a later seam wants runtime music-promising studies, it needs a separately reviewed family-level admission contract strong enough to justify that broader promise.

3. **Wait for E1 — BLOCKS D4 IMPLEMENTATION, not drafting.** Write the D4 brief after these answers, but dispatch implementation after E1 supplies the excerpt object, its durable identity and the first candidates that pass E1’s non-owner gate. This lets one selector compare a controlled generated source with an authentic excerpt and avoids a temporary generated-only rule that would need redesign when excerpts arrive.

## Additional constraints

- **CONSTRAINS THE D4 BRIEF:** `role: transfer` and `transferOf` are selection intent and relationship facts. They never count as transfer evidence.
- **CONSTRAINS THE D4 BRIEF:** “Unseen” must be computed for the exact versioned material identity. A new seed may be new material; a regenerated item under a new version may also be new material. A renamed id with the same identity is not new.
- **LATER WAVE:** The post-E evidence task must replace v0’s `firstContact + different itemId` rule with a skill-relative policy that reads contact novelty, context relationship and measured evidence together.
- **PRUNE/MERGE:** Do not build a second encounter-history model in D4. Persist the minimum identity and contact facts needed now, then let Part 27’s encounter model consume or migrate them.

No owner decision or musical judgment is required for these scoping choices.
