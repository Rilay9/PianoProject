## Ask owner (collected, now answered above)

- **Keys while a panel is open (B1-04, S1):** with a settings panel open over something running (a score run, a Memorise
  pass, a Simon turn, a drill card), the keys you play still count. Keep counting, ignore keys until the panel closes,
  or pause what is running when a panel opens?
- **Hands during a run (B2-01, S1, optional):** a Hands tap during a run now ends that run with its summary, as changing
  Mode does. Right, or lock the Hands buttons until you stop?

- **Warmness's left hand (S4):** your recording holds bass keys down under the pedal while the rest of the broken chord
  plays, so the app writes a second left-hand voice almost every bar; the published page writes one voice a hand and
  lets the pedal hold. (a) Write pedalled played-in files like the published page: each note runs to its hand's next
  note while the pedal is down (measured: Warmness extra voices 1.14 → 0.35 a bar; a melody note held over moving notes
  then looks shorter, though it sounds the same); or (b) keep showing which keys were actually held.
- **Seize the Day bars 157–159 (S4, M5):** the fast notes are real triplets and stay; the held notes around them look odd
  because the file plays every note about 95% of its length. (a) Build a fix that undoes that shortening next (a bigger
  change, with its own risks); or (b) leave it.

- **Splits where lines overlap (B9-01):** in Adjust lines, a tap where two lines overlap adds the split to the upper
  line. (a) keep that (tap the lower line below the shared strip; fails only when one line sits wholly inside another);
  (b) the line whose middle is nearest the tap; (c) inside the shared strip, the lower line (overlaps come from pulling
  a line's bottom down over the next).

- **Drills dealing (B8-01):** a set of N cards is drawn at random from the set, never the same card twice running, so
  a small set repeats items within one set. Keep that, or deal like a shuffled deck (every item once before any
  repeats)?
